# Under Armour Data Breach

## What Happened

In November 2025, [the Everest ransomware group claimed Under Armour as a victim and attempted to extort a ransom](https://hackread.com/everest-ransomware-under-armour-users-data/), alleging they had obtained access to 343GB of data. [In January 2026, customer data from the incident was published publicly on a popular hacking forum](https://x.com/justabreach/status/2012933753853657288), including 72M email addresses. Many records also contained additional personal information such as names, dates of birth, genders, geographic locations and purchase information.
