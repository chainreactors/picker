# Vibe Coding Isn't Using AI to Make Things

The line is whether you understand what you built

November 7, 2026

[#ai](/archives/?tag=ai) [#technology](/archives/?tag=technology)

[**AIL***1*](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail "AIL 1 — Human Created, Minor AI Assistance")

 Krahing…

[![Two builders with the same AI arm feeding both engines: one works inside the open engine, the other leans on a sealed purple box](/images/vibe-coding-isnt-using-ai.webp)](/images/vibe-coding-isnt-using-ai.webp)

Slight (ok medium-sized) pet peeve here.

**Vibe Coding** isn't using AI to make things.

Vibe Coding is making things where you have no idea how it works on the backend. Where the inner workings are a complete mystery to you.

📚 Andrej Karpathy [coined the term](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1886192184808149383) in February 2025, for code that "grows beyond my usual comprehension."

[Engineering](https://buildingsoftware.ai) is the opposite. You know exactly how everything works.

So you could be using AI to write 100% of your code and pushing dozens or hundreds of features/products per month, and it can still be engineering.

Or you can use AI to ship a random website where you don't know what stack it's on, in what language, how security was done, etc., and that's Vibe Coding.

The difference is your **level of understanding** of what was built, and why those decisions were made, not the use of AI.
