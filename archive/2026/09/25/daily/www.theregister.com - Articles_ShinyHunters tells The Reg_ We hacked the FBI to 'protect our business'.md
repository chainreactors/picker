ShinyHunters, the data theft and extortion crew that has stolen sensitive information belonging to [millions](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/09/10/shinyhunters-expose-64m-in-attack-on-medical-supplier-mckesson/5295550) of [cancer patients](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/08/31/healthcare-cyberattacks-hit-pacemakers-and-millions-of-patient-records/5293537), [university](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/06/11/shinyhunters-raids-nottingham-uni-for-student-alumni-data/5253961) and [K-12 students](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/05/14/security-pros-doubt-canvas-attackers-really-deleted-stolen-student-data/5240799), and [Carnival cruisers](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/05/28/carnival-shinyhunters-cruised-off-with-6m-customer-records/5247808), wanted to preserve their reputation and keep their “business” afloat. So it [hacked the FBI](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/09/22/shinyhunters-claims-fbi-hack-this-is-not-financially-motivated/5298385) to make a statement, the group told The Register.

“It’s a game and it’s the world we live in,” a ShinyHunters spokesperson told us. “We are just protecting our business as any other business would do. It’s about who does their job better.”

On Friday, the FBI confirmed the breach to The Register, after earlier in the week saying the bureau was investigating ShinyHunters’ claims.

REG AD

"The FBI is aware of a cyber-criminal enterprise group claiming a compromise of the FBIJobs.gov portal and alleged impact to FBI employee personally identifiable information (PII),” an FBI spokesperson told The Register. "While the point of breach is still undetermined - whether a third-party or the FBI’s enterprise - we are actively and aggressively investigating this matter and working closely with those third-party providers that support FBIJobs.gov to mitigate any and all risk."

REG AD

On Tuesday, the criminals told us that they broke into the bureau via yet [another](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/06/11/shinyhunters-claims-oracle-peoplesoft-0-day-hit-100-orgs/5254443) [Oracle PeopleSoft](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/06/29/nissan-says-oracle-peoplesoft-break-in-may-have-spilled-payroll-records-ssns/5263534) zero-day flaw in the FBIJobs.gov portal, which [remains down](https://apply.fbijobs.gov/) as of Friday. Then, they breached the FBI’s managed servers on AWS GovCloud and swiped thousands of personnel files belonging to current, former, and prospective FBI employees.

“We hold very sensitive data on almost ALL FBI Agents and individuals who filed an application with the FBI for a job,” the group claimed in a message posted online and addressed to FBI Director Kash Patel and Brett Leatherman, assistant director of the FBI’s Cyber Division.
