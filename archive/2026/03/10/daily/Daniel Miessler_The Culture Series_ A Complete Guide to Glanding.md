# The Culture Series: A Complete Guide to Glanding

Every drug gland secretion from Iain M. Banks' Culture novels

March 10, 2026

[#culture](/archives/?tag=culture) [#books](/archives/?tag=books) [#science-fiction](/archives/?tag=science-fiction) [#iain-banks](/archives/?tag=iain-banks)

[![The Culture Series: A Complete Guide to Glanding](/images/blog/the-culture-series-complete-guide-to-glanding/header.webp)](/images/blog/the-culture-series-complete-guide-to-glanding/header.webp)

In Iain M. Banks' [Culture series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_series), citizens have a genetically engineered organ called the drug gland that secretes psychoactive substances directly into their bloodstream on demand.

Every glanding substance mentioned or described across the ten novels:

| Substance | Effect | Category | Notable Appearance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sharp Blue** | Heightened mental acuity and pattern recognition. The thinking drug. | Cognitive | [*The Player of Games*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Player_of_Games), [*Excession*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excession), [*Surface Detail*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_Detail) |
| **Snap** | Instant alertness. Clears mental fog immediately. | Stimulant | [*The Player of Games*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Player_of_Games), [*Use of Weapons*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Use_of_Weapons) |
| **Calm** | Reduces anxiety, promotes tranquility without dulling awareness. | Emotional | [*Excession*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excession), [*Look to Windward*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look_to_Windward), [*The Hydrogen Sonata*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hydrogen_Sonata) |
| **Gain** | Pure euphoria and pleasure amplification. | Recreational | [*The Player of Games*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Player_of_Games) |
| **Focus** | Deep concentration on a single task. | Cognitive | [*Excession*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excession) |
| **Diffuse** | Opposite of Focus. Broadens awareness, promotes lateral thinking. | Cognitive | [*Excession*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excession) |
| **Quicken** | Accelerates subjective perception of time. Seconds stretch into minutes. | Perception | [*Use of Weapons*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Use_of_Weapons), [*Excession*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excession) |
| **Slow** | Decelerates subjective time. Makes long waits feel brief. | Perception | [*Look to Windward*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look_to_Windward), [*The Hydrogen Sonata*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hydrogen_Sonata) |
| **Softnow** | Gentle contentment. A warm sense of the present moment. | Emotional | [*The Hydrogen Sonata*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hydrogen_Sonata) |
| **Crystal Fugue State** | Deep altered consciousness for processing complex information or grief. | Altered State | [*Excession*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excession), [*Look to Windward*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look_to_Windward) |
| **Recall** | Enhanced memory retrieval. Makes past experiences vivid and accessible. | Cognitive | [*Look to Windward*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look_to_Windward) |
| **Crush** | Emotional dampening for grief, trauma, or overwhelm. Compresses feelings rather than eliminating them. | Emotional | [*Look to Windward*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look_to_Windward), [*Surface Detail*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_Detail) |
| **Rage** | Controlled aggression and combat readiness while maintaining tactical thinking. | Combat | [*Use of Weapons*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Use_of_Weapons) |
| **Wam** | Physical warmth and comfort. Counteracts cold environments. | Physical | [*The Player of Games*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Player_of_Games) |
| **Cool** | Reduces body temperature. The thermal opposite of Wam. | Physical | [*The Player of Games*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Player_of_Games) |
| **Somnabsolute** | Guaranteed perfect sleep. Instant, deep, restorative. | Sleep | [*Excession*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excession) |
| **Wake** | Instant full wakefulness from any depth of sleep. Zero grogginess. | Stimulant | [*Excession*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excession) |
| **Thrill** | Adrenaline rush without the panic. Pure excitement and heightened readiness. | Stimulant | [*The Hydrogen Sonata*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hydrogen_Sonata) |
| **Dampener** | Reduces overall emotional intensity. More general than Crush. | Emotional | [*Surface Detail*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_Detail) |
| **Glide** | Social ease and charm. Enhances conversational fluency. | Social | [*The Player of Games*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Player_of_Games) |
| **Scape** | Mild psychedelic state. Enhances sensory perception and aesthetic appreciation. | Recreational | [*The Player of Games*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Player_of_Games) |
| **Adrenalize** | Floods the system with combat-ready hormones. Fight without the flight. | Combat | [*Consider Phlebas*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consider_Phlebas), [*Use of Weapons*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Use_of_Weapons) |
| **Detox** | Rapidly clears other substances from the bloodstream. The reset button. | Utility | Multiple novels |
| **Pain-block** | Selective pain suppression without loss of sensation. Can target specific regions or go systemic. | Medical | [*Use of Weapons*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Use_of_Weapons), [*Surface Detail*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_Detail) |
| **Sexual enhancement** | Heightened arousal, intensified sensation, extended pleasure. Fully customizable. | Recreational | [*The Player of Games*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Player_of_Games), [*Excession*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excession) |
| **Immune boost** | Accelerates immune response for novel pathogens. | Medical | Multiple novels |
