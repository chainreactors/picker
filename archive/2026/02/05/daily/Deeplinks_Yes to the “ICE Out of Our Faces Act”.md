Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) and Customs and Border Protection (CBP) have [descended into utter lawlessness](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/01/eff-statement-lawless-actions-ice-and-cbp), most recently in [Minnesota](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/01/eff-close-friday-solidarity-national-shutdown). The violence is shocking. So are the intrusions on digital rights and civil liberties. For example, immigration agents are routinely scanning faces of people they suspect of unlawful presence in the country – [100,000 times](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/ice-facial-recognition-app-mobile-fortify-dfdd00bf?gaa_at=eafs&gaa_n=AWEtsqe3OVPfe6cO4b8_aZgmFfLY-knlR_vuv98eX3Oq08BQ6FclhexqiRek&gaa_ts=698141d3&gaa_sig=Hi7WtlTmdWn9ZvwSqCrYkmAUDPq2_F5hjMAZJ-NLAaA1O_oymZf2cQ0c50yG5NUkhVtU0_mG6uw7dt9eU63G7A%3D%3D), according to the Wall Street Journal. The technology has already [misidentified](https://www.404media.co/ices-facial-recognition-app-misidentified-a-woman-twice/) at least one person, according to 404 Media.

Face recognition technology is so dangerous that [government should not use it at all](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2021/10/resisting-menace-face-recognition)—least of all these out-of-control immigration agencies.

To combat these abuses, EFF is proud to support the “[ICE Out of Our Faces Act](https://www.markey.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/ice_out_of_our_faces_act.pdf).” This new federal bill would ban ICE and CBP agents, and some local police working with them, from acquiring or using biometric surveillance systems, including face recognition technology, or information derived from such systems by another entity.
