# [Attackers Heat French Supermarket Fridges Up to 50°C by Hijacking Danfoss Controllers](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attackers-heat-french-supermarket.html)

**Swati Khandelwal**Oct 13, 2026Cyber Attack / Network Security

[![](data:image/png;base64...)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEh64tQNgn3aqDhyphenhyphengoK8NxQG3DPvWWi0fP185meJUr14eUmcX18mKJdkUz6QsMU6bTxmwAtHrd8I0J5FQjvTENA1-N1F60zsR1x2iDzaJNhPbg_6iGYTELpBOwiTbBiaMWLmWQ4naaMfCLCcThNEfC302zF-YCjlev6138DvUrXfr5noGCpZaT9JnGd7hUw/s1700-nu-rw-lo-l85-e365/supermarket.jpg)

Attackers took remote control of fridges and freezers at hundreds of French supermarkets on the night of October 11 and warmed them, forcing stores to throw food away, several retail chains said.

The attackers reached them through the controller that runs all of a store's fridges and freezers, a device made by Danish company Danfoss that maintenance firms can manage remotely. Les Mousquetaires, which owns the Intermarché chain, said its fridges and freezers were put into defrost and heated to as much as 50°C.

CERT-FR, the French government's computer emergency response team, said in an [alert published in French](https://www.cert.ssi.gouv.fr/alerte/CERTFR-2026-ALE-012/) on October 12 that it is aware of numerous compromises affecting two Danfoss controller series, the AK-SM and the AK-SC. It told operators to disconnect the controllers or filter access to them.

CERT-FR is part of ANSSI, France's national cybersecurity agency. ANSSI's head, Vincent Strubel, called it urgent to take the two series off the internet or filter access very tightly, [AFP reported](https://www.lalibre.be/dernieres-depeches/2026/10/12/des-supermarches-francais-touches-par-une-cyberattaque-inedite-de-leurs-installations-frigorifiques-TRPRUN2A5VH2BFX7SOKZEXGIEY/).

Neither French authorities nor Danfoss has so far said who carried out the attack or how the attackers got into the controllers. Danfoss said most of the units hit were older models that it no longer updates.

### Which Chains Were Hit

The chains gave their own figures to AFP, Reuters and broadcaster RTL. Each counts the stores the attack reached.

| Retailer | Stores Reported Hit | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intermarché and Netto (Les Mousquetaires) | At least 300 across both brands, per AFP. About 150 stores, per Reuters | Company statements, October 12 |
| Coopérative U | 80 of 2,000 | Chief executive Dominique Schelcher, on RTL, October 13 |
| Carrefour | 80 supermarkets | Company statements to AFP and Reuters, October 12 |
| Monoprix | About 30 | Company statement to AFP, October 12 |
| E.Leclerc | Several stores | Company statement to AFP, October 12 |

A store on that list did not always lose its stock. Schelcher told RTL that the cold chain held in most of the 80 Coopérative U stores hit, and that only a minority could not react fast enough and lost goods, [according to AFP](https://www.strategies.fr/actualites/culture-tech/LQ6738014C/cyberattaque-dans-les-supermarches-le-pdg-de-cooperative-u-tempere-l-ampleur-des-degats.html). He praised the quick reaction of store staff and refrigeration contractors.

Intermarché, by contrast, said its affected stores had to throw away all the products concerned.

[![Cybersecurity](data:image/png;base64...)](https://thehackernews.uk/growth-ai-control-d)

Thierry Cotillard, who heads Les Mousquetaires, told broadcaster BFMTV he expected losses of tens of millions of euros across France, [according to Reuters](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-13/french-supermarkets-hacked-causing-fires-and-food-loss/107258724).

In its statements on October 12, Danfoss gave no figure for how many systems were hit. It told Reuters it was "aware of incidents in France affecting a limited number of refrigeration management systems."

### Which Controllers, and Whether They Can Be Patched

Danfoss [told AFP](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/cyberattack-supermarkets-refrigerators-france-fires-food-losses/) that most of the units hit were older products that no longer get software or security updates, and it named two: the AK-SM 800 and the AK-SC 355.

Danfoss declared the AK-SM 800 end-of-life in May 2021 and phased out the AK-SC 355 in 2016. In an [October 2025 newsletter](https://www.danfoss.com/en/service-and-support/articles/dcs/tech-insider-october-2025/), it said it would end all services for such older products and urged customers to plan replacements.

Some units of the current model, the AK-SM 800A, were also hit. Those were running outdated firmware, according to Danfoss's statement to trade publication [Cooling Post](https://www.coolingpost.com/world-news/danfoss-moves-to-mitigate-cyberattack/), which did not name the versions.

| Controller | Status, According to Danfoss | Advice From CERT-FR and Danfoss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AK-SM 800 | End of life since May 2021. No software or security updates | Disconnect it or filter access. Danfoss urges replacement |
| AK-SC 355 | Phased out in 2016. No software or security updates | Disconnect it or filter access. Danfoss urges replacement |
| AK-SM 800A | Current model. Package 5.3.x, dated October 2026, was the latest listed on October 13 | Disconnect it or filter access. Danfoss advises keeping firmware up to date |

As of October 13, Danfoss's [security advisories page](https://www.danfoss.com/en/service-and-support/coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure/danfoss-security-advisories/) listed no advisory about the attack or about either older model.

### What Operators Should Do

CERT-FR's first instruction covers every AK-SM and AK-SC controller: disconnect it or filter access to it. Exposing a management interface to the internet goes against good practice, the alert says.
