**[![Vir.IT eXplorer for Windows achieves in July 2026  - VB100 Test](https://www.tgsoft.it/immagini/news/VirusBulletin/VB100_FB_202607.png "Vir.IT eXplorer for Windows achieves in July 2026  - VB100 Test")](https://www.tgsoft.it/immagini/news/VirusBulletin/VB100_FB_202607.png "OpsWat Certified Products")**

**TG Soft** is pleased to announce that **Vir.IT eXplorer PRO** passed the latest **‘VB100’ test** carried out in **July 2026** with excellent results.
With this certification **Vir.IT eXplorer PRO** has achieved over **40 VB100** awards, demonstrating its ongoing commitment to ensuring an efficient product that is always up to date against new threats.

The **VB100** award is a certification programme launched in 1998 by Virus Bulletin in London, one of the most authoritative independent testing laboratories. VB100 certification is awarded to products that meet the strict criteria of comparative tests carried out by VB laboratories to assess security on different operating systems. These products are therefore recognized as effective and working anti-malware solutions. Its purpose is to verify whether antivirus products detect malware listed in the ‘WildList’ and do not block ‘good’ files. The test result is binary, either positive or negative.

## VB100 certification and passed tests

The **VB100** award is a certification granted to products that meet fundamental standards according to the strict criteria of comparative tests conducted by VB laboratories, which evaluate security across different operating systems, and are therefore recognized as effective and reliable anti-malware solutions.

The product is tested on Windows 11 Pro, where is assesssed the antivirus' ability:

* **to detect and block** malware in the file set during Certification**;**
* **not to report false positives** when scanning the set of harmless Virus Bulletin files
