# Privacy's Defenders Podcast: The Secret Room

![Privacy's Defender: Stories From the Fights for Digital Rights](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/pd-podcast-banner.png?itok=esO9M5UT "Privacy's Defender: Stories From the Fights for Digital Rights")

The government’s surveillance powers grew rapidly after Sept. 11, 2001, some in public view and some in secret. EFF suspected but couldn’t prove that the National Security Agency was spying on Americans' communications. The New York Times sat on a story about it for over a year, finally publishing it in December 2005. Then, in January 2006, a retired AT&T technician named [Mark Klein](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/03/memoriam-mark-klein-att-whistleblower-about-nsa-mass-spying) walked into EFF's office with a manila folder of documents that blew the story wide open.

Welcome to the third episode of “Privacy’s Defenders,” a podcast about the people – lawyers, journalists, hackers, and others – who’ve fought to secure your digital liberties since before most people even knew what the internet was.
