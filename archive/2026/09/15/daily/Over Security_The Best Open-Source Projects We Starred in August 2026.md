August's stars leaned heavily on AI agent tooling and everyday dev-workflow essentials, with a couple of blockchain/wallet picks thrown in. 1. [shadcn/ui](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui?ref=hackerstribe.com) - Composable, accessible UI components with thoughtful defaults, build your own component library on top of Radix and Tailwind. (123.8k stars)
2. [FastAPI](https://github.com/fastapi/fastapi?ref=hackerstribe.com) - High-performance Python web framework, easy to learn, fast to code, ready for production. (102.3k stars)
3. [free-for-dev](https://github.com/ripienaar/free-for-dev?ref=hackerstribe.com) - A massive curated list of SaaS, PaaS and IaaS offerings with free tiers for developers and infra folks. (137.4k stars)
