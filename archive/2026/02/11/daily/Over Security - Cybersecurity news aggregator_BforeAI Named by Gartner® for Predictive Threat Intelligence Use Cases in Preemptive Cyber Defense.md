BforeAI Named ‘Tech Innovator in Preemptive Cybersecurity’ by Gartner®

New York, New York – January 8, 2025

New York, New York – February 11, 2026 – BforeAI, a leader in predictive attack intelligence, today highlighted its inclusion in Gartner latest research report, Emerging Tech: Top Use Cases in Preemptive Cyber Defense, which examines how preemptive technologies are reshaping modern cybersecurity strategies.

The Gartner report identifies predictive threat intelligence (PTI) as a critical use case within preemptive cyber defense, enabling organizations to anticipate and disrupt cyberattacks before they materialize. Gartner’s case-based research found that organizations adopting preemptive tech cuts investigation time by 65%, offering instant forensic data for swift action.
