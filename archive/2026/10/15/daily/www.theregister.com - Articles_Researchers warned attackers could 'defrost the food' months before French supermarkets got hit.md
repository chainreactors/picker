Security researchers warned about exploitable vulnerabilities in refrigeration controllers months - and in one case, more than a year - before cyberattacks disrupted hundreds of French supermarkets.

Late Sunday night, unknown criminals hacked thousands of controllers, [cranking the heat](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/13/cyberattacks-crank-french-supermarket-refrigeration-systems-up-to-50c/5302784) as high as 122°F (50°C), spoiling food and sparking fires across nearly [700 of the country’s supermarkets](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/cyberattacks-disrupt-refrigeration-systems-at-european-supermarkets).

France's national cybersecurity agency ANSSI on Monday [warned](https://cert.cyber.gouv.fr/alerte/CERTFR-2026-ALE-012/) of widespread compromises involving Danfoss refrigeration controllers, and a day later added affected systems from Copeland and its Dixell brand. The compromised devices were accessible over the internet, with many reportedly no longer receiving software or security updates and others running outdated firmware.

REG AD

Danfoss did not immediately respond to The Register’s request for comment. Copeland emailed us the following statement:

REG AD

“We acknowledge the vulnerability identified in a discontinued, end-of-life generation of controllers. Protecting our customers’ operations has always been a top priority. We are currently working with impacted customers to mitigate the risk. We will continue to support customers in strengthening cybersecurity through supported products, current software and industry-recommended practices.”

Operational technology (OT) security firm Claroty’s Team82 sounded the alarm on these buggy devices in August, warning that hackers could “freeze the controller” and “defrost the food.” At the time, the researchers published details of dozens of vulnerabilities, including authentication bypass and operating-system command execution flaws, affecting [Danfoss AK-SM 800A](https://claroty.com/team82/research/freeze-the-controller-defrost-the-food-uncovering-vulnerabilities-in-danfoss-refrigeration-controllers) and [Copeland XWEB controllers](https://claroty.com/team82/research/chilling-discoveries-unpacking-vulnerabilities-in-copeland-xweb-pro-controllers).
