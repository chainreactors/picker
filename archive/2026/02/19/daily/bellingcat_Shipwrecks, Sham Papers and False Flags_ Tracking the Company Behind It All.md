# Shipwrecks, Sham Papers and False Flags: Tracking the Company Behind It All

February 19, 2026

* [Environment](/tag/environment)
* [False Flags](/tag/false-flags)
* [Sanctions](/tag/sanctions)

A shipwreck in India, an ammunition seizure in Senegal, and a raid on an oil tanker in Malaysia – all three incidents involve ageing vessels, operating with false papers and one recurring figure: Captain Suniel Kumar Sharma.

For over a decade, Sharma has been condemned by the governments of [Dominica,](https://dominica-registry.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/CD-MSC-06-14-Rev01-Known-Instances-of-False-Representation-and-Fraudulent-Certificates.pdf) [Guyana](https://web.archive.org/web/20260218155106/https%3A//marina.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Circular-Letter-No.4502-Communication-From-The-Government-Of-The-Co-Operative-Republic-OfGuyana-Secretariat.pdf), [Samoa](https://www.ft.com/content/77bc3b1e-25e8-495e-8547-4f366611dd59), [the Federated States of Micronesia](https://www.facebook.com/piofsm/posts/the-federated-states-of-micronesia-files-criminal-case-against-suniel-k-sharma-d/2965951210153824/) and [Eswatini](https://www.ft.com/content/77bc3b1e-25e8-495e-8547-4f366611dd59), as well as the [UN International Maritime Organisation (IMO)](https://web.archive.org/web/20260218155106/https%3A//marina.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Circular-Letter-No.4502-Communication-From-The-Government-Of-The-Co-Operative-Republic-OfGuyana-Secretariat.pdf), for issuing fraudulent paperwork to vessels, including false flag certificates.

In a recent [interview with the Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/77bc3b1e-25e8-495e-8547-4f366611dd59), Sharma said his most prominent flag registry, the International Maritime Safety Agency of Guyana (IMSAG), was no longer operational. However, a Bellingcat investigation has found certificates issued by IMSAG as recently as December 2025. In the same interview, Sharma [denied setting up any more registries](https://www.ft.com/content/77bc3b1e-25e8-495e-8547-4f366611dd59). Yet Bellingcat has found evidence of a newly launched website linked to Sharma offering flag registration in Nicaragua.

In June 2020, a [typhoon off the Indian coast](https://x.com/FSUIINDIA/status/1268069826641223681) forced the 38-year-old oil tanker, MT Basra Star (IMO 8515817), to run aground. Despite an [insurance inspector’s report](https://web.archive.org/web/20260218153345/https%3A//environmentclearance.nic.in/writereaddata/Online/TOR/07_Mar_2022_16503939335399526Annexure-AdditionalAttachmentsFile.pdf) recommending her immediate demolition, the vessel remained for five years, rusting away on the beach, until she was finally [scrapped in January this year](https://www.lokmat.com/ratnagiri/the-basra-star-ship-stranded-on-mirya-beach-in-ratnagiri-will-be-scrapped-a-a746/).

![Screenshot of Instagram post, December 2025. ](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/02/image1-1-1200x888.jpg)

*The Basra Star became a local tourist attraction during the five years it lay rusting on the beach. Instagram post, December 2025.*

[The insurance report](https://web.archive.org/web/20260218153345/https%3A//environmentclearance.nic.in/writereaddata/Online/TOR/07_Mar_2022_16503939335399526Annexure-AdditionalAttachmentsFile.pdf) states Basra Star was sailing under a Samoan flag and its classification society (the company that certifies the vessel as seaworthy) was Ascent Navals.

Two years before MT Basra Star ran aground, the Samoan government and the IMO [issued a warning](http://sing-lloyd.com/media/circulars/MCI-Circular-181__Notice_of_Fraudulent_Ship_Registry_and_Recognised_Organisation.pdf) about a fraudulent company called Ascent Navals, and its director, Captain Suniel Kumar Sharma, for appearing to operate on behalf of Samoa, but without official authorisation.

![Screenshot of part of the IMO circular warning, dated 6 June 2018.](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/02/image5-edited-1.jpg)

**Screenshot of part of the* [*IMO circular warning*](http://sing-lloyd.com/media/circulars/MCI-Circular-181__Notice_of_Fraudulent_Ship_Registry_and_Recognised_Organisation.pdf)*, dated 6 June 2018.**

By sailing under [a false flag (Samoa False)](http://sing-lloyd.com/media/circulars/MCI-Circular-181__Notice_of_Fraudulent_Ship_Registry_and_Recognised_Organisation.pdf) and a [fraudulent classification society (Ascent Navals)](http://sing-lloyd.com/media/circulars/MCI-Circular-181__Notice_of_Fraudulent_Ship_Registry_and_Recognised_Organisation.pdf), when the worst-case scenario did occur, [no jurisdiction (flag state) was legally responsible](https://www.imo.org/en/ourwork/legal/pages/registration-of-ships-and-fraudulent-registration-matters.aspx) for the marooned ship.

Bellingcat contacted the vessel’s owners, Shat Al Arab Marine Supply LLC, the insurance surveyors, Uday Bhogate & Associates, and Ascent Navals and its director, Suniel Kumar Sharma. None responded to requests for comment.

Nearly two years after Basra Star was shipwrecked, another ageing vessel, Eolika (IMO 8214968), was found operating under a false flag while laden with illicit cargo.
