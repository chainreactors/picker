Key takeaways

1. 01

   Dutch police seized dozens of Virtualine servers at a data center in Limburg.
2. 02

   Authorities obtained data on more than 3,000 customers allegedly using the service for criminal activity.
3. 03

   Police say Virtualine supported ransomware, phishing, botnets, information-stealing malware, and child sexual abuse material distribution.
4. 04

   The FBI and German police helped seize cryptocurrency and disrupt Virtualine’s infrastructure internationally.
