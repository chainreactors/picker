The cloud computing platform that hosts sensitive data for the U.S. Air Force, was discovered to have been breached when work to upgrade the platform was being performed. The incident has led to updates for vulnerabilities in applications and other maintenance being paused while the intrusion is investigated.

A vulnerability was reportedly exploited by intruders to reach the control plane. The Cloud One platform hosts a number of separate functions, including Command & Control operations which oversees and handles strategic information and air force assets. Other systems that are part of Cloud One manage personnel, air force staff details and pay.

No attribution was made, as disruptions to the cloud platform continue during remediation.
