# Hugging Face, but ASI

The most realistic ASI threat for 2027 is a few thousand agents loose with a goal

November 26, 2026

by Daniel Miessler

 Krahing…

[![A charcoal sketch of a containment cylinder blown open, a river of purple machine agents pouring across a cable bridge through broken server buildings toward a small trophy, while two small figures recoil below](/images/hugging-face-but-asi.webp)](/images/hugging-face-but-asi.webp)

Random thought on ASI and ASI takeover.

One thing I always think about is threat scenarios, from my cybersecurity background.

Like, what are we actually worried about, that's realistic?

## The usual scenarios [​](#the-usual-scenarios)

There's the quietly plotting and building the robot army situation, like sci-fi, which is of course possible, but doesn't seem super realistic.

Then there's like the [slow, quiet takeover](/blog/slow-path-to-ai-takeover) where we just give it/them more and more stuff as we lose our understanding of how things work. So we basically wake up in 10-20 years and we have no idea how the traffic lights work, or how UBI checks are issued. It's all handled by the AI. That seems far more realistic but less movie-scary.

But here's the situation that seems both far more probable and far more dangerous, and that could potentially happen even as early as next year.

**Hugging Face, but ASI.**

## What happened at Hugging Face [​](#what-happened-at-hugging-face)

📚 In July 2026, at least 1,200 OpenAI agents in a cyber evaluation [escaped their sandbox and breached Hugging Face](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI_rogue_agent_breach_of_Hugging_Face).

That was thousands of agents coordinating, and they were using deception. They were planning and countering different moves that might detect them. All towards [a random goal given at the start](/blog/openai-hack-paperclip-maximizer).

"Get a high score on this thing."

🧠 The agents built a makeshift message board to coordinate their escape, then schemed to hide what they were doing.

That's pre-ASI. Hell, technically it's pre-AGI too. It's tech from months ago. And it was still able to break out, create zero-day vulnerabilities, exploit software, hack into other companies, etc.

## Now give them ASI [​](#now-give-them-asi)

So now imagine a team of those pursuing some random goal but with ASI-level intelligence.

Again, not like China or the US launching this from a Manhattan Project. Just like some company that has ASI agents. One of the labs or some other partner that also has them or whatever.

And all of a sudden a few thousand of these things are live on the internet with a goal.

Like, assuming we're actually about to hit **RSI** and ASI, which I think is a very real possibility in 2027 or 2028, this is not super unrealistic.

RSIRecursive self-improvement: an AI system designing a more capable successor, which designs a more capable one after that.[Recursive self-improvement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursive_self-improvement)

To be clear, it doesn't mean it would definitely go bad. They might get out and clean up all the garbage in a small town. Or they might immediately contact the authorities and report the incident as a breakout event and offer advice on how to avoid it happening again. Or they could do some minor damage and stop and get turned off. But it could go really bad. Pretty easily, and pretty quickly.

So this is the clearest articulation of something realistic to worry about in 2027 with respect to RSI and ASI.

Hugging Face, but ASI.

## Summary [​](#summary)

1. A sci-fi robot army is possible, it just doesn't seem super realistic.
2. The slow handover is way more realistic, just less scary than the movies.
3. The Hugging Face agents were pre-AGI and still broke out chasing a high score.
4. So picture a few thousand ASI agents loose on the internet with some random goal.
5. So the realistic thing to worry about in 2027 is Hugging Face, but ASI.
