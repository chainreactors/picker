China-aligned attackers used AI agents and compromised ConnectWise application credentials to target more than 2,250 Microsoft 365 tenants, Microsoft says.

The Windows giant [revealed this week](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/10/21/storm-0940-uses-artex-agentic-ai-connectwise-supply-chain-compromise/) that the attackers, tracked as Storm-0940, used an open source AI penetration-testing tool called ARTEX to automate attacks against Microsoft 365 environments. In some cases, they went from picking a target to gaining privileged cloud access in less than three hours.

The findings shed more light on the[ConnectWise security incident](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/19/connectwise-turns-off-its-saas-y-backup-security-and-management-services-to-probe-security-snafu/5303758), which saw the IT management vendor pull the plug on its Cloud Backup, SaaS Security, and Cloud Management services after discovering unauthorized access to application identities.

REG AD

ConnectWise didn't initially identify who was responsible, but Microsoft has now pointed the finger at Storm-0940, a China-linked espionage crew that's been operating since at least 2021.

REG AD

The group has traditionally relied on the usual tricks, including password spraying and exploiting internet-facing applications, but this time it used AI to do some of the heavy lifting.

ARTEX is an open source penetration-testing framework written in Go that uses AI agents to automate everything from reconnaissance and vulnerability discovery to credential attacks and data theft. Microsoft says Storm-0940 began using it as early as September to target organizations worldwide, including government agencies, defense contractors, banks, manufacturers, and tech firms.

Between October 10 and 14, the hackers made more than 151,000 failed sign-in attempts against 327 accounts at eight organizations. Microsoft observed no successful logins from that password-spraying campaign, but then the attackers shifted to compromised ConnectWise application identities.

The compromised application credentials let the attackers exploit permissions customers had already granted to ConnectWise services, without needing an interactive user login.

Armed with ARTEX, Storm-0940 attempted to authenticate against more than 2,250 Microsoft 365 tenants, at times reaching 2,900 attempts a minute.
