# University of Pennsylvania Data Breach

## What Happened

In October 2025, [the University of Pennsylvania was the victim of a data breach followed by a ransom demand](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/university-of-pennsylvania-hacker-claims-1.2-million-donor-data-breach/), largely affecting its donor database. After the incident, the attackers sent inflammatory emails to some victims. [The data was later published online in February 2026](https://www.thedp.com/article/2026/02/penn-hack-donor-data-ransom-one-million-shinyhunters-gse-emai) and included 624k unique email addresses alongside names and physical addresses. For some donor records, additional personal information was exposed, including gender and date of birth. A small subset of records also contained religion, spouse name, estimated income and donation history.
