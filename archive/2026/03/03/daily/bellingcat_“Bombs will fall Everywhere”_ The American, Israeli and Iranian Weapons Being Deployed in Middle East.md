# “Bombs will fall Everywhere”: The American, Israeli and Iranian Weapons Being Deployed in Middle East

March 3, 2026

* [Conflict and Human Rights Team](/tag/conflict-and-human-rights-team)
* [Iran](/tag/iran)
* [Israel](/tag/israel)

The United States and Israel launched an attack on Iran on Saturday morning, killing Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei as well as several senior regime figures and striking multiple sites across the country. Iran retaliated by firing at targets across the region, including Israel, Bahrain, Qatar, the UAE and other Gulf states. The conflict is ongoing despite [no declaration of war by the US Congress](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/watch-limiting-trumps-authority-with-war-powers-act-is-dangerous-johnson-says). US President Donald Trump initially called for regime change in Iran but has since [delivered a mixed message](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-contradicts-us-intelligence-iran-address-regime-change/story?id=130684219) about the aims of “Operation Epic Fury”.

Israel has said it dropped more than [2,000 bombs](https://www.timesofisrael.com/idf-over-2000-bombs-dropped-on-iran-in-30-hours-air-supremacy-achieved-on-1st-day/) in the first 30 hours of the war. While the US claims to have struck [over 1,000 targets](https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/Article/4418959/secretary-of-war-pete-hegseth-and-chairman-of-the-joint-chiefs-of-staff-gen-dan/) in the first 24 hours, with President Trump [stating](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/feb/28/iran-vows-no-leniency-reprisal-attacks-israel-us-air-bases) that “bombs will fall everywhere”. In response, Iran is reported to have launched [at least 390 missiles and 830 drones](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/03/01/world/middleeast/gulf-states-strikes.html) in the first two days.
Bellingcat has been monitoring strikes across the region, including those that caused [civilian harm](https://bsky.app/profile/bellingcat.com/post/3mfwwf6v4oc23), and identified a wide variety of weapons have been used so far, including missiles and drones.

## **US-Made Weapons and Tomahawks Launched**

The US [reported](https://youtu.be/ZJRXDHljBrM?t=1281) that some of the first weapons they launched were Tomahawk missiles. [Footage from the US McFaul](https://x.com/Osinttechnical/status/2028320464548819386?s=20) also showed Tomahawks being launched.

There is also reporting that a [new variant of the Tomahawk](https://www.twz.com/sea/black-tomahawk-cruise-missile-seen-for-the-first-time-in-strikes-on-iran) was used in these strikes.

Imagery of many other different munitions used by the US, Israel and Iran have appeared on social media.

This article covers some of the munitions Bellingcat has seen imagery of as the war enters its fourth day.

Many of the weapons used so far have also been deployed in other recent US conflicts, including the [12-day Israel-Iran war](https://osmp.ngo/collection/iran-israel-conflict-june-2025/), and US strikes in [Yemen](https://osmp.ngo/collection/us-munitions-in-yemen/) and [Venezuela](https://osmp.ngo/location/venezuela/).

The US is the major supplier of arms to allies in the region, including for Israel, Kuwait, Qatar, UAE, and Jordan.

On Sunday, the US Department Of Defence (DOD) published photos showing weapons being prepared for loading on aircrafts, including the MK-80 series of bombs like MK-82 500-pound bombs, and BLU-109 2,000-pound ‘bunker busters’ equipped with Joint Direct Attack Munition (JDAM) bomb guidance kits.

![Left:  Feb. 27. 500-pound bombs equipped with JDAM guidance kits. Right: Feb. 28. 2,000-pound BLU-109 ‘bunker busters’ equipped with JDAM guidance kits. Sources: US Navy/DVIDS and US NAVY/DVIDS. ](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/03/Screenshot-2026-03-03-at-11.33.59.jpg)

Left: Feb. 27. 500-pound bombs equipped with JDAM guidance kits. Right: Feb. 28. 2,000-pound BLU-109 ‘bunker busters’ equipped with JDAM guidance kits. Sources: US Navy/DVIDS and US NAVY/DVIDS.

The DOD has also released several photos showing the C variants of the [AGM-154 Joint Standoff Weapon (JSOW)](https://www.navair.navy.mil/product/jsow). As documented by the [Open Source Munitions Portal](https://osmp.ngo/?s=AGm-154), this weapon has been used recently by the US in Yemen and Venezuela.

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/03/Screenshot-2026-03-03-at-11.35.41.jpg)

*Feb. 27. AGM-154C JSOW bombs being loaded onto aircraft. Source:* [*US Navy/DVIDS*](https://www.dvidshub.net/image/9542594/us-forces-launch-operation-epic-fury)

The DOD also released a slideshow showing images from the first 24 hours of the war, including an image showing the [first combat use of the Precision Strike Missile](https://www.twz.com/land/americas-new-prsm-ballistic-missile-just-made-its-combat-debut-in-iran-strikes). The DOD further [released a list of some equipment used](https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2028196633230348358?s=20), including the [THAAD ballistic missile defense system](https://missilethreat.csis.org/system/thaad/).

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/03/Screenshot-2026-03-03-at-11.36.55.jpg)

*Image of a Precision Strike Missile being fired in the first 24 hours of the war. Source:* [*US CENTCOM*](https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2027990979253334026?s=20)*.*

Many of the weapons deployed by the US have also been used by Israel. This includes the MK-80 series of bombs, BLU-109 bombs and Joint Direct Attack Munition (JDAM) bomb guidance kits.

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/03/Screenshot-2026-03-03-at-12.09.41-1200x623.jpg)

A *Feb. 28. image shows an IAF F-15 equipped with a BLU-109 bomb with a JDAM guidance kit. Source:* [*Israeli Air Force*](https://x.com/IAFsite/status/2027683687765070145?s=20)*.*

Israel also produces some of its own munitions, which they released video or photos of since the start of the conflict, including MK-83 1,000-pound bombs equipped with Israeli [SPICE-1000 bomb guidance kits](https://www.rafael.co.il/system/spice-family-1000-2000/).

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/03/Screenshot-2026-03-03-at-11.39.57.jpg)
