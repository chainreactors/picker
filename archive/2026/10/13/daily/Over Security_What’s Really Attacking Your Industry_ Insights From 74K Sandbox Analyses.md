What’s Really Attacking Your Industry? Insights From 74K Sandbox Analyses

Every day, thousands of SOC and IR teams worldwide analyze suspicious files, emails, and links with [ANY.RUN](https://any.run/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=threat-data-for-enterprises&utm_term=131026&utm_content=linktolanding). Beyond individual investigations, this activity generates [valuable data](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/h1-2026-cyber-risk-report/) on emerging threats, industry trends, and SOC operational performance.

Drawing on real-world investigations across thousands of organizations, ANY.RUN provides visibility into threats affecting [different sectors](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/phishing-risk-industries/), from banking to retail. Security teams can see not only what they’re encountering, but also how those threats compare with the broader industry landscape.

Detecting a single threat gives you a verdict. Understanding it in the context of your industry’s threat landscape helps you make a decision.

This visibility helps SOC leaders benchmark performance, identify emerging risks, and make informed decisions about detection and response priorities.

To show what this looks like in practice, we examined 74,086 [Sandbox](https://any.run/features/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=threat-data-for-enterprises&utm_term=131026&utm_content=linktosandboxlanding) analyses from one financial organization over a quarter and compared its findings with [banking](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/phishing-us-finance/)-sector benchmarks.

## Key Takeaways

* **Threat data reveals more than individual verdicts.** It shows which submissions are most likely to be malicious, how quickly threats are detected, and which malware families are prevalent.

* **Industry intelligence puts findings into context.** Comparing an organization’s data with industry benchmarks reveals differences in threat exposure and helps prioritize security efforts.

* **ANY.RUN already has visibility into threats across industries.** Organizations can benefit from this knowledge without waiting to encounter every threat themselves.

* **These insights can be applied to your own organization.** ANY.RUN offers [personalized demos](https://any.run/enterprise/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=threat-data-for-enterprises&utm_term=131026&utm_content=linktoenterprise#contact-sales) using your organization’s data to show its threat landscape, SOC performance metrics, and industry comparisons.

## What Can Threat Data Reveal About Your Organization?

Every suspicious file or URL analyzed contributes to a bigger picture of an organization’s threat landscape. Over time, this data reveals which submissions are most likely to be malicious, how quickly threats are detected, and where potential risks are concentrated.

Combined with industry-wide intelligence, these findings help security leaders understand not just what’s happening in their own environment, but how it compares with [threats affecting their sector](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/us-soc-pain-points/).

To illustrate this, we’ll use data from 74,086 [Sandbox](https://any.run/features/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=threat-data-for-enterprises&utm_term=131026&utm_content=linktosandboxlanding) analyses conducted by an anonymized financial organization between March 30 and June 22, 2026.

The findings reveal some interesting differences between individual threat exposure and industry-wide trends.

### Data Methodology & Privacy

The statistics in this article are generated automatically from public ANY.RUN analyses and telemetry shared by participating organizations. Industry-wide insights are based on aggregated data, without manual access to customers’ private analyses. Organization-specific reports are accessible only to the respective customer.
