Contacts without a full or structured name fail vCard 4.0 validation during publication. They now receive a full name from their organization, email, phone, or “Unnamed Contact”.

Generated names preserve decoded text, remove matching email or phone URI prefixes regardless of case, and refresh when their source changes or is removed. Imported names remain intact, including names marked `DERIVED=true`.

This PR covers vCard name generation and ownership. Repairing names saved by older versions is outside its scope.
