In the first half of March 2026 I collected 95 events (6.34 events/day) with a threat landscape dominated by **malware** with **47%**, (it was 42% in the second half of March), once ahead of **account takeovers** (up to **16%** from 14%) and **ransomware** (down to **8%** from 11%), which confirmed their positions.

As always, **cyber crime** led the **motivations** chart with **63%**, nearly the same value of the [previous timeline](https://www.hackmageddon.com/2026/03/06/16-28-february-2026-cyber-attacks-timeline/), when it was 62%; once again ahead of **cyber espionage** down at **16%** from 21%. **Hacktivism** is back in the chart, fueled by the events in the Middle East, with **5%**, whilst **Cyber warfare** closes the chart, with **3%**, slightly down from the previous fortnight when it was 4%.

In this first half or March, the **initial access** techniques saw **phishing** on top with **14%** (up from 10%), ahead of **social engineering** (**13%** down from 15%), and **supply chain compromises** plummeted at **9%** from 19%.

And even in this timeline, targets belonging to the **Information and Communication** sector were the most hit with a share of **16%**, (it was 13%), ahead of **Public** **Administrations** (slightly up at **14%** from 13%). Targets belonging to the **Fintech** sector close the podium with **9%**.

Besides these trends, as always the timeline is rich of interesting events, such as additional [mega breaches](https://www.hackmageddon.com/2026/01/29/mega-breaches-in-2026/) , so my final suggestion does not change: browse the timeline for the details of all the events collected in this fortnight.
