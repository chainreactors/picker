[Wisconsin’s S.B. 130](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/2025/proposals/sb130) / [A.B. 105](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/2025/related/proposals/ab105) is a spectacularly bad idea.

It’s an age-verification bill that effectively bans VPN access to certain websites for Wisconsinites and censors lawful speech. We wrote about it last November in our blog “[Lawmakers Want to Ban VPNs—And They Have No Idea What They're Doing](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/11/lawmakers-want-ban-vpns-and-they-have-no-idea-what-theyre-doing),” but since then, the bill has passed the State Assembly and is [scheduled for a vote](https://x.com/FSCArmy/status/2023875905169629408) in the State Senate tomorrow.

In light of this, EFF sent a letter to the entire Wisconsin Legislature urging lawmakers to reject this dangerous bill.

You can [read the full letter here](https://www.eff.org/document/eff-letter-re-wi-sb-130-ab-105).

The short version? This bill both requires invasive [age verification](https://www.eff.org/issues/age-verification) for websites that host content lawmakers might deem “sexual” and requires that those sites block any user that connects via a Virtual Private Network (VPN).
