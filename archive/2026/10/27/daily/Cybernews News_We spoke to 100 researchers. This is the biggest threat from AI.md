Key takeaways

1. 01

   Cybernews surveyed 103 AI researchers about advanced AI, superintelligence, and major risks to humanity.
2. 02

   About 64% say self-improving AI could plausibly pose a major risk within five years.
3. 03

   Most researchers see human misuse and concentrated power as bigger threats than AI acting on its own.
4. 04

   Nearly 40% want frontier AI development slowed, while 15.7% support a temporary pause.
