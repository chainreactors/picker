## Key takeaways

Sekoia explains how its multi-tenant AI SOC platform helps MSSPs win, manage and protect clients while evolving into MDR providers.

* The managed security service market is growing fast, pushing MSSPs to find tools that manage multiple clients and add value.
* The Sekoia SOC platform is built for MSSPs, supporting their shift from SIEM management toward MDR and XDR services.
* Over 190 integrations and an endpoint agent let MSSPs connect the platform to almost any client infrastructure.
* A multi-tenant mode isolates each client's data while letting MSSPs manage rules and playbooks centrally in one click.
* Pre-built playbooks automate routine tasks and asset-based pricing keeps costs predictable, improving MSSP margins.

The managed security service market is blooming. [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1230718/managed-security-services-market-it/) states it's projected to reach 65.53 billion U.S. dollars in 2028. Although this forecast looks promising, MSSPs still compete and seek the right tools to manage multiple clients and enhance their offerings. With the multi-tenant Sekoia's AI SOC platform, MSSPs can centralize management, integrate with any infrastructure, and leverage pre-built playbooks designed to automate repetitive tasks.

In this article, we explain how using Sekoia's SOC platform, designed from the ground up for MSSPs, benefits providers and helps them win and manage SMBs.

## **Sekoia SOC platform for MSSPs: Outstanding features**

Historically, MSSPs have been about outsourcing IT services, often starting with SIEM management. In contrast, MDRs focus on running value-added services, particularly on top of endpoint detection. Seeking to enhance their value proposition, MSSPs are gradually transforming into MDRs.

The Sekoia SOC platform is designed to support this transformation, offering an intuitive eXtended Detection & Response (XDR) approach. The platform enables MSSPs to provide additional value, delivering a multi-source perspective and scalability. Moreover, the Sekoia team prioritizes functionality and features needed by MSSPs managing multiple clients. Let's consider some of these features.

### **Integrations and compatibility**

The Sekoia SOC platform stands out for its remarkable compatibility, featuring **over 190 integrations** and an effective [Endpoint collection agent](https://docs.sekoia.com/xdr/features/collect/integrations/endpoint/sekoiaio/). The large number of connectors ensures smooth integration with any infrastructure, providing MSSPs with unmatched adaptability.

### **Multi-tenant mode**

The Sekoia SOC platform relies on sub-communities and supports a multi-tenant mode for managing different clients. Sub-community data is isolated and includes events, alerts, assets, and users. However, enabling any rule on all sub-communities is possible in a single click.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69b190e7f47e4c44b0ee07d8/6a92a5be9f9b7f8b2cd16704_6a92a2ba5538c880c2530687_6a2a7d798298c471d7c79888.webp)

The advantage of the multi-tenant mode lies in the ability to manage all Sekoia objects, except alerts and events, from the main platform. This approach greatly facilitates the administration of different subsidiary or client communities. Moreover, centralized management and administration allow MSSPs to apply solutions developed for addressing specific client issues to all communities.
