## What's Changed

### ⚠️ Compatibility Notes

* Require Python 3.11 or newer in every package; Python 3.10 installs resolve to 2.54.0 and earlier by [@Kludex](https://github.com/Kludex) in [#9526](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/9526)
* Record the tool requests of `ExaSearch`, `YouSearch`, `LocalStack` and other harness capabilities under durable execution by [@DouweM](https://github.com/DouweM) in [#9705](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/9705)
* Bound retained realtime audio like retained images with `retain_audio_max_seconds` by [@DouweM](https://github.com/DouweM) in [#9702](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/9702)
* Prefix `LogfireMCP` tool names with `logfire_` by [@mpfaffenberger](https://github.com/mpfaffenberger) in [#9868](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/9868)
* Serialize `FileUrl.media_type` as `null` for extensionless URLs instead of raising by [@pydanty](https://github.com/apps/pydanty) in [#8405](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/8405)
* Take realtime history, usage, and reply waits from the session core on OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, and xAI by [@DouweM](https://github.com/DouweM) in [#9612](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/9612)
