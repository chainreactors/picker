# Careers360 Data Breach

## What Happened

In November 2026, [data allegedly taken from the Indian education and careers platform Careers360 appeared on a public hacking forum](https://x.com/DailyDarkWeb/status/2121550256668131811). The 22M records included 3.6M unique email addresses alongside names, phone numbers, genders and caste categories, and most also contained students' entrance exam results, including ranks and marks.
