Join [EFF Executive Director Cindy Cohn](#cindy) in conversation with [404 Media Cofounder Jason Koebler](#jason) to discuss *Privacy's Defender: My Thirty-Year Fight Against Digital Surveillance,*Cindy’s personal story of standing up to the Justice Department, taking on the NSA, and tangling with the FBI to protect our right to digital privacy. The highly anticipated book asks the fundamental question: Can we still have private conversations if we live our lives online? Join the livestream for a live discussion followed by by Q&A.
