Asks that hold the agent until you allow or deny them now come up in the middle of the window, one at a time, once you've stopped typing, and only for the session in front. That covers the agent's permissions, the sandbox gateway's calls (a push, leaving the sandbox) and connections the sandbox holds. A session behind calls for you from its tab.

## Behaviour

* **The prompt:** a dialog over a dimmed window at the dialog layer, with the existing answers: Allow once `y`, Always `a`, Deny `n`, Deny with note `⇧n`, through `ask.answer`. Esc, its ✕ or a click outside puts it off without denying it: it waits in the Inbox and the console and comes back next time its session comes to the front.
* **Not mid-typing:** it shows once no key has gone down for `inbox.prompt_pause` (default 3s). It shows at once if no key has gone down since the session came to the front, or since a prompt was last up. So switching to a session shows its prompt straight away, and the keys that answered one don't hold up the next.
* **One at a time:** the first waiting prompt for the session in front, in order. One answered elsewhere, or withdrawn by the agent, drops out and the next follows. A confirm dialog that's up goes first.
