# Rad Gifts That Fund the Fight: EFF's Holiday Merch Guide for 2026

![](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/stim-sticker-banner-flat.jpg?itok=Cp3IuPuT)

EFF supporters are why our lawyers can [sue the government](https://www.eff.org/cases) when it spies on you and chills your free speech. EFF members make it possible for our technologists to [build the tools](https://www.eff.org/pages/tools) to protect privacy on the streets and online. And donations to EFF are what fuels our activists as they [fight to pass good laws](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/10/effs-state-legislative-agenda-end-alpr-mass-surveillance) and to [end bad company practices](https://encryptitalready.org/).

**But you can also support EFF by buying gear from the [EFF Online Shop](https://shop.eff.org/)! And starting today, take 20% off your entire order with coupon code: "[BL4CKFRID4Y](https://shopeff.org/discount/BL4CKFRID4Y)" !  From now until midnight on Monday the 30th, stock up and stand out with rad, stylish EFF merch:**

[Shop Now](https://shopeff.org/discount/BL4CKFRID4Y)

[Support Digital Rights with Every Purchase](https://shopeff.org/discount/BL4CKFRID4Y)
