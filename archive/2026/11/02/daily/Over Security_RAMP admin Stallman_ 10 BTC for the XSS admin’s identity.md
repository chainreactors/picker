The administrator of RAMP, the Russian-language ransomware forum the FBI seized on 28 January 2026, ran its escrow service personally at a 5% cut, bought US network access by the day, and on 1 February 2024 offered LockBit the identity of the XSS forum’s administrator for 10 bitcoin, in writing. Nine months after the seizure he has not been charged or publicly named. This is what the forum’s own records say about him.

*About this investigation. The research, analysis and confidence assessments below are mine. I reviewed a copy of the RAMP forum database that circulated in the underground after the seizure, the same material CloudSEK and other researchers have described and that forum members cross-checked against the seized site. Every quotation attributed to the operator is a dated message he typed, reproduced as written, with my translation beneath. Where I draw an inference rather than quote a record, I say so and give my confidence. I worked the same way I did on the [5socks.net history](https://ransomnews.com/5socks-proxy-service-history-2026/): records first, public reporting second, inference last and labelled. Nothing here identifies the operator by legal name, and I do not claim to know it. Ransomnews edited this piece for style and checked the quotations against the source material and the public reporting cited.*

### In brief

* RAMP’s `admin` and `Stallman` accounts were one operator, confirmed by a six-minute account switch on one server on 17 April 2023 and by his own public post of 1 July 2022.
* He described his trade in his third week as buying US government network access, and he ran the forum’s escrow personally at a 5% cut.
* On 1 February 2024 he offered LockBit’s operator the XSS administrator’s deanonymisation for **10 BTC, roughly $430,000 at the time**, in a private message that survives verbatim.
* His escrow arithmetic matches the blockchain to the satoshi. His master wallet took in **55.84 BTC across 596 transactions**, and on 2 January 2025 about 118 BTC moved through mixer-style transactions that included his addresses.
