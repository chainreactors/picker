76% Faster Triage: How US SOCs Increase ROI Through Visibility and Automation

October marks Cybersecurity Awareness Month, a timely opportunity to look at how American organizations can strengthen their security operations.

Among the biggest challenges SOC teams face is making sure they not only detect and investigate threats, but doing so quickly enough to make the right security decisions without overwhelming analysts or increasing operational costs.

In this article, [ANY.RUN](https://any.run/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=us-soc-increase-roi&utm_term=201026&utm_content=linktolanding) explores how better threat visibility and automation can help US-based SOCs achieve this, using a real phishing investigation and metrics to demonstrate how faster triage translates into greater ROI from existing SOC investments.

## Key Takeaways

* Faster triage reduces investigation costs. A [healthcare MSSP](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/healthcare-mssp-success-story/) achieved 76% faster phishing triage with ANY.RUN, reducing investigation time from 30–40 to 4–7 minutes.

* Better visibility supports faster decisions. Behavioral analysis and automation help analysts identify genuine threats without lengthy manual checks.

* More cases resolved at Tier 1 improve SOC capacity. In the same customer case, the Tier 1 closure rate increased from 20% to 70%.

* Higher SOC ROI starts with existing resources. Fewer unnecessary escalations, faster investigations, and better resource utilization help teams scale efficiently.

## The SOC Efficiency Challenge: What the Numbers Reveal

SOC teams face a difficult balancing act: alert volumes keep growing, but the time and resources available to investigate them remain limited. Analysts must sort through potentially harmless activity while ensuring that genuine threats don’t go unnoticed.

Recent Microsoft research highlights the scale of the problem:

According to a [2026 Microsoft and Omdia study](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/02/17/unify-now-or-pay-later-new-research-exposes-the-operational-cost-of-a-fragmented-soc/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), 42% of security alerts go uninvestigated, while 46% of respondents believe that more than half of their alerts are false positives.

This means SOC teams spend valuable time filtering out false alarms while a significant share of alerts never receive proper investigation, potentially leaving real threats undetected.

The consequences go beyond analyst fatigue. A total of 91% of surveyed security leaders reported serious security incidents caused by operational gaps.

For US-based organizations, this highlights a critical priority: reduce the time spent on false positives and routine checks to improve efficiency and get greater value from your security investments.

## How Visibility and Automation Helps SOCs Increase ROI

Increasing SOC ROI starts with helping analysts make accurate decisions faster, without adding unnecessary manual work or operational costs.
It takes providing analysts with enough information to make accurate decisions for them to avoid spending unnecessary time on manual checks and switching between security solutions.

According to ANY.RUN data, two capabilities have proved to make most difference: **visibility into actual threat behavior and automation of repetitive investigation steps**.
