## Summary

* At `-O1`, reserve R11 as a pure codegen scratch so `emit_va_arg` cannot clobber mem2reg-promoted pointers.
* Propagate call-survival `forbid_mask` through `IR_COPY` for loop-carried merges.
* `-O0` register allocation is unchanged.
* Add e2e cases: `addr_two_consumers`, `addr_sole_use_fold`, `variadic_fmt_live_loop`.
