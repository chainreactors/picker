## What Happened

On 18 June 2026, the latest phase of [Operation Endgame targeted the SocGholish malware operation](https://www.politie.nl/en/news/2026/june/18/international-law-enforcement-initiate-hunt-on-malware-group-socgholish.html), a prolific malware distribution network used to compromise systems and facilitate further cybercrime. Coordinated by international law enforcement agencies with support from Europol and Eurojust, the operation remediated almost 15,000 compromised websites and disrupted more than 100 servers and domains used to distribute malware. Authorities also provided HIBP with 154k impacted email addresses and more than half a million previously unseen passwords.
