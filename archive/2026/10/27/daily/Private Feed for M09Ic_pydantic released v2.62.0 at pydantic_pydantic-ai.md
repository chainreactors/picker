* Add `ModelRequestRejectedError`, `ModelAuthenticationError` and `ModelNotFoundError` model error categories by [@DouweM](https://github.com/DouweM) in [#11081](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/11081)
* State loaded deferred capabilities' instructions in the system voice, append their changes, and show them to `before_model_request` by [@DouweM](https://github.com/DouweM) in [#11103](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/11103)
* Prefix hosted MCP capabilities' tool names with the service by default, configurable with `tool_prefix` by [@DouweM](https://github.com/DouweM) in [#10376](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/10376)
* Store the `SummarizingCompaction` summary as a `CompactionPart` instead of a system prompt, and send provider-less `CompactionPart`s to every model by [@DouweM](https://github.com/DouweM) in [#10573](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/10573)
