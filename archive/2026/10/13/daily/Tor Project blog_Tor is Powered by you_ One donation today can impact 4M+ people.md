# Tor is Powered by you: One donation today can impact 4M+ people

by [arturom](/author/arturom)
| October 13, 2026

![](/tor-is-powered-by-you/lead.png)

Unlike Big Tech, Tor has never and will never sell your data to make money. [6,490 donors](https://blog.torproject.org/financials-blog-post-2023-2024/), and [thousands of volunteer relay operators and others](https://metrics.torproject.org/), keep Tor powered for [4M+ at any given moment](https://metrics.torproject.org/userstats-relay-country.html)\* at zero cost to users.

You can ensure people worldwide can exercise their human rights to digital privacy and access to information by making a donation today. Every donation, of any size, makes a difference.

[![Donate Button](/tor-is-powered-by-you/button.png)](https://torproject.org/donate/donate-bp1-yec2026)

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, the Tor Project relies on donations to power its tools â the [Tor network](https://blog.torproject.org/tor-network-community-health-update/), [Tor Browser](https://download.torproject.org/), [Tails OS](https://tails.net/install/index.en.html), and the [Tor ecosystem](https://blog.torproject.org/strength-numbers-entire-ecosystem-relies-tor/). These tools are trusted by millions and always free to use, without collecting, selling, trading, or renting any of your data for profit. Read our [2023-2024 financials](https://blog.torproject.org/financials-blog-post-2023-2024/) to be confident your investment is being stewarded with the utmost importance.

In the last year we have reached some major milestones thanks to your support:

* **Tor VPN beta** extends Tor protections beyond the browser. It offers device-level network protection on Android, helping bring Tor's protections to messaging apps, social media, email, and other mobile services of a user's choosing. [Learn more](https://blog.torproject.org/tor-vpn-beta/) about what makes our per-app isolation different from commercial VPNs, the surprising UX lesson when trying to bypass blocks, and the mobile foundation we're building for what's next.
* **Privacy Resilience Grants**: Dispersed $23,201 to support 8 Tor partners in conducting Tor activities on the ground. Among these groups, women activists in Bolivia have adopted Tor tools, shifting the thinking of using other tools for power and resistance. Meanwhile, journalists in Afghanistan have reduced their reliance on VPNs, and have begun using Tails OS to securely share on the ground information globally.
* **Funding internet freedom:** Our [first crowdfunding round](https://blog.torproject.org/funding-internet-freedom-together/) in partnership with Funding the Commons raised more than $200k for projects working across privacy, censorship circumvention, secure communications, and public-interest digital infrastructure. This is Torâs first attempt at building a new funding infrastructure for the internet freedom community.

All of these major milestones have a real life impact on people globally. People who need privacy for their safety, people who rely on Tor tools to be connected to the world, and people who are casual users seeking an alternative to Big Tech.
