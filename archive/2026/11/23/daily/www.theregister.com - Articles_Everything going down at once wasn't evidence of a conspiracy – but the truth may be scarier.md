# Everything going down at once wasn't evidence of a conspiracy – but the truth may be scarier

[KETTLE](https://www.theregister.com/tag/Kettle/)

Rogue AI or nation-state hackers would be preferable to the amount of technical debt the past week of wobbling services laid bare

Brandon Vigliarolo
[Brandon
Vigliarolo](https://www.theregister.com/author/brandon-vigliarolo)
GOVERNMENT AND IT NEWS REPORTER

Published
mon 23 Nov 2026 // 14:04 UTC

[Make us preferred on Google](https://google.com/preferences/source?q=theregister.com)

Have you or someone you love been affected by a tech outage this week? Yeah, us too.

You can listen to the latest episode of The Kettle right here on this page, as well as on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6N7GaSQcYzhPre8ooOk8mo)[,](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0GDsvVSe00Jsax9tMIRwWs) [Apple Music](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/everything-going-down-at-once-wasnt-evidence-of/id1882523636?i=1000803489517)[,](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-jensen-huang-saga-could-be-nvidias-or-even-ais-undoing/id1882523636?i=1000802148369) or [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIPPvqzPcn4&list=PLh_fFjg_9ZRwgcIuiHsHUQ29UUzm48XHU&index=56). Those platforms also let you subscribe to The Kettle, so you are always notified when the latest episode goes live

In a week of big stories the biggest one seems to be the fact that everyone from Microsoft to the NSA was [dealing with outages](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/17/daily-disruptive-outages-that-ground-planes-and-halt-work-welcome-to-the-new-normal/5310634), and here's what might be the worst part: It doesn't seem to be AI, nor hostile countries intent on destroying our infrastructure.

REG AD

Unfortunately for all of us, what the past week of outages reveal is that – as we discussed on The Kettle less than a month ago – all of our essential tech infrastructure is basically held together [with duct tape and twine.](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/26/we-shouldnt-be-surprised-about-the-phpocalypse-as-the-internet-is-built-on-sticks-and-glue/5305186)

Join host [Brandon Vigliarolo](https://www.theregister.com/author/brandon-vigliarolo), cybersecurity editor [Jessica Lyons](https://www.theregister.com/author/jessica-lyons), and open source reporter [Liam Proven](https://www.theregister.com/author/liam-proven) to discuss what comes next, and whether we can fix any of this without tearing all of it out and starting from scratch.
