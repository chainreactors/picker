Powerful institutions are using automated decision-making against us. Landlords use it to decide who gets [a home](https://www.consumerreports.org/electronics/algorithmic-bias/tenant-screening-reports-make-it-hard-to-bounce-back-from-tough-times-a2331058426/). Insurance companies use it to decide who gets [health care](https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/prior-authorization/how-ai-leading-more-prior-authorization-denials). ICE uses it to decide who must submit to location tracking by [electronic monitoring](https://mijente.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Automating-Deportation.pdf). Bosses use it to decide [who gets fired](https://abcnews.go.com/Technology/dangers-ai-hiring-firing-employees-focus-book/story?id=108890705), and to predict who is [organizing a union](https://prospect.org/labor/2025-03-13-amazon-uses-arsenal-of-ai-weapons-against-workers/) or [planning to quit](https://www.cnbc.com/2019/09/10/this-algorithm-can-predict-when-workers-are-about-to-quitheres-how.html). Bosses even use AI to assess the [body language and voice tone](https://www.bbc.com/worklife/article/20240214-ai-recruiting-hiring-software-bias-discrimination) of job candidates. And these systems often [discriminate](https://www.urban.org/urban-wire/three-ways-ai-can-discriminate-hiring-and-three-ways-forward) based on gender, race, and other protected statuses.

Fortunately, [workers](https://laborcenter.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Data-and-Algorithms-at-Work.pdf), [patients](https://cdt.org/insights/civil-rights-standards-for-21st-century-employment-selection-procedures/), [and renters](https://www.aclu.org/documents/digital-discrimination-in-hiring) [are](https://civilrights.org/resource/civil-rights-principles-for-hiring-assessment-technologies/) [resisting](https://innovation.consumerreports.org/what-cr-found-surveying-americans-about-ai-deciding-access-to-housing-employment-healthcare-and-more/).
