In late 2024, we urged [Google and Amazon](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2024/12/amazon-and-google-must-keep-their-promises-project-nimbus) to honor their human rights commitments, to be more transparent with the public, and to take meaningful action to address the risks posed by Project Nimbus, their cloud computing contract that includes Israel’s Ministry of Defense and the Israeli Security Agency.
