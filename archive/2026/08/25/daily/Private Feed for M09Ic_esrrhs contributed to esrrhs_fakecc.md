## Summary

Enable correct by-value passing of nested structs/unions with bitfields, mixed integer/float members, and flexible array members. Fixes three issues that blocked GCC c-torture `pr23324.c`:

* **parser**: zero-length arrays (`[0]`) were forced to length 1, breaking flexible array member layout. Now `[0]` yields length 0.
* **ast**: nested struct/union classification recursively merges member classes (GCC/SysV alignment) instead of always returning MEMORY.
* **ir**: empty unions/structs (size 0) now reserve a 1-byte pinned alloca so codegen's `IR_ADDR` works at `-O1` (previously crashed with "IR\_ADDR on non-pinned alloca").
