## Disrupt face recognition, one Ghostyle at a time

1. ![A face in the lab with the detection box and the 68-point scaffold drawn over it.](/images/home/story/naomi-landmarks.jpg)

   Define a baseline

   The clean face is saved as the identity.
   Every distance after this is measured against this frame.
2. ![The same face and the same frame, with the Ghostyle 1 pattern painted across it.](/images/home/story/naomiwu-success.jpg)

   Paint the face

   Ghostyle 1 goes on, and the system cannot
   even find a face any more. That is the cleanest way to
   *win this game*.

   threshold 0.58
   no face found
3. ![The pattern of Ghostyle 1 alone, laid over the outline of a face on a cream ground.](/images/home/story/ghostyle-1.svg)

   This pattern is a Ghostyle

   A reusable pattern. Apply it as an AR
   overlay in the lab, paint it for real, share it, and try it
   on another face.
4. ![A second face, clean, before the Ghostyle is applied.](/images/home/story/balotelli.jpg)

   A second face tests the Ghostyle

   A darker skin takes the previous success:
   save the baseline, load Ghostyle 1, paint the face, and…
5. ![The second face wearing Ghostyle 1, still recognised by the lab.](/images/home/story/balotelli-ghostyle1.jpg)

   It failed

   This colour combination did not fool the
   system. A Ghostyle that works on one face can fail on another,
   and skin tone is one of the reasons. But a Ghostyle is a
   starting point: it can be adapted.

   distance **0.33**
   threshold 0.58
   still recognised
6. ![The second face with a redrawn pattern, Ghostyle 2, in lighter pigments.](/images/home/story/balotelli-ghostyle2.jpg)

   Smaller shapes, lighter pigments

   Ghostyle 2 keeps the idea, drops the lines
   on the forehead and lightens the colours. The detector no
   longer finds a face at all.

   threshold 0.58
   no face found
7. ![A third face, clean, before the Ghostyle is applied.](/images/home/story/greta.jpg)

   Does Ghostyle 1 work on every light skin?

   A third face. In Ghostmaxxing you can test
   with the digital overlay or by painting for real. And,
   surprisingly…
8. ![The third face wearing Ghostyle 1, still recognised by the lab.](/images/home/story/greta-ghostyle1.jpg)

   Not reusable yet

   Ghostyle 1 on this face measures 0.52,
   under the 0.58 threshold: the face is still matched. For
   scale, simply turning your head moves the reading by about
   0.15.

   distance **0.52**
   threshold 0.58
   still recognised
9. ![The third face wearing Ghostyle 3, the same shapes in a leopard pattern, no longer matched.](/images/home/story/greta-ghostyle3.jpg)

   Same shape, new colours

   Ghostyle 3 reads 0.61, above the
   threshold: the biometric points no longer match what is saved.
   See [how these were made](/workshops.html), or
   [open the lab](/lab.html) and draw the next one.

   distance **0.61**
   threshold 0.58
   escaped

Ghostmaxxing is your personal laboratory for **testing any kind of camouflage**.
Use your phone or webcam, change how your face looks (makeup, clothing, prosthetics…),
and measure how face recognition reacts. **Everything runs on your device. Nothing leaves it**,
unless you choose to share a success in the [public gallery](/gallery.html).

Built
for hands-on [workshops](workshops.html) and
self-experiments.

How
it works

1. Save the baseline (clean face)

![Stylised face framed as a saved local baseline](/images/home/how-save-baseline.svg)

Save your face while it's clean. A private
baseline, kept on your device, to test camouflage against.

2. Paint until camouflaged

![Stylised face with asymmetric paint around the eyes, cheek and mouth](/images/home/how-paint.svg)

Apply a Ghostyle or your own intervention:
makeup, an overlay, a physical change to the face.

3. (Optional) Upload

![Stylised face card with an upload arrow and an explicit optional consent control](/images/home/how-upload.svg)

Share your result to the gallery. Together
we will find the most subtle, least invasive, and still effective
camouflage.
