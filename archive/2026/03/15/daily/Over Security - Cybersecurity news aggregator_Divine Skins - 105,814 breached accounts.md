# Divine Skins Data Breach

## What Happened

In March 2026, [the League of Legends custom skins service Divine Skins suffered a data breach](https://discord.com/channels/1272878928368762996/1272879634488496138/1482141964559319210). The incident was disclosed via the service's Discord server, where Divine Skins stated that an unauthorised third party accessed part of its systems, deleted all skins from the database and exposed email addresses and usernames. The data also contained a history of purchases made by users.
