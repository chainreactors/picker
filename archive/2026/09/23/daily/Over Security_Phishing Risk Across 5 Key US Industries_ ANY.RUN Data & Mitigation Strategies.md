Phishing Risk Across 5 Key US Industries: ANY.RUN Data & Mitigation Strategies

According to fresh [ANY.RUN](https://any.run/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=phishing-risk-industries%C2%A0&utm_term=230926&utm_content=linktolanding) data, phishing exposure remains above 70% in several critical industries. This doesn’t happen because organizations aren’t protected enough. Companies have been implementing email filtering, MFA, and phishing-awareness training for years.

However, threats continue to evolve, and security methods that were highly effective yesterday can develop visibility gaps as attackers adapt.

In this article, ANY.RUN explores data-driven insights to get to the bottom of phishing risk across key industries in the United States and examines how SOC teams can mitigate it.

See [our previous article](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/phishing-us-finance/) on phishing risk among US-based financial organizations.

## Key Executive Takeaways

* **Phishing remains a cross-industry risk.** Exposure is consistently high across critical US sectors, making it an organization-wide concern rather than an isolated email-security problem.
* **Email is only the beginning of the attack chain.** Archives, PDFs, links, redirects, and post-click activity create visibility gaps that email controls alone cannot cover.
* **Phishing is increasingly targeting identity and access.** AiTM, session theft, token abuse, and user-driven execution make attacks harder to detect and contain with traditional controls alone.
* **Speed and visibility are critical to reducing exposure.** Behavioral analysis helps SOC teams uncover evasive activity quickly and move from suspicious content to informed response faster.

## Phishing Risk Remains High Across Critical Industries

| Industry | Key ANY.RUN Data | Impacted Business Processes & Operational Risks | Priority SOC Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Finance | 73.4% phishing exposure; 58.7% of analyzed files are emails | Credential theft, fraud, sensitive financial data exposure | Strengthen email and post-click threat analysis |
| Banking | ClickFix 71%, EtherHiding 63.8%, Sneaky2FA 62.3% prevalence | Session hijacking, credential compromise, unauthorized access | Detect AiTM, user-driven execution, and evasive activity |
| Manufacturing | 72.2% phishing exposure | Business disruption, sensitive data and system compromise | Detect and contain phishing chains before they progress |
| Government & Administration | 67.9% emails, 15.6% archives, 6.7% PDFs | Credential theft and compromise through document-based lures | Analyze attachments, links, and post-click behavior |
| Technology | Tycoon, Sneaky2FA, EvilProxy, ClickFix, EvilTokens among top threats | Credential, session, and access-token compromise | Prioritize identity-focused and post-click threat detection |

Across finance, banking, technology, manufacturing, and government [**phishing**](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/csuite-attack-analysis/) exposure reaches **69.9%**, showing that phishing remains a widespread risk across critical sectors. The highest exposure levels appear in **[finance](https://any.run/by-industry/finance/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=phishing-risk-industries%C2%A0&utm_term=230926&utm_content=linktofinance)** at **73.4%** and **[manufacturing](https://any.run/by-industry/manufacturing/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=phishing-risk-industries%C2%A0&utm_term=230926&utm_content=linktomanufacturing)** at **72.2%**.
