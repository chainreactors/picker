In the rush to block young people from certain parts of the internet, lawmakers are creating a privacy and security nightmare for everyone. This scenario is already playing out globally. [Help us stop it and keep the web open and accessible for all.](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/spring--DL5)

[JOIN EFF](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/spring--DL5)

Protect the web for everyone

Even with the best intentions, every online age verification scheme has the same result: *users are forced to reveal sensitive personal information to third parties simply to access the web.* Once that valuable data is centralized, it becomes an immediate target for leaks, hacks, and misuse. This isn’t hypothetical: [it has](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2024/06/hack-age-verification-company-shows-privacy-danger-social-media-laws) [already](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2024/06/hack-age-verification-company-shows-privacy-danger-social-media-laws) [happened](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2024/06/hack-age-verification-company-shows-privacy-danger-social-media-laws) [several](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2026/02/age-verification-vendor-persona-left-frontend-exposed) [times](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/13/how-we-found-teaonher-spilling-users-drivers-licenses-in-less-than-10-minutes).
