In the scorching heat of every Las Vegas summer, EFF joins thousands of hackers, makers, policy analysts, and activists for the world's largest computer security gathering. If you're there during this summer security week, be sure to say hello to us at [BSides Las Vegas](https://www.eff.org/event/eff-bsides-las-vegas-1), [Black Hat Briefings](https://www.eff.org/event/eff-black-hat-usa-2), and [DEF CON 34](https://www.eff.org/event/eff-def-con-34). While tech companies align with governments to target the people, our community is harnessing technology to fight back. Will you lend your support this year?

[JOIN EFF](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/VirtualVegas--DL)

EFF’s relentless work in the legal system makes a meaningful difference for privacy and free expression everywhere. But we also know that your rights won't wait while the wheels of justice turn.

Sometimes hacking the system means creating tools and resources to protect your rights today. That includes EFF’s [Privacy Badger](https://www.eff.org/pages/privacy-badger), [Certbot](https://www.eff.org/pages/certbot), [Surveillance Self-Defense guide](https://ssd.eff.org), and the countless security trainings that our team conducts for vulnerable populations—[all thanks to EFF member support](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/VirtualVegas--DL).

Technology is inseparable from our workplaces, schools, healthcare, the justice system, and our democratic process. If you think tech should benefit everyone and not just accumulate wealth and control for the powerful, then congratulations: [We'd like to welcome you to the team.](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/VirtualVegas--DL)
