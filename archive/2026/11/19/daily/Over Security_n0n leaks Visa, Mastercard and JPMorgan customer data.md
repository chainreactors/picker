The extortion group [n0n](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/group/n0n/) published marketing records on customers of Visa, Mastercard and JPMorgan Chase on 19 November 2026, after a 48-hour deadline ran out. The JPMorgan file holds about 16,000 customer email addresses from the bank’s invitation-only Reserve card, according to Cybernews. All three companies say the data breach happened at outside providers and that no card numbers or payment systems were affected.

:   About 16,000 JPMorgan Chase customer emails, more than 6,000 Visa cardholders and about 2,000 Mastercard entries (Cybernews count) · Marketing records: names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal codes, campaign data

Ransom status
:   Data published after a 48-hour deadline expired

Verification
:   All three companies confirmed incidents at third-party providers

### In brief

* n0n published about 16,000 JPMorgan Chase customer emails, records on more than 6,000 premium Visa cardholders in Asia Pacific, and about 2,000 Mastercard entries that Cybernews researchers say may be test data.
* None of the published reviews of the files reports card numbers, passwords or account balances.
* All three companies say outside providers were breached, and none has named one. A Cybernews researcher says the three sets look like they came from the same third party.
* Stealer logs already hold 126,660 saved user logins for chase.com, visa.com, mastercard.com and jpmorganchase.com, by our count on 19 November. Of those, 106,128 are for chase.com.
* The Ransomtracker has logged 393 n0n entries since 18 September, and 25 new numbered placeholders appeared the evening before the leak.
