# 🎩 Tech Monopolies Make Outages Worse | EFFector 38.21

![](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/effector-green-web.png?itok=fMSatfxr)

Service outages, like the ones we've seen take down everything from airlines to email, seem to be coming faster than ever. Their specific causes might be different, but these wide-scale service disruptions share one thing in common: the over-reliance on one company or system to make our world work.

In [our latest EFFector newsletter](https://www.eff.org/effector/38/21), we're talking about how digital monoculture has made tech companies [too big to fail](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/11/outage-chaos-when-tech-too-big-fail?utm_source=effector).

[JOIN OUR NEWSLETTER](https://www.eff.org/effector/)

For over 35 years, [EFFector](https://eff.org/effector) has been your guide to understanding the intersection of technology, civil liberties, and the law. This issue covers [ALPR at the ballot box](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/11/voters-reject-alprs-and-surveillance-tech-ballot-box?utm_source=effector), [surveillance wearables](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/11/tech-companies-are-trying-shift-entire-concept-privacy-again?utm_source=effector), and [how digital monoculture makes service outages worse](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/11/outage-chaos-when-tech-too-big-fail?utm_source=effector).

Prefer to listen in? EFFector is now available on all major podcast platforms. This time we're talking to EFF's Rory Mir about tech monopolies, service outages, and what we can do to build a more resilient internet. You can find the episode and subscribe [on your podcast platform of choice](https://effector.simplecast.com/):
