## What's New in v2.7.8

> **v2.7.8 kills the malformed-frame crash ([#366](https://github.com/x90skysn3k/brutespray/issues/366))**: hosts that answer RDP handshakes with garbage bytes now produce one connection error and get skipped — they no longer take the whole scan down with them.

### Fixed: `panic: runtime error: makeslice: len out of range` on Garbage RDP Hosts (`#366`)

* **Root cause** — a live "RDP" host answered the X.224 Connection Request with two garbage bytes (`00 01`). The bundled RDP library (grdp) routed them to the fastpath parser, computed the payload length as `shortLength - 2 = -1`, and called `make([]byte, -1)`. Go aborts the entire process for a negative allocation, and the panic fired inside the library's own reader goroutine — outside any recover boundary BruteSpray could install. One malformed reply on port 3389 killed an hours-long scan
* **Fix (grdp v1.0.6, [x90skysn3k/grdp#4](https://github.com/x90skysn3k/grdp/pull/4))** — every length field read from the wire is validated before subtraction: the async reader (`StartReadBytes`) and the sync `ReadBytes` helper both reject negative lengths with `io.ErrUnexpectedEOF`, and the TPKT handlers emit a typed error at each malformed minimum. This also closes the licensing (`WMsgSize-4`), capability (`capLen-4`), and server-certificate (`Keylen-8`, `SignatureBlobLen-8`) sites on background emitter goroutines
