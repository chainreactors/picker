2026年7月24日，中方在四川省成都市主办APEC人工智能高级别论坛，论坛主题为“促进人工智能普惠包容发展”。APEC成员经济体重申，我们致力于在人工智能领域深化交流、凝聚共识、加强合作、促进创新，并致力于继续实施《2040年APEC布特拉加亚愿景》、《APEC互联网和数字经济路线图》和《APEC人工智能倡议（2026—2030）》。

考虑到APEC成员经济体的多样化需求，我们鼓励各经济体开展经验分享，推动人工智能在亚太地区负责任的发展和应用，并期待在以下人工智能领域促进合作：

促进人工智能安全发展和部署。我们鼓励推动安全优先的人工智能发展，促进旨在应对风险、造福人民的创新。鼓励各成员经济体就促进发展可靠、可及、可扩展、可信、安全且有韧性的人工智能基础设施，以及提升数字基础设施可及性交流最佳实践。我们将促进数据跨境流动，这对人工智能发展至关重要，同时增强消费者和企业信任。

加快人工智能的负责任普及应用。我们鼓励以多种形式推进人工智能赋能经济发展，为人工智能融入经济社会各领域营造有利环境。我们推动面向社会各群体提供可及的教育和培训资源，提高人工智能素养和技能。我们认识到，人工智能在促进贸易、推动海关程序现代化和提升企业竞争力方面的作用日益增强，并鼓励进一步交流这些领域的最佳实践，支持经济增长。

营造有利于人工智能创新的环境。我们赞赏采取以市场为导向、以创新为驱动的政策，促进人工智能发展和广泛应用。我们鼓励成员经济体考虑采取兼顾风险应对和负责任应用的人工智能政策。在尊重安全、数据保护和知识产权的同时，我们鼓励各成员经济体支持在开发和部署过程中采用强大安全保障措施的开源模型和项目。

推动广泛参与、普惠共享的APEC合作。我们欢迎加强亚太地区成员经济体之间的能力建设和知识分享，并通过开放对话和协同行动，包括同公共部门和私营部门开展合作，促进创新和经济增长。我们将继续支持所有APEC成员经济体参与人工智能驱动型经济，并使人工智能最大限度惠及全体民众。

我们认识到APEC数字经济指导组、电信工作组及APEC其他子论坛在加强亚太地区人工智能合作方面发挥的重要作用，致力于在APEC数字经济指导组全体会议期间举办人工智能政策对话，推动人工智能造福人民，探讨人工智能创新、应用、可及性及其他新兴议题，同时对人工智能发展过程中出现的新进展保持开放态度。我们感谢中方主办本次论坛，以及四川省和成都市为本次会议提供的周到安排。我们期待未来继续在APEC数字经济指导组框架下开展人工智能对话。

APEC High-Level Forum on AI

Statement on Promoting AI Development in the Asia-Pacific Region

The APEC High-Level Forum on AI, hosted by China under the theme of “Promoting AI Development for Good and for All,” was held in Chengdu, Sichuan, China on July 24, 2026. APEC member economies reaffirmed our commitment to deepening exchanges, building consensus, forging cooperation and promoting innovation on AI, while further implementing the APEC Putrajaya Vision 2040, the APEC Internet and Digital Economy Roadmap (AIDER), and the APEC AI Initiative (2026-2030).

While bearing in mind the diverse needs of APEC member economies, we encourage economies to engage in experience sharing to foster responsible development and adoption of AI across the Asia-Pacific and look forward to promoting cooperation in the following areas of AI:

Promote the secure development and deployment of AI. We encourage AI development that prioritizes security and promotes innovation that addresses risks for the benefit of our people. Member economies are encouraged to exchange best practices on fostering the development of reliable, accessible, scalable, trusted, secure and resilient AI infrastructure and enhancing the accessibility of digital infrastructure. We will work to facilitate cross-border data flows, which are critical to AI development, while strengthening consumer and business trust.

Accelerate the responsible adoption of AI applications. We encourage the implementation of various forms of AI-enabled economic growth to build an enabling environment for the integration of AI across economic sectors and throughout our societies. We promote the provision of accessible educational and training resources for all segments of our societies to improve AI literacy and skills. We recognize the growing role of AI in facilitating trade, modernizing customs procedures, and enhancing business competitiveness, and encourage the further exchange of best practices in these areas to support economic growth.

Foster an enabling environment for AI innovation. We commend policies that promote market-oriented and innovation-driven approaches for the development and broad adoption of AI. We encourage member economies to consider policies that address risks and promote responsible AI adoption. While respecting security, data protection and intellectual property rights, we encourage member economies to support open-source models and projects that employ strong security assurance through development and deployment.

Foster broad-based cooperation to maximize participation and benefits across APEC. We welcome capacity building, and knowledge sharing across member economies in the Asia-Pacific region and fostering innovation and economic growth through open dialogue and collaborative efforts, including with the public and private sectors. We will continue support for all APEC member economies to participate in the AI-driven economy and maximize the benefits of AI for our people.

Recognizing the important role of the APEC Digital Economy Steering Group (DESG), the Telecommunications and Information Working Group, and other APEC sub-fora in strengthening AI cooperation in the Asia-Pacific region, we are committed to organizing policy dialogues on AI during the DESG plenary to promote AI for the benefit of our people and explore AI innovation, adoption, access, and other emerging issues while remaining open to new developments as AI evolves. We express our gratitude to China for hosting this Forum and our appreciation to the City of Chengdu and Sichuan Province for the exceptional hospitality extended at this meeting. We look forward to continued DESG dialogues on AI in the future.

（来源：中国网信网）
