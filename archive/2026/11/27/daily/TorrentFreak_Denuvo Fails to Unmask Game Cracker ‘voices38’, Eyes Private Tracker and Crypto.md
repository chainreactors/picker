# Denuvo Fails to Unmask Game Cracker ‘voices38’, Eyes Private Tracker and Crypto

today by
[Ernesto Van der Sar](https://torrentfreak.com/author/ernesto/)

[Home](https://torrentfreak.com "Go to TorrentFreak.") > [Lawsuits](https://torrentfreak.com/category/lawsuits/ "Go to the Lawsuits category archives.") >

Denuvo's legal effort to reveal the identity of anonymous game cracker 'voices38' by subpoenaing Discord, Reddit and Valve has failed. According to Denuvo, one of the Steam accounts it links to the cracker was deleted on the same day the court approved the subpoenas. Denuvo now asks the court for 90 more days, as it shifts its focus to email providers, ISPs, a private tracker, and crypto addresses.

![voices38](https://torrentfreak.com/images/voicesnov-600x420.png)Last month, [Denuvo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denuvo) [obtained permission](https://torrentfreak.com/court-approves-voices38-subpoenas-after-denuvo-drops-payment-and-login-data-requests/) to subpoena [Discord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discord), [Valve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valve_Corporation) and [Reddit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reddit), hoping to unmask the anonymous game cracker ‘voices38’.

The anti-piracy company [filed a DMCA anti-circumvention lawsuit](https://torrentfreak.com/denuvo-sues-game-cracker-voices38-for-bypassing-its-anti-tamper-drm/) against the cracker in September, accusing ‘voices38’ of bypassing its anti-tamper technology on more than two dozen games. Before the lawsuit can move forward, however, Denuvo has to find out who is behind the popular handle.

To do so, Denuvo focused on the cracker’s public Reddit profile, several Steam accounts and a Discord account, and the court allowed it to subpoena all three platforms.

The platforms had until November 3 to hand over names, email addresses, IP addresses and other contact details. This week, Denuvo informed the court that these efforts didn’t lead to a name or an address. The company therefore asks for more time to serve the lawsuit.

## Discord, Reddit and Valve Come Up Empty

In a filing submitted on Wednesday, Denuvo summarizes the responses. Discord replied first, on October 23, with a one-page record that lists the username as “deleted\_user\_6ea778fffa59#0”.

The email address, phone number, and IP address fields all say “Not found”. According to Discord, it “generally does not possess legal names or other personal information” as users don’t have to provide these when they sign up.

Reddit didn’t hand over anything. On October 30, the company served eleven objections, citing the First Amendment, the Stored Communications Act, overbreadth and burden, among other things. Reddit also informed Denuvo that it “will not produce documents responsive to this request as drafted.”

Valve did share information. On October 28, the company produced records for all seven Steam accounts listed in the subpoena. However, none of the public profiles list a name or location, and the accounts use seven different email addresses, five hosted by U.S. companies and two by Russian email services.

The billing names on these accounts differ too, while the billing and login data point to countries including Germany, Brazil, India, Kazakhstan, and the United States.

## Steam Account Marked ‘[deleted]’

One of the Steam accounts stands out according to Denuvo. This was created on October 13, 2025, the same day the Discord account was registered, and most of its logins through September came from IP addresses that Valve associates with Germany.

Valve handed over two reports for this account. The second, dated October 28, shows that the account’s email address was changed to “Terminated” and its player name to “[deleted]” on October 16, at 17:39 UTC.

*‘Terminated’*

![Terminated](https://torrentfreak.com/images/voices38-terminated.png)

That’s the same day the court authorized the subpoenas. The filing doesn’t mention who made the change, but Denuvo describes it as a deletion and says it suggests that ‘voices38’ is aware of the lawsuit. The company adds that denying its request would reward the cracker for avoiding identification.

## Tracker, Crypto and Email Providers

Denuvo isn’t giving up. The requested extension would give it 90 more days to identify and serve the cracker. In that time, it will look into follow-up subpoenas for the email addresses in Valve’s data and the Internet providers behind the IP addresses. The current order only covers Discord, Valve and Reddit.

The anti-piracy company also hopes to resolve Reddit’s objections, either through an agreement or, if that fails, through the court.

Denuvo’s search for additional information continues as well. Its attorney writes that the company is evaluating other sources of identifying information, “including a private tracker and forum on which Defendant distributes releases, and analysis of the cryptocurrency addresses Defendant publishes in its NFO files.”

*Other sources*

![Other sources](https://torrentfreak.com/images/voices38-other-sources.png)

The filing doesn’t name the tracker or forum, and Denuvo notes that each of these options would require further permission from the court or legal process abroad. Crypto came up before as well. In October, Denuvo [said it had found](https://torrentfreak.com/denuvo-asks-court-to-unmask-game-cracker-voices38-reveals-crypto-transactions/) cryptocurrency transactions by the cracker on the exchange FixedFloat, without explaining how it made that link.

Shortly after the lawsuit was filed, ‘voices38’ [responded to our coverage](https://www.reddit.com/r/CrackWatch/comments/1whseof/comment/pa51i9k/) on Reddit, writing that “All is fine. Everything will continue as normal.”

The cracker has indeed continued to release new cracks since, starting with Atomfall in October. The most recent one, Phantom Blade Zero, was posted on Reddit a few days ago.

That post came from a new account, ‘v0ices38’, spelled with a zero. The original ‘voices38’ Reddit account, which Denuvo cited in its complaint and targeted with its subpoena, was deleted after the lawsuit was filed.
