After orchestration skill detection marks setup complete, reopening the floating terminal now keeps **Enable orchestration** hidden when CLI status is `unsupported` with reason `launch_mode_unavailable`. That reason occurs in paired browser clients and unpackaged desktop development launches where CLI registration is unavailable in that launch mode.

Packaged `launcher_missing`, `platform_not_supported` and `wsl_unreachable` failures retain the setup prompt, along with missing PATH, absent completion markers, unknown reasons and status-request errors. Existing dismissal and setup-event behavior are preserved. This changes presentation only; it does not infer that the remote host's CLI is ready or change installation, discovery or RPC behavior.
