# Institutional Knowledge

Written by

[j4vv4d](https://javvadmalik.com/author/j4vv4d/)

in

[blog](https://javvadmalik.com/category/blog/)

I was talking to someone the other day and we were talking about layoffs that occur and how institutional knowledge walks out the door. He tilted his head slightly and asked what institutional knowledge actually was, because everything could or should be documented and you can Google or ask AI for answers on pretty much anything. So his question was really, is it as big a deal as people make it out to be.

For me, it’s a bit like coming downstairs at 6 AM and finding the fridge door open about an inch. You go through the list of suspects in your mind… who was last in there? My son, at 9pm, for a yoghurt he didn’t finish. My daughter, who denies everything on principle. My wife, who would never, and even if she did, I would never accuse.

You look at the situation and the first question is whether the milk is fine. You check the butter, which is slightly soft… but then again, it’s always slightly soft.

If you push the door closed, after about two seconds, it slightly drifts open again. Because the seals failed a couple of years ago. Or maybe it’s the hinges. I don’t know. All I know is that the door only stays shut if you lift it slightly and push it at a very specific angle. Everyone in the house has learnt that trick without ever discussing it. It’s a family skill, like knowing which stairs to avoid when sneaking up late at night and not wanting the creaks to give you away.

My mother-in-law is visiting… clearly she’s never mastered it. And that’s what lack of institutional knowledge can lead to. Plus who doesn’t like to blame their mother-in-law for something?
