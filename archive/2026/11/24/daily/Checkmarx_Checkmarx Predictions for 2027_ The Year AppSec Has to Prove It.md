## Never Miss an Update.

AppSec research and analysis from Checkmarx, straight to your inbox.

A year ago, Checkmarx leaders and experts [put their predictions for 2026 on the record](https://checkmarx.com/blog/checkmarx-influencer-2026-predictions/). Turns out, they were accurate – almost prophetic – about the direction, impact and risks of AI-generated code, including:

* **CEO Sandeep Johri** predicting the industry’s obsession with AI-driven productivity would peak the moment organizations realized how much their security posture had weakened.

* **Chief Product Officer Jonathan Rende** saying security could no longer stop at static versus dynamic testing. It would have to take on AI governance, model integrity and agent-level risk.

* **Product Management leader Frank Emery** warning that as people with no production experience started building apps with AI, insecure ideas would reach markets fast, and get attacked just as fast.

All of it happened in 2026. Now, with AI still accelerating the volume, velocity and risk of software, the shifts ahead look bigger than the ones behind us.

So we asked our leaders and experts one question: what will be the biggest change in how teams secure the software they build in 2027?
