Added `is_tls` field to declarations so `extern __thread` retains declaration-only status (`storage_class == 2`) and `static __thread` retains internal linkage (`storage_class == 1`). Updated `is_type_start` and `is_function_declaration_lookahead` to recognize TLS keywords at declaration start.
