# Publishing ‘Publish Your Threat Model’

![The paper headline](/images/blog/img/2025/publish-your-threat-models-published-1648w.png)

Two new versions of our “Publish your threat model” work are now
available. The first is the [presentation](/files/podcasts/OSTIF-nov-2025.pptx) I gave today to [OSTIF
Community meetup](https://ostif.org/community/). The second is the [pre-print version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08295) of our
HealthSec 2025 paper. For that, we also have an [epub](files/conference/healthsec/Publish-your-TM.epub) version for Kindle reading.
