## I Am in the Epstein Files

[Once](https://www.jmail.world/thread/EFTA02451032?view=inbox). Someone named “Vincenzo lozzo” wrote to Epstein in email, in 2016: “I wouldn’t pay too much attention to this, Schneier has a long tradition of dramatizing and misunderstanding things.” The topic of the email is DDoS attacks, and it is unclear what I am dramatizing and misunderstanding.

Rabbi Schneier is also mentioned, also incidentally, also [once](https://www.jmail.world/thread/EFTA02442876?view=inbox). As far as either of us know, we are not related.
