# Privacy’s Defenders Podcast: Cowboys, Cypherpunks and Visionaries

![Privacy's Defenders: Stories From the Fights for Digital Rights](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/pd-podcast-banner.png?itok=esO9M5UT "Privacy's Defender: Stories From the Fights for Digital Rights")

People are increasingly concerned about the ways in which mass surveillance is tracking our every move: from Flock license plate readers to face recognition to creepy ads that – based on what we see and do online – seem to know everything we’re thinking and planning. It didn’t have to be this way, and since the early days of the internet, a dedicated band of activists, lawyers and technologists have fought for a better, more secure and private digital future – a future that’s still attainable.

Cindy Cohn, who just finished a 26-year run with the Electronic Frontier Foundation including 11 years as its executive director, has lived this fight. She says privacy isn’t just about secrecy: It's ultimately about power – who has it, and who has the ability to protect themselves from it.

Welcome to the first episode of “Privacy’s Defenders,” a podcast about the people – lawyers, journalists, hackers, and others – who’ve fought to secure your digital liberties since before most people even knew what the internet was.
