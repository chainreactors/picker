Qiko3.0 取证智能体赋能电子数据取证：

今天是美亚官方为首批用户举办的软件操作培训线上课程。看完了课程内容感觉还不错，下面就从安装Qiko3.0到解析一个网络流量包为例，走一遍完整的实操过程：

2.点击下一步安装即可。

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcEhqEGqnQKibgHIR47ASxU1w5rD2R0VeLTiaicT8ic1VgwvmBJ0hNVceicys5ZFdcrQtV5dZCFfzcTItYOicoAib1yGRN0eXGNwibUKHLA/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcHiaguAnGib1WBThKxVCPPfJICibibPfo7Awq1H6BdyYJTic7zA3PXuK56ph9CdAbibaGxiatJBjTicWqicROybQ717wkyoOeOqbP5g8NZo/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcEqJhY3o6h0vuBxasjdMHeJ0bWuLdQxeKqTnnPMAPWGBa3JZQRhPvARUJyNyEKWrFaWLMib8cZ6uDg9XiaiaGhL0FOBCSLsz0W47M/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

3.安装中：

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcHb5rKrApeyvtv0yicHYgCFHy4JzbLBmXacm4Wib4qevIIp68VSG5mPb7icB3PvAXpPicxSEoeVsJNFPCicVFk3rRaOZeu2hsZU5vvc/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

4.点击完成即可运行：

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcH1uU1QYicYSIibc0FAHu2uZATrxKicrgO70FcahcX8ibgPb7TtvAQTeESEiaAUpNdeKefYnbic8Qz323ocMeWyC2ibb8srARV3zXAHy4/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

5.软件提供多种登录方式，这里使用短信验证码的方式进行登录：

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcGuXRw9qS42h3aBZSEW355L0O8UqHxNg774ApFR7icLsfj5bwHLxIxdibZQkFSJsBBewicIpgKolcTrAde0TjsbRNeW1O6eA86CoM/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

6.登录成功后，在首页点击“签到”，即可获得100积分。

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcHib1vk45xdLbuEpo3ukJH8dD1XeV5avpicTp2yApw9W7XMPA17AMAOe6bUCHOK1SbzGE2RtVziabxWwCUicnJgTcGtE2P3jOH3OBc/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

7.这些积分可以用于美亚的在线大模型（注：敏感案件建议使用本地大模型）

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcEsUsZdEfeJLqsQQuESEb9ZVEzVWVF0viaov7007KBNpvVhh3s5gMqXcEMDLowVkyViczuV42gaZRE0mPvrVW7cUjCIE9BQFjdfM/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

8.接下来进入实操：

在首页点击“开始任务”即可新建任务，也可以从“最近任务”里看到历史的案件：

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcHhLdxCquGjMDVib9Ij0tcsjOFRge5MUDGXXiaTa5483DvozWd4CvS9iaTgaHYbOYzdT8uGInXujte1rp8rMF2vicYOXiciaP9n9tUK4/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

9.这里有一个亮点：检材可以添加多个，同时进行取证。

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcHnYkrug5NnsEQU3hoRRfibxOZNdhZjqtGzg2WiaOdicDNZkxcNygaJiboaMNRGW7ZKTtOd3D6TUYqMTZu6xxvsHqukxRhnXqsRZqQ/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

10.如果要对本地磁盘进行取证，点击“计算机”即可：

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcFUL41ES6aHJ8HgShfDjkw2dlk9yXDQVdTMXLL1Ur2lZDXRn3uqUjoUibibe3GwLPpXvfuVlicicKz8NxeAvRUeQSOwfK4D5mNicNIo/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

11.网络流量包的检材需要选择文件夹，也就是要解析的流量包需要先放进一个文件夹内。

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcEuVjW0Lbtqkp61uruXPe7nLWLNX3tEU6icHyGKFoW1Nm6fQGlbI3zDzgSYcZtXw4V8bj4Wf8JYUfJH35vMJS148icXVEDNYlgLE/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_png/b34oV9VTkcHrP9OsPaQHWdY17iaCqtq6tTMt3IncbiafJu0bt3Vhxrj2rvG72ibhFJFxCJakJibXdW23IOakIkeQUbVhQBDVRmgQfhmW8bZN2Ys/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcEUF2jhTgIeyHkwJAwpldGtsQe4EZJqsxsJgicnDvTzDwl6iad0Wko5HiatLmBPj9wB87yYkuBJhLaRhZyYs3hQicDOIFx22ThBPI4/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

12.只勾选“流量分析”：

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcGpx9n8FJY27YRmic7otVznH6Nic46fGpXjRfydUR05xyc6UOoetk5AzG003NrqDzFBLsk8Jpu4CLOzOuc3jgfSrgO8jDdvRSufE/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcEQQxR8NpEicyOvZNANDGCFoOHSUk0dZjedNG9xHjgLzyddWFLaib76eH0YBFJpeKwuCC4bathCDxBYcIyQ7fRc12n54qOibKGMnY/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcFzDL5e9a4AZkN8GoFcUSNh74oXzo81m6XAvURz4dxrOxZ5fLTicxWnR4zibjnGP0l87EaSCPdURVNQyxZMiafLckIDCdicuE48coY/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/b34oV9VTkcGvQfZNY4TX4VGqkDWUbNR5hrGtI10FPLDMtLdsNEMPNmyU9T2vOQf5HiaZ5iahnDMbJ39ZQTbcemibe2gIAax9dpGfCIpcLAvicLs/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&from=appmsg)
