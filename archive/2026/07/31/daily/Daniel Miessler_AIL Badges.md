# AIL Badges

A six-level badge family for the AI Influence Level framework

July 31, 2026

[#ai](/archives/?tag=ai) [#design](/archives/?tag=design)

[**AIL***3*](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail "AIL 3 — AI Created, Human Full Structure")

 Embedding-projecting…

[AI Influence Level](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail) has been a text label since 2023. A line at the bottom of a post saying how much of it I wrote and how much the AI did.

Text at the bottom is fine for disclosure. It's bad for glancing. So here's a mark.

## The family [​](#the-family)

Six badges, AIL 0 through AIL 5, one per level. Each links back to the framework.

[**AIL***0*](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail "AIL 0 — Human Created, No AI Involved") Human Created, No AI Involved

[**AIL***1*](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail "AIL 1 — Human Created, Minor AI Assistance") Human Created, Minor AI Assistance

[**AIL***2*](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail "AIL 2 — Human Created, Major AI Augmentation") Human Created, Major AI Augmentation

[**AIL***3*](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail "AIL 3 — AI Created, Human Full Structure") AI Created, Human Full Structure

[**AIL***4*](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail "AIL 4 — AI Created, Human Basic Idea") AI Created, Human Basic Idea

[**AIL***5*](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail "AIL 5 — AI Created, Little Human Involvement") AI Created, Little Human Involvement

Two zones. **AIL** is reversed out of a solid ink panel on the left, the way a film certificate card carries its issuing mark. The level and a five-segment rail sit in the tinted field beside it.

The rail is the part that makes it readable without a legend. Five segments, filled to the level. AIL 0 shows five empty ones, which is exactly the claim being made: no AI in it.

## The colors [​](#the-colors)

The ramp runs muted sage to dusty brick.

I wanted the progression obvious and the judgment absent. A bright green at 0 would read as a gold star, and a bright red at 5 would read as an accusation. Neither is what this measures. A 5 tells you how the thing was made, and that's all it tells you.

## At real size [​](#at-real-size)

## Using it [​](#using-it)

Add `ail: N` to a post's frontmatter and the badge appears in the sidebar:

yaml

```
---
title: "Some Post"
tags: ai|future
ail: 3
---
```

1
2
3
4
5

Or drop one inline anywhere with `<AilBadge level="3" />`.

The note at the bottom of each post stays where it's always been. The badge is the glance, the note is the explanation, and they say the same thing.

#### Notes

1. The framework itself, from 2023: [AI Influence Level (AIL) v1.0](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail). The six levels haven't changed.
2. The badge came out of a bake-off — sixteen candidates across four design directions, scored against a weighted rubric. This one won on being legible small, holding together across all six levels, and reading as issued rather than decorated.
3. 🤖 **AIL 3:** Daniel set the design intent, the color direction, and picked the winner. I (Kai) ran the bake-off, built the component, and wrote this up. [Learn more about AIL](https://danielmiessler.com/blog/ai-influence-level-ail).
