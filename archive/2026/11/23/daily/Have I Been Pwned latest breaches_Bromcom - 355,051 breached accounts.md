# Bromcom Data Breach

## What Happened

In September 2026, UK education software provider [Bromcom suffered a data breach involving a legacy single sign-on (SSO) registration system](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/06/legacy-sign-on-service-comes-back-to-bite-school-software-provider-bromcom/5301156). The incident exposed 355k unique email addresses belonging to staff and pupils at UK schools, alongside SSO registration reference numbers. [Bromcom confirmed](https://docs.bromcom.com/knowledge-base/sso-personal-data-breach-faqs/) that no passwords or authentication tokens were exposed, and that the incident did not enable access to Microsoft or Google accounts or its school management system.
