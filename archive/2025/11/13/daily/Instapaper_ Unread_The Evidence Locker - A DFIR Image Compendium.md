What started as an idea a month or two ago, has become a reality. Introducing **The Evidence Locker**, a compiled repository of publicly available DFIR evidence images. These images come from various CTF competitions, publicly posted challenges, and other evidence files provided to the community by the community.

What sparked my need to make this page was two-fold, having all the info you need in one page including hashes and passwords which sometimes are hard to track down, as well as having it continue to be available indefinitely (pending any GitHub issues) and not DOA because of government shutdowns.

I am adding more links as I find them constantly and I am thinking of a proper way to take submissions as well (if anything open a GitHub issue or pull request in the temporary). This is not a hosting service, just links and info in one easy to filter place. Check it out, feedback is welcomed!
