# Governor Newsom Signs Student-Backed Digital Literacy Bills Alongside Misguided Bans

![young EFF'ers show phones with security icons](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/youth-3.jpg?itok=D86tVixW)

Governor Newsom signed a package of 12 bills yesterday aimed at “protecting children” online. One of them was [AB 1709](https://www.eff.org/press/releases/we-all-deserve-better-internet-not-smaller-one), which EFF has opposed this legislative session and serves as a functional ban on young people under 16 using social media. However, EFF supported two of the bills signed into law, [AB 2071](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billHistoryClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB2071) and [AB 2298](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB2298), which require that children learn critical digital literacy and cybersecurity topics. The bills are an affirmative and constitutional way for the state to address valid concerns about young people’s internet use [without violating](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/02/eff-ninth-circuit-young-people-have-first-amendment-right-use-social-media-and-all) their [First Amendment rights](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/12/states-tried-censor-kids-online-courts-and-eff-mostly-stopped-them-2025-review).

Unlike [blanket bans](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/06/how-and-why-fight-back-against-social-media-bans), A.B. 2071 and A.B. 2298 address online safety through education rather than prohibition. Young people rely on the internet not just for entertainment, but for [civic engagement](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/classroom/classroom-voices/student-voices/2020/11/student-voice-how-young-people-use-social-media-to-engage-civically), [education](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/may/19/australias-social-media-ban-preventing-teens-from-accessing-the-news-research-finds), [self-expression](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2024/03/thousands-young-people-told-us-why-kids-online-safety-act-will-be-harmful-minors), and [community](https://news-decoder.com/social-media-bans-shut-door-to-those-who-lack-safe-spaces/)—especially [vulnerable youth](https://www.lgbttech.org/post/lgbt-tech-leads-coalition-statement-in-opposition-to-age-minimum-social-media-bans) who may lack support in their physical surroundings.
