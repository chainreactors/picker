SAN FRANCISCO – The Electronic Frontier Foundation sent complaints today to the attorneys general of California and New York urging them to investigate Google for deceptive trade practices, related to the company's broken promise to give users prior notice before disclosing their information to law enforcement.

The letters were sent on behalf of Amandla Thomas-Johnson, [whose information was disclosed to U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/04/google-broke-its-promise-me-now-ice-has-my-data%20) (ICE) without prior notice from Google.

For nearly a decade, Google [has promised](https://www.eff.org/who-has-your-back-2017) billions of users that it will notify them before disclosing their personal data to law enforcement. Many times, the company has done just that. But through a hidden and systematic practice, Google has likely violated that promise numerous times over the years.
