# 1 little known secret of net.exe

Posted on [2026-02-14](https://www.hexacorn.com/blog/2026/02/14/1-little-known-secret-of-net-exe/ "12:43 am")  by  [adam](https://www.hexacorn.com/blog/author/adam/ "View all posts by adam")

In Windows 11 24H2+ and Windows Server 2025+ you can use new *net.exe* command line arguments to Configure [alternative SMB ports](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-server/storage/file-server/smb-ports?tabs=powershell): TCP, QUIC, RDMA:

* /TCPPORT <port>
* /QUICPORT <port>
* /RDMAPORT <port>

I am not the first one to point it out — see this [article](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/filecab/smb-alternative-ports-now-supported-in-windows-insider/3974509).
