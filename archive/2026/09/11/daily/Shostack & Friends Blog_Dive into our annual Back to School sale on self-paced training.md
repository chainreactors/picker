# Dive into our annual Back to School sale on self-paced training

Upgrade your engineering skills this fall with 20% off our self-paced Threat Modeling courses.
![A watercolor image of a book-covered desk in autumn. An open book with diagrams sits open on the desk, with a pencil and coffee nearby.](/images/blog/img/2026/back-to-school-sale-2026-1000w.jpeg)

As summer winds down, the autumn engineering cycle kicks into high gear. Before your teams dive into writing code for new features, level up their security skills with our self-paced training courses at a special back to school discount.

In cybersecurity, waiting until production to test your defenses is like trying to wing a final exam without attending lectures. Threat modeling is the mandatory **101** course your team needs to build resilient systems from day one.

## Class is in session: master threat modeling on your own time

Development schedules are packed, which is why the Shostack + Associates self-paced Threat Modeling CBT courses fit seamlessly into busy professional routines.

* [Threat Modeling Essentials](https://courses.shostack.org/courses/essentials-201-self-paced-course): Deepen skills to consistently identify threats, mitigate risks, and champion security.
* [Threat Modeling Intensive](https://courses.shostack.org/courses/intensive-222-self-paced-course): Advanced training for technology professionals to systematically secure complex products.

Take advantage of our Back-to-School promotion to access 20% off our premium self-paced courses until the end of the month and graduate to a secure development lifecycle this fall.

To unlock the savings today on [any of our self-paced courses](https://courses.shostack.org/collections/self-paced), use code SECURE101 at checkout.
