On March 23, the FCC [issued](https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-adds-routers-produced-foreign-countries-covered-list) an [update](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-420034A1.pdf) to their Covered List, a list of equipment banned from obtaining regulatory approval necessary for U.S. sale (and thus effectively a ban on sale of new devices), to include all new routers produced in foreign countries unless they are specifically given an exception by the Department of Defense (DoD) or DHS.
