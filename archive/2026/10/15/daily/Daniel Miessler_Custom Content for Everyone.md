# Custom Content for Everyone

Your AI could interview you for nine hours, then build you ten games and ten TV series of your own

October 14, 2026

[#ai](/archives/?tag=ai) [#future](/archives/?tag=future) [#culture](/archives/?tag=culture)

[**AIL***1*](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail "AIL 1 — Human Created, Minor AI Assistance")

 Stormlight-archiving…

[![A huge theater of purple steel rigging builds castles, starships and temples for one viewer in the only seat, while a tiny studio off to the side holds up a single boxed game](/images/custom-content-for-everyone.webp)](/images/custom-content-for-everyone.webp)

People are starting to think about 1-3 person teams making full games with AI that rival [AAA studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AAA_%28video_game_industry%29).

The idea being that they're competing by making stuff people want.

What I don't think enough people are thinking about is your AI interviewing you for 9 hours and building you 10 perfect games just for you.

> Soon we’ll have games customized to your favorite storylines and fetishes that are continuously varied by AI.[Gaming is Metaverse 1.0 (2022)](/blog/gaming-is-metaverse-1-0)

Or making you 10 full TV series like [Game of Thrones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_Thrones), except fully customized to you.

Basically skipping past us getting our content from indie game developers or small media studios, and going right to AI building everyone their own custom content.

That's 🤯, and not currently factored in at all.
