Another day, another disruptive network outage (or two) as government officials and company execs scramble to assure an anxious populace that these are not coordinated cyberattacks – and security experts call this the new normal.

A classified network that the US National Security Agency uses for internal communications, called NSANet, went offline on Tuesday in what the agency described as a “temporary degradation during scheduled routine maintenance.”

“While some internal systems were affected, mission-critical capabilities remain fully operational and our ability to defend national security continues,” the NSA [said in a statement](https://www.nsa.gov/Press-Room/Press-Releases-Statements/Press-Release-View/Article/4650245/nsa-statement-on-network-issues/) following [media reports](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/17/politics/highly-classified-national-security-communications-network-goes-down) about the outage. “NSA's infrastructure is designed with multiple layers of redundancy to maintain operations during planned maintenance or unexpected events.”

Around the same time, Apple users began reporting issues across multiple services – including Apple Support, iCloud, the App Store, Apple Pay, and more – with [Apple retail stores](https://www.nbcnews.com/video/shorts/customers-stopped-at-apple-store-amid-internal-outage-273671749612) worldwide also reportedly experiencing disruptions that halted sales and in-person support.

A day earlier, customers at major US banks including JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, and US Bank reported service outages that [Chase attributed](https://x.com/Chase/status/2122433813955379268) to “a software bug in on-premise hardware systems - there is no evidence this is cyber-related.”

All of these high-profile outages followed last week’s US Federal Aviation Authority (FAA) order [g](https://www.theregister.com/offbeat/2026/11/13/faa-blames-ground-stop-on-faulty-copper-wiring/5309912)[rounding all planes](https://www.theregister.com/offbeat/2026/11/13/faa-blames-ground-stop-on-faulty-copper-wiring/5309912) on Friday due to problems with copper wiring during an L3Harris network upgrade, and a [Microsoft 365 outage](https://www.theregister.com/saas/2026/11/12/microsoft-struggling-to-fix-massive-cloud-outage-thats-taken-down-email-and-intune/5309531) on Thursday.
