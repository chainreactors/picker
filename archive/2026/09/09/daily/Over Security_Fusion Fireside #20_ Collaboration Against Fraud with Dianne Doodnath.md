## Fusion Fireside #20: Collaboration Against Fraud with Dianne Doodnath

09 September 2026

![](https://www.threatfabric.com/hubfs/Social/TF_FusionFireside_DianneDoodnath.jpg)

### Jump to

In this episode of Fusion Fireside we speak to Dianne Doodnath, Principal at UK Finance. Dianne shares her perspective on fraud prevention, consumer protection, and the power of cross-sector collaboration. Drawing on a career spanning both gambling and financial services, she explains why tackling modern fraud requires banks, telecom providers, technology platforms, regulators, and governments to work together.

##

## Insights from the Fireside

* **Fraud is no longer just a banking problem:** Criminals target consumers through calls, texts, social media, messaging apps, and online platforms, making fraud a cross-industry challenge.
* **Collaboration drives meaningful change:** Successful fraud prevention initiatives often emerge from partnerships between financial services, telecom providers, regulators, and technology companies.
* **Consumer harm is the real metric:** Beyond financial losses, fraud damages confidence, wellbeing, and trust in the digital economy.
* **Social engineering is reshaping fraud:** Modern scams increasingly rely on manipulating people rather than compromising systems.
* **Digital safety should become a habit:** Consumers need to approach online interactions with the same instinctive caution they apply in everyday life.
* **Mobile threats cannot be ignored:** Smartphones have become central to consumers' lives, making mobile malware a growing concern.
* **Better data leads to better protection:** Accurate classification of fraud incidents is critical to understanding emerging threats and directing resources effectively.
* **The challenge is growing, but so is the response:** Industries across the ecosystem are investing in new technologies, partnerships, and initiatives to stay ahead of criminals.

Despite the growing threat landscape, Dianne remains optimistic. Her message is clear: protecting consumers requires collaboration, innovation, and a shared commitment across industries to make the digital world safer for everyone.

## Final Thoughts

Fraud continues to evolve as criminals exploit new technologies, platforms, and communication channels. Yet one message stood out throughout this conversation with Dianne Doodnath: no single industry can solve the problem alone.

Whether tackling call spoofing, SMS impersonation, social engineering, or emerging mobile threats, meaningful progress comes from collaboration between financial institutions, telecom providers, technology companies, regulators, and governments. Equally important is empowering consumers with the knowledge and confidence to navigate an increasingly digital world.

While the threat landscape continues to expand, Doodnath remains optimistic. The combination of innovation, cross-sector cooperation, and a shared commitment to reducing consumer harm offers a clear path forward. The challenge is significant, but so is the collective determination to make digital services safer for everyone.
