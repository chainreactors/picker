Finding a new CEO is just one of their problems. Nvidia is investigating an alleged breach following claims by ransomware group The Gentlemen's affiliates that they hacked the AI giant and stole sensitive information.

The [embattled chipmaker](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/11/18/business-as-usual-but-huang-aint-coming-back-says-nvidia/5310772) told The Register's that it was investigating a possible third-party data breach. “We have found no indication that Nvidia systems or networks were affected,” a spokesperson said. “We continue to monitor the situation."

The ransomware gang’s data-leak site, [shared on social media](https://x.com/IntCyberDigest/status/2123027818162344194) and seen by The Register, doesn’t show any stolen files or indicate what information the group claims to have stolen. Nvidia first appeared on the criminals’ blog on Wednesday, with The Gentlemen promising “DATA INFO - SOON.”

REG AD

The post also included a countdown, ending November 28.

REG AD

While we can’t verify the criminals’ claims, the group, which [emerged in mid-2025](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/09/01/the-gentlemen-come-calling-as-nutex-confirms-sensitive-data-theft/5293642), is one of the most prolific ransomware-as-a-service operations. Its affiliates claimed more than 320 victims as of April, with the majority of these alleged ransomware infections (240) occurring in the first half of 2026.

The actual count, however, is likely much higher. Check Point researchers accessed an affiliate’s live command-and-control server during an incident response case earlier this year. The server contained victim telemetry that revealed [more than 1,570 victims](https://research.checkpoint.com/2026/dfir-report-the-gentlemen/), mostly located in the US, and primarily corporate and organizational environments.

According to an operational technology (OT) threat report from Trellix published on Wednesday, industrial [ransomware claims jumped nearly 51 percent](https://www.trellix.com/advanced-research-center/threat-reports/ot-threat-report-nov-2026/) - to about 1,500 posts- compared to last quarter, and The Gentlemen’s affiliates were the most prolific claim posters during this time period. They accounted for about 16 percent of all posts.
