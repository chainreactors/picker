## I wrote a new book – this time about money

November 25, 2026
[rtrouton](https://derflounder.wordpress.com/author/rtrouton/) [Leave a comment](#respond)
[Go to comments](#comments)

Over the past year, I’ve been working on a new book covering a topic that’s different from my usual focus: personal finance. In my case, I wanted to know why I always seemed to be broke right before my next scheduled payday. In the process of discovering how my money was coming in and going out, I learned just how poorly I was managing my money.

As part of my effort to do better and start managing my money successfully instead of letting my money manage me, I wrote a book titled *Funding Future You* about what I learned along the way.

![Funding Future You.](https://derflounder.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/funding-future-you.png?w=387&h=600 "Funding-Future-You.png")

*Funding Future You* is available now as an eBook from Apple Books and Barnes & Noble.

* Apple Books: <https://books.apple.com/us/book/funding-future-you/id6838495576>
* Barnes & Noble: <https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/funding-future-you-rich-trouton/1152067165>

Please visit the following link for a list of other eBook vendors offering *Funding Future You*:

* Books2Read: <https://books2read.com/u/aPNZEx>

A paperback edition is planned for the near future.

Whether you’re looking for better ways to manage your own money or want to help someone else do the same, *Funding Future You* may be just what you’re looking for. Please consider ordering your copy today!
