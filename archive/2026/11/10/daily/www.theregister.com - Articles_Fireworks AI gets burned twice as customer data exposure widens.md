Fireworks AI has disclosed another cyberattack in October and a wider customer data exposure involving billing records, datasets, and incomplete model weights.

The company, which lets customers train and run AI models, [said](https://fireworks.ai/security/bulletins/october-2026-security-incident-update) a third-party cloud provider hosting its research and development machines was compromised on October 23. Attackers used that access the following day to reach Fireworks development systems and run an unauthorized workload.

Fireworks suspects links to financially motivated criminals involved in [LLMJacking](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/02/token-thieves-costing-companies-100k-per-day-in-illicit-llm-charges/5306967) – selling unauthorized access to AI models through illicit gateways.

REG AD

Fireworks grouped the affected data into three categories:

REG AD

* Resource metadata: Resource identifiers, display names, descriptions, creation times, and more
* Billing information: Billing and shipping addresses, invoice and payment histories, account balances, and credit usages. Payment card numbers are not saved and therefore unaffected
* Model weights and datasets: For certain customers, a subset of incomplete model weights and datasets were accessed, Fireworks said

Lin Qiao, Fireworks co-founder and CEO, said the apparent motive was not stealing model weights in response to concerns raised by Cantina Security CEO Hari Mulackal.

"From what we see attackers are not after weights," [said](https://x.com/lqiao/status/2119996096512774241) Qiao. "Most downloads were partial and stopped."

Render founder and CEO Anurag Goel [shared a notification from Notion](https://x.com/anuraggoel/status/2120009813983924318) warning that some chats with its AI assistant may have been exposed through the [Fireworks](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/07/fireworks-says-youre-wasting-money-on-ai-unless-youre-using-all-the-models/5308277) incident.

"Following an investigation by the Notion security team, we have determined that your workspace, Render, was among the <1 percent of Notion workspaces that may have been affected by the Fireworks security incident," the email sent to Goel stated.

"Some unattributable chat sessions with Notion AI may have been exposed in this incident."

Notion said the exposed transcripts lacked separate information linking them to particular users or workspaces. As [Reddit users pointed out](https://www.reddit.com/r/Notion/comments/1xsddi7/comment/19sesv7ptfhgj/), however, the conversations themselves could contain identifying details.
