*The Legal Aid Society*

**Bronx, NY**

*Lighthouse*

**United States**

Senior consultant role performing forensically sound ESI collections, expert-level forensic analysis of computers, mobile devices and cloud sources, preparing detailed reports, providing expert testimony, and supporting litigation and investigation projects.

[View Job](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/senior-digital-forensics-consultant-at-lighthouse-4405174865)

### Junior Digital Forensic Analyst

*Peraton*

**Arlington, VA**

On-site junior forensics analyst role supporting a federal investigations lab. Conduct forensic examinations of digital devices and media, produce court-admissible reports, provide expert witness testimony, and support search warrant executions using industry-standard tools including Cellebrite, Magnet Axiom, and X-Ways.

[View Job](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/junior-digital-forensic-analyst-at-peraton-4404477811)

**United States**

*The Amatriot Group*

**Linthicum Heights, MD**

Onsite DoD-supported role requiring active Secret clearance. Examines digital media—computers, phones, storage devices—in support of federal criminal investigations, using tools like EnCase, FTK and Cellebrite. Expert witness testimony may be required.

[View Job](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/digital-forensics-examiner-at-the-amatriot-group-4397285645)

**Washington, DC**

[View Job](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/sba-digital-forensics-engineer-at-cfocus-software-incorporated-4412178094)
