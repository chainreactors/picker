# Japan confirms arrest of Russian Qilin operative, extradition to Germany

Japan’s National Police Agency confirmed on Thursday the arrest and extradition of a Russian national accused of being involved in the Qilin ransomware gang.

Japanese officials [said](https://www.npa.go.jp/news/release/2026/20261008news.pdf) they received an arrest warrant from Germany for the 28-year-old Russian national, whose name was not released, for their alleged role in a ransomware attack on a German company. Japan’s Ministry of Justice worked to detain the suspect before they were extradited to Germany.

The news outlet Nippon [reported](https://www.nippon.com/en/news/yjj2026100800838/) that the operation began in May when officials learned the suspect planned to come to Japan for vacation. He was arrested at a hotel in Osaka that month and sent to Germany in June. German law enforcement did not respond to requests for comment.

The Qilin ransomware gang has been behind dozens of high-profile attacks, including incidents in both Germany and Japan.

The group took credit for an [April ransomware attack](https://therecord.media/hackers-threaten-to-leak-german-political-party-data) on German democratic socialist political party Die Linke and another devastating incident last year involving [Japanese beverage giant Asahi](https://therecord.media/qilin-ransomware-gang-alleged-asahi-hackers).

Asahi spent weeks recovering from the attack, which disrupted its order processing, shipping, and customer services. The attackers also leaked Asahi’s financial records, employee data, contracts and development forecasts.

The group [faced increased law enforcement scrutiny](https://therecord.media/qilin-ransomware-gang-darknet-site-down-london-hospitals) in 2024 after an attack on a British healthcare company that prompted [major disruptions](https://therecord.media/ransomware-nhs-cyberattack-disruption) to medical services. It quickly returned with attacks on the [government of Palau](https://therecord.media/palau-health-ministry-ransomware-recover) and [one of the largest newspaper chains in the U.S.](https://therecord.media/newspaper-lee-enterprises-cyberattack-ssn).

Qilin was [one of the most active](https://therecord.media/qilin-ransomware-gang-hits-hundreds-of-orgs-2025) ransomware operations in 2025. targeting [Kuala Lumpur International Airport](https://therecord.media/malaysia-pm-says-country-rejected-ransom-demand-airport-cyberattack), the [Texas city of Sugar Land](https://therecord.media/houston-suburb-cyberattack-services), and more.

The group has [continued](https://therecord.media/hackers-threaten-to-leak-german-political-party-data) to launch [damaging attacks](https://therecord.media/romania-conpet-oil-pipeline-ransomware-attack) in 2026, with researchers saying it was the second most active ransomware gang in July with 127 reported attacks. French rugby club Stade Français Paris [confirmed](https://therecord.media/french-rugby-club-restores-systems-after-cyberattack) it had been attacked after being added to Qilin’s leak site.

In August, Qilin actors took credit for a [ransomware attack](https://therecord.media/doj-atf-cyberattack-qilin-ransomware) on the U.S. Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF). The group allegedly stole information about targets of ATF investigations.
