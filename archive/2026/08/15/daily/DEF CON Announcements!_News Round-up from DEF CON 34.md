## News Round-up from DEF CON 34

Posted 2026-08-14

![DEF CON 34 logo](../../images/defcon-34/dc-34-logo-transparent.webp)

DEF CON 34 was a busy weekend in all kinds of ways, including news coverage. Please enjoy a roundup of some of the stories that came out of DC34. We’ll add to it in the coming days and weeks as more gets published.
