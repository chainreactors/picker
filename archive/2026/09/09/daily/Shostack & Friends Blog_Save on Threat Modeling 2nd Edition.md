# Save on Threat Modeling 2nd Edition

Barnes & Noble is having a preorder sale. Save on Adam's new book, coming February 2027!
![A promotional image for Threat Modeling, the bible of threat modeling, available for pre-order now!](/images/blog/img/2026/B&N-preorder-1000w.jpeg)

We have been absolutely thrilled by the response to the initial book announcement, especially when it hit #1 for new releases in two Amazon categories! The early enthusiasm and support from our community underscore just how foundational the first version of Threat Modeling has been and how much appetite there is for updated techniques for a new era of software development.

The upcoming book, Threat Modeling: Designing for Security in an AI World, expands on structured approach mechanics to tackle the systems, software, and emerging AI technologies that didn't exist when the classic first edition was originally published.

For organizations and practitioners looking to secure upcoming systems or grab a copy early, a limited-time discount is live right now. From today to Friday, Barnes & Noble has an exclusive members-only promotion for preorders, including Threat Modeling: Designing for Security in an AI World.

* **25% Off All Preorders:** Barnes & Noble Rewards and Premium Members save 25% across print books, audiobooks, and ebooks using code PREORDER25 at checkout.
* **Free Enrollment Available:** If you aren't a member yet, joining the standard B&N Rewards tier is completely free.

[Preorder your copy of Threat Modeling: Designing for Security in an AI World](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/threat-modeling-adam-shostack/1150306289) and take advantage of the discount directly with Barnes & Noble.

While you're here, sign up for our three-day course, [Threat Modeling Intensive Using AI](https://owasp.glueup.com/event/167174/register/e), in person in San
Francisco in association with OWASP’s [Global Appsec Conference](https://owasp.glueup.com/event/owasp-global-appsec-usa-2026-167174/).
