Ransomnews has confirmed 25 ransomware and extortion attacks for October 2026 so far, against 58 for September and 121 for October 2025. The figure is provisional and will rise for months. Everest and The Gentlemen lead with three confirmed victims each, government is the most-hit sector with five, and in 11 of the 19 incidents with a leak-site listing the gang went public before the victim did.

## The count, and why it will rise

A month’s count is lowest the week it closes. An attack enters the dataset only once the organisation, a regulator or a stock-exchange filing confirms it, and most of those confirmations come weeks or months later. August is the clearest example: it stood at 51 when we published its report on 7 September and is at 116 now. Between October 2025 and August 2026, months settled between 85 and 129. This report first ran on 11 November with 13 incidents; a review of press-confirmed cases the same day took it to 25, and about 50 further October candidates are still waiting for a confirming source or an editor’s review. Every row, with its source, is on the [October 2026 data page](https://ransomnews.com/ransomware-attacks/2026-10/) and in the [confirmed attacks register](https://ransomnews.com/confirmed-ransomware-attacks/).

## How were the 25 confirmed?

In two ways, and the difference matters. In 13 incidents the organisation, an official or a filing says in terms that it was ransomware or that systems were encrypted. In the other 12 the organisation confirmed an intrusion or a data theft without naming ransomware, and the link to ransomware is a group’s leak-site listing ...
