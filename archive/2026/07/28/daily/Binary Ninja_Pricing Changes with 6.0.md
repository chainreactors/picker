## Perpetual Licensing

Your Binary Ninja license does not expire. Renewing extends access to updates and support for an additional year but it is not required to use versions youâve had access to.

As commercial software has increasingly moved toward subscriptions, we considered whether we should too. The advantage is a more consistent funding model, but we didnât feel it was right for us.

Instead, with 6.0, we are reducing our renewal discounts. This does increase the price of renewals, but they remain discounted from the cost of a new license and unlike a subscription, the software does not expire.
