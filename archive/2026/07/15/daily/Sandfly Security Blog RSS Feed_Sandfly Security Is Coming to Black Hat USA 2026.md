# Sandfly Security Is Coming to Black Hat USA 2026

15 July 2026

News

Sandfly Security is heading to Las Vegas for Black Hat USA 2026, running August 4–6 at Mandalay Bay. You'll find us in Startup City at booth 6014, and we look forward to seeing you there.

### **See Sandfly 5.8 with Agentless Response Live in Action**

We're excited to demo our latest release, [Sandfly 5.8](https://sandflysecurity.com/blog/sandfly-58-agentless-response-and-ssh-key-management), which introduces Agentless Response. Now you don't just detect threats, you can act on the instantly and without agents. Kill or suspend malicious processes, retrieve process binaries for analysis (even ones deleted from disk to evade forensics), delete rogue SSH keys everywhere they exist, and clean up duplicate keys across your fleet. All of it works across every Linux distribution, architecture, and system age we support — cloud, on-prem, legacy, air-gapped, and embedded. Visit our booth to see live threats detected and neutralized in real time.
