Key takeaways

1. 01

   Columbia University agreed to pay $16.1 million to settle a lawsuit over a 2025 cyberattack.
2. 02

   The breach exposed data from more than 868,000 students, applicants, faculty, and former students.
3. 03

   Stolen information included Social Security numbers, academic records, financial details, insurance data, and health information.
4. 04

   Eligible victims may claim up to $8,500 if they can prove losses from the breach.
