## Invasive tracking, forced biometric identification

Outlier is a platform operated by Scale AI, one of the biggest players in this field is the US. More than 900,000 people work as freelancers on the platform, often earning low wages while helping train AI systems. Outlierâs customers include major corporations such as Meta, OpenAI, xAI, Google, Anthropic, and the US government

Our technical analysis found that, while workers use the Outlier platform, trackers associated with companies including TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, the survey platform Sprig and the data analytics company Datadog collect and transmit personal information to third parties every few seconds. This information includes IP addresses, contact details, unique identifiers and information about the pages workers visit.

Workers are also repeatedly required to undergo biometric verification through a company called Persona. The process includes recording a video of their face and uploading an identity document. In addition, Persona’s privacy policy allows it to collect extensive personal information, including IP addresses, device information, geolocation data, addresses and consumer credit data.

## Complaints filed in four EU countries

Today, noyb, an NGO working to enforce data protection laws, filed five GDPR complaints against Scale AI with data protection authorities in France, Italy, the Netherlands and Austria.

The complaints concern several alleged violations of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the lack of a valid legal basis for placing trackers on workers’ devices without their consent, the processing of biometric data, and violations of the principle of data minimisation.

According to noyb, Scale AI also failed to respond to the complainants’ requests for access to their personal data, potentially violating their right of access under the GDPR.
noyb is asking the competent authorities to investigate the alleged violations and proposes that they impose administrative fines to deter similar practices in the future.

Read the full press release by noyb here: <https://noyb.eu/en/ai-training-platform-outlier-illegally-surveils-european-platform-workers>
