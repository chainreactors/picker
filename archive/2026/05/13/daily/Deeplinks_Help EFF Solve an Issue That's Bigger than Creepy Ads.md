Millions of people around the world use EFF's [Privacy Badger](https://privacybadger.org). This browser extension blocks the hidden trackers that twist your web browsing into a commodity for Big Tech, advertisers, scammers, and data brokers. But did you know that we’re trying to solve an issue that’s even bigger than creepy ads and user profiling? [You can help.](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/spring--DL3)

[JOIN EFF](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/spring--DL3)

Online tracking isn't just creepy and unethical. It also enables government surveillance. [Widespread commercial surveillance](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/01/online-behavioral-ads-fuel-surveillance-industry-heres-how) and weak privacy laws allow data brokers to harvest your data and [sell it to law enforcement agencies](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/03/targeted-advertising-gives-your-location-government-just-ask-cbp) including the FBI, CBP, and ICE.
