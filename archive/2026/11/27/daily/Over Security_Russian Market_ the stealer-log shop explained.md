Russian Market is a criminal online shop that sells stealer logs, the saved passwords and session cookies that infostealer malware copies from an infected computer. Analysts at Lunar [saw 14.1 million logs listed](https://lunarcyber.com/blog/severvault-unmasked-the-ai-log-marketplace-is-selling-russian-markets-logs/) there on 16 November 2026, and Rapid7 [found a typical price](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/tr-inside-russian-market-uncovering-the-botnet-empire/) of about $10 in 2025. The FBI and CISA [name it](https://www.ic3.gov/CSA/2025/250729.pdf) as a place where the Scattered Spider group buys employee logins.

### In brief

* Rapid7 dates Russian Market to early 2020 and SOCRadar [to February 2019](https://socradar.io/blog/dark-web-market-russian-market/). The shop added stolen card data and stealer logs in 2021 and stopped selling remote-desktop access in January 2024, Rapid7 says.
* Lunar’s count of 14,125,159 logs from 42 vendors is about 2.6 times the 5.35 million that Secureworks [recorded](https://www.sophos.com/en-us/research/the-growing-threat-from-infostealers) at the end of February 2023.
* ReliaQuest [found single sign-on credentials](https://resources.reliaquest.com/image/upload/v1748640352/Threat_Report_-_The_Infostealer_Pipeline_uqvjq6.pdf) in 77% of the Russian Market logs it analysed and software-as-a-service credentials in 61%.
* Ransomnews’s own Infostealer Exposure Dataset, a source separate from Russian Market’s listings, attributes 31.6% of 17.2 million stealer-log records to LummaC2 as of 1 November 2026, and Vidar overtook RedLine to take second place later in the month.
* Europol [told Genesis Market victims](https://www.politie.nl/binaries/content/assets/politie/nieuws/2023/april/press-release---operation-genesis-market.pdf) in 2023 to remove the malware before changing any password, and that order applies to any individual whose logins are in a stealer log.
