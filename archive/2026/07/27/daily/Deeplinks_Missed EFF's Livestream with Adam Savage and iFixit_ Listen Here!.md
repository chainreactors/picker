Either way, if you want to stay on top of these monthly conversations, you can now subscribe to our new podcast feed for EFFecting Change—starting with our conversation on the [Right to Repair movement](https://www.eff.org/issues/right-to-repair) with Adam Savage and iFixit CEO Kyle Wiens:
