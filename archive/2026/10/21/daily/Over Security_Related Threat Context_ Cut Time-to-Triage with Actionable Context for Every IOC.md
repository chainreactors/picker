Your New MTTR Shortcut — Introducing Related Threat Context: Cut Time-to-Triage with Actionable IOC Context

During triage, analysts may overlook valuable threat intelligence simply because they don’t know it exists. Previous investigations, related malicious activity, and industry-specific observations could provide the context needed to decide what to investigate next.

**Now, all that valuable context is finally within reach!**

With **Related Threat Context**, now available to [ANY.RUN Enterprise](https://any.run/enterprise/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=related-threat-context-release&utm_term=211026&utm_content=linktoenterprise#contact-sales) clients, SOC and MSSP teams can immediately see these connections alongside IOCs found during Interactive Sandbox analysis and explore relevant findings. **This helps Tier 1 analysts reduce** [**time-to-triage**](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/triage-analyst-guide/)**, identify what deserves closer investigation, and make better-informed decisions about which cases to prioritize or escalate.**

## The Problem: Missing IOC Context Slows Down Triage

A Tier 1 analyst investigates a suspicious URL in [ANY.RUN Interactive Sandbox](https://any.run/features/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=related-threat-context-release&utm_term=211026&utm_content=linktosandboxlanding). The analysis reveals malicious behavior and several IOCs, but the analyst still needs to decide whether to close the case, investigate further, or escalate it.

**Relevant context may already exist in previous team investigations, industry-related analyses, or** [**ANY.RUN Threat Intelligence**](https://any.run/threat-intelligence-lookup/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=related-threat-context-release&utm_term=211026&utm_content=linktothreatintelligencelanding)**.** Yet analysts may spend time searching for it manually or make decisions without knowing it’s available.

**The result is longer time-to-triage, duplicated investigations, and unnecessary Tier 2 escalations.**

**But not anymore!**

Related Threat Context makes these connections visible directly in the Sandbox, helping analysts discover useful intelligence earlier and make [faster, better-informed decisions](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/efficient-soc-for-fast-response/).

## The Triage Boost You’ve Been Waiting For: Related Threat Context

[![](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/related_context2.jpeg)](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/related_context2.mp4)

*Related analyses help SOC teams discover additional context about IOCs to better inform response*

When ANY.RUN identifies an IOC during a sandbox analysis, Related Threat Context can surface three types of information.

* **Industry Context:** Shows whether an IOC has appeared in analyses associated with the [organization’s industry](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/threat-data-for-enterprises/), helping internal [SOCs](https://any.run/enterprise/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=related-threat-context-release&utm_term=211026&utm_content=linktoenterprise) prioritize indicators that deserve closer investigation. Industry data is anonymized, and a match is an additional signal rather than proof of targeting. This context is unavailable to [MSSPs](https://any.run/mssp/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=related-threat-context-release&utm_term=211026&utm_content=linktomssp).

* **Team Context:** Connects IOCs with previous team [Sandbox](https://any.run/features/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=related-threat-context-release&utm_term=211026&utm_content=linktosandboxlanding) analyses, allowing analysts to reuse existing findings, avoid duplicate investigations, and coordinate with colleagues across shifts.
