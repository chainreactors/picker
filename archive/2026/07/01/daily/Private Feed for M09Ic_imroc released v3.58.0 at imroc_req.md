## New Features

* **Add `SetTLSFingerprintSpec` for custom ClientHelloSpec support** — accepts a `*utls.ClientHelloSpec` for fine-grained TLS fingerprint customization (JA3/JA4). Closes [#477](https://github.com/imroc/req/pull/477), [#478](https://github.com/imroc/req/pull/478).
* **Add `SensitiveHeadersRedirectPolicy`** — strips custom auth headers (e.g. `X-API-Key`, `X-Auth-Token`) on cross-domain redirects to prevent credential leakage (CWE-200). Fixes [#489](https://github.com/imroc/req/issues/489).
