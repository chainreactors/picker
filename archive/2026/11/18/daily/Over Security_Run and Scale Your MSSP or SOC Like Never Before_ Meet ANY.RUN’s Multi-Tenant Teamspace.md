## **From Teamwork to Teamspace: A Major Step Forward**

![](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/image.gif)

*Teamspace upgrades the standard Teamwork for more effective SOC and MSSP operations*

[Teamwork](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/teamwork/) has long helped security teams work together in ANY.RUN. Now, we’re taking it further.

**Multi-Tenant Teamspace builds on the existing Teamwork capabilities**, adding new ways to manage customers, teams, resources, and security results.

Here’s what’s new:

* **Multi-Tenancy:** Separate client environments to keep customer data and investigations private.

* **Customer View:** Direct access for customers to follow their security work and see the results of your service.

* **Multi-Team & Centralized Management:** Independent security teams with clear roles, access control, and shared resources.

* **Cases & Teamhistory:** Related analyses in one case and shared investigation history to avoid repeated work and [speed up triage & response](https://any.run/cybersecurity-blog/increasing-soc-roi/).

* **Team Performance Overview:** Track team activity, investigation results, and key detection metrics to understand how your teams are performing.

* **Threat Landscape Overview:** See detected threats, trends, and analysis coverage across your organization, teams, or individual customers.

From managing customer work to reviewing detection results, these tasks now turn into one flow.
