# Groups Associated With India’s Far-Right Receive Millions Through US Donor-Advised Funds

October 6, 2026

* [India](/tag/india)
* [USA](/tag/usa)

*Sign up [here](https://bellingcat-investigations.beehiiv.com/) to receive Bellingcat’s biggest investigations by email as soon as they are published*.

US non-profits whose founding leaders or organisations have been associated with India’s controversial Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) – or initiatives the Hindu nationalist group has endorsed – received millions of dollars over recent years through grants that allow donors to remain anonymous, a Bellingcat analysis has found.

The right-wing and Hindu nationalist volunteer organisation is [widely considered](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crkjp3g2k8lo) the [ideological parent](https://thediplomat.com/2024/09/rss-the-ideological-mentor-of-indias-ruling-bjp-enters-its-100th-year/) of India’s ruling Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP). Prime Minister [Narendra Modi](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/world-asia-india-29593336), a former RSS worker, is one of its most famous supporters.

But the RSS’ vision of India as a [Hindu nation](https://indianexpress.com/article/political-pulse/mohan-bhagwat-interview-ls-polls-reading-rss-chief-message-8376285/) is controversial both [at home](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/why-rss-was-banned-three-times-in-the-past-1149307.html) and abroad: it was previously banned in India [three times](https://frontline.thehindu.com/politics/rss-centenary-hindutva-secularism-political-strategy-impact-india/article70070085.ece), and a bipartisan US commission on religious freedom recently [called for the US government to sanction](https://www.uscirf.gov/sites/default/files/2026-03/USCIRF_2026_AR%20%282%29.pdf) the group “for their responsibility and tolerance of severe violations of religious freedom”. Almost a hundred US politicians, both Democratic and Republican, [recently pledged](https://amp.scroll.in/article/1095396/two-days-after-bhagwats-speech-in-new-york-90-us-politicians-pledge-to-not-take-rss-linked-funding) not to take funding from RSS-linked entities.

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/10/2026-03-22T132329Z_1416121088_MT1ZUMA00067J15W_RTRMADP_3_ZUMA-1-1200x798.jpg)

*India’s far-right Hindu nationalist volunteer group RSS remains an unregistered entity in India. Source: Prabhat Kumar Verma/ZUMA Press Wire via Reuters Connect*

While there have been [efforts to map out](https://rssproject.caravanmagazine.in/) RSS-related entities, it can be challenging to establish concrete links between the organisations because the RSS remains an [unregistered entity](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/why-has-the-rss-not-registered-itself-the-law-and-history-behind-the-persistent-debate-10744212/) in India and has [no formal membership](https://thediplomat.com/2026/07/why-is-the-rss-the-parent-organization-of-indias-ruling-bjp-still-unregistered/), maintaining that it is a “[body of individuals](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/rss-is-recognised-as-body-of-individuals-bhagwat-tells-critics/article70258943.ece)” with shared ideological views.

“The RSS functions without independent oversight of its resources, finances, influence, and activities,” said Dr Angana P Chatterji, an anthropologist and scholar of South Asia studies at the University of California, Berkeley. “This is not proportional to the vast power, reach, and influence it possesses. Its leadership and cadre are protected from accountability and scrutiny,” she added.

The RSS had not responded to multiple requests for comment, as of publication.

Bellingcat identified two US-registered non-profits that appeared to have past and present ties to the RSS, or organisations associated with them, based on the non-profits’ or RSS websites and publications, as well as statements by the non-profits’ founders and spokespeople. The non-profits were the Ekal Vidyalaya Foundation of USA Inc (Ekal USA) and Sewa International Inc (referred to as Sewa USA).

We looked at all [Form 990s](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/) – tax returns that US non-profit organisations are required to file annually – listing [Ekal USA](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/770554248) and [Sewa USA](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/200638718) as grant recipients, as well as the Form 990s of the charities themselves, from 2020 to 2024. As of September, the latest available Form 990s for Sewa USA were for the fiscal year ending December 2024, while Ekal USA also filed a form for the fiscal year ending December 2025, which was excluded for consistency, although the same pattern was observed during this period.

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/10/KUE3W-non-profit-funding-for-ekal-usa-and-sewa-usa--1200x437.jpg)

This analysis showed that the charities received at least US$24.4 million from 34 organisations that manage donor-advised funds (DAFs), or 20.7 percent of the total $117.8 million they reported receiving in contributions and grants during this period.

The actual amount from DAFs may be higher, as we excluded filings that covered partial years outside of this period – for example, June 2019 to June 2020. The filings from the two charities we identified were based on the calendar year.

The Form 990s also revealed that the vast majority of the money spent by these two charities on grants to other organisations or individuals was in India or the South Asia region rather than in the US: Ekal USA donated $33.7 million in foreign grants – all of which went towards the Ekal Vidyalaya project in India according to its filings –  and none in domestic grants in the five years from 2020 to 2024, while Sewa USA reported $29.7 million in foreign grants and $622,613 in domestic grants during the same period.
