Last year during LGBTQ+ Pride month, we launched an [LGBT Q&A](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/06/lgbt-qa-your-online-speech-and-privacy-questions-answered) where we answered your most pressing digital rights questions on EFF’s [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/efforg/) and [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/%40efforg)  accounts.

Ahead of LGBT Q&A Season 2 launching next week, we’re posting a recap with some of the questions we answered. Check them out below.

1. You [wanted to know](https://www.instagram.com/efforg/reel/DSdQCPGEq8o/): How to stay safe when dating online.
2. You [asked](https://www.instagram.com/efforg/reel/DMh0XtkSq0z/): I'm a 17 year old trans woman and my address is public on the Internet. What steps can I take to mitigate this risk?
3. You [wondered about](https://www.instagram.com/efforg/reel/DLaTcBTykWP/): Tips for staying safe at Budapest Pride.
4. You [questioned](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DMIaLrCytof/): Why does homophobic content I report on social media not get removed?
5. You [asked](https://www.instagram.com/efforg/reel/DL5IW8zIF29/): What pictures are safe to use on dating apps?
6. You [wanted to know](https://www.instagram.com/p/DPEr9tUkihO/): Is it safe to have gay, trans, and Palestinian flags in my bio?

We’re here to help build an online space where *you* get to decide what aspects of yourself you share with others, how you present to the world, and what thing...
