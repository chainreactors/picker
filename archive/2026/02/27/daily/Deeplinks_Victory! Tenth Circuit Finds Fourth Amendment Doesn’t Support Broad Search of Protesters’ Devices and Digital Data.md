In a big win for protesters’ rights, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Tenth Circuit [overturned](https://www.ca10.uscourts.gov/sites/ca10/files/opinions/010111390292.pdf) a lower court’s dismissal of a challenge to sweeping warrants to search a protester’s devices and digital data and a nonprofit’s social media data.

The case, [*Armendariz v. City of Colorado Springs*](https://www.aclu-co.org/cases/armendariz-and-chinook-center-v-city-colorado-springs-et-al/), arose after a housing protest in 2021, during which Colorado Springs police arrested protesters for obstructing a roadway. After the demonstration, police also obtained warrants to seize and search through the devices and data of Jacqueline Armendariz Unzueta, who they claimed threw a bike at them during the protest.
