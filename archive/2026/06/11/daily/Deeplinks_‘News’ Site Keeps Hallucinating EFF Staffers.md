What do EFF staffers [Sarah Chen](https://news-usa.today/honoring-the-internet-legend-who-made-us-laugh-for-a-decade/), [Javier Morales](https://news-usa.today/alabama-family-pleads-for-return-of-son-missing-in-japan/), [Caitlin Chin](https://news-usa.today/burlington-police-track-cross-state-incident-with-flock-cameras/), [Emma Rodriguez](https://news-usa.today/top-computer-science-researcher-at-sam-houston-state-university-in-huntsville-tx/), and [Mikko Kopponen](https://news-usa.today/pierre-3d-model-wip-bringing-my-favorite-character-to-life-in-look-outside/) have in common?

For one thing, they don’t exist.

For another, all have been quoted as EFF experts in articles published in the past two months on a site called [News-USA Today](https://news-usa.today/), which [describes itself](https://news-usa.today/about-us/) as “an independent news publisher focused on clear, accurate, and useful journalism.”

Uh…

(Please don’t confuse this site with [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/), in which real EFF experts are accurately quoted on a regular basis.)
