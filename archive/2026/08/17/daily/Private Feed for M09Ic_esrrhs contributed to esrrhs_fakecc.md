## Summary

* Batch-port 118 self-contained tests from `gcc.c-torture/execute/`, bringing total GCC torture ports from 32 → 150
* Auto-conversion pipeline: download → filter (skip `#include`, `__builtin`, `_Complex`, `volatile`, K&R, varargs, extern calls) → convert (`abort()`→`return 1`, `exit(0)`→`return 0`) → verify (compile + run)
* All 499 e2e cases pass at both `-O0` and `-O1`
* 223 difftest cases agree with the gcc oracle
