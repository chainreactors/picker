# Harness Gardens

We love tending our harnesses, and I want to keep that even as the bitter lesson eats the scaffolding

October 8, 2026

[#ai](/archives/?tag=ai) [#lifeos](/archives/?tag=lifeos)

[**AIL***1*](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail "AIL 1 — Human Created, Minor AI Assistance")

 Ideal-stating…

[![A gardener tends flowers inside a hand-built wooden frame while a great purple tree grows up through it and far beyond](/images/harness-gardens.webp)](/images/harness-gardens.webp)

I feel like everyone's treating their [harnesses](/blog/the-answer-to-the-harness-question) kind of like a garden.

They're so different from each other. And so personal.

There are some problems with the analogy though, because while we're getting extreme pleasure from tending our harness gardens, they also have a purpose that can be done more or less efficiently.

I'd like for us to be able to keep this dynamic, though, even as the [bitter lesson](/blog/bitter-lesson-engineering) eats a lot of the work we've done with scaffolding.
