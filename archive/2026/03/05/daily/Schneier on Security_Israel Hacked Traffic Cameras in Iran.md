## Israel Hacked Traffic Cameras in Iran

[Multiple](https://www.timesofisrael.com/report-israel-hacked-tehran-traffic-cameras-to-track-khamenei-ahead-of-assassination/) [news](https://www.ft.com/content/bf998c69-ab46-4fa3-aae4-8f18f7387836) [outlets](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/iran-war-inside-plan-kill-ali-khamenei-5966861) are reporting on Israel’s hacking of Iranian traffic cameras and how they assisted with the killing of that country’s leadership.

*The New York Times* has an  [on the intelligence operation more generally.](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/03/01/us/politics/cia-israel-ayatollah-compound.html)
