## Exploitation was underway before patches landed, at least one victim reports ransomware demand

![icon](/design_picker/d518b499f8a6e2c65d4d8c49aca8299d54b03012/graphics/icon/vulture_red.svg)
[Carly Page](/Author/Carly-Page "Read more by this author")

Fri 1 May 2026  //
13:10 UTC

![](/design_picker/d2e337b97204af4aa34dda04c4e5d56d954b216f/graphics/icons/social_share_icon.svg)

CISA has added a critical cPanel bug to its known-exploited list, confirming that attackers are already poking holes in one of the internet's most widely used hosting stacks.

The vulnerability, tracked as [CVE-2026-41940](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-41940), carries a near-worst-case CVSS score of 9.8 and affects all supported versions of cPanel and Web[Host Manager (WHM) released after version 11.40, along with WP Squared, a WordPress management layer built on top of the same platform.

In plain terms, a successful exploit can hand over full control of the server.

[![](https://pubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/ad?co=1&iu=/6978/reg_security/cybercrime&sz=300x50%7C300x100%7C300x250%7C300x251%7C300x252%7C300x600%7C300x601&tile=2&c=2afWE8O-tTJnVO_JbyfcTUwAAARE&t=ct%3Dns%26unitnum%3D2%26raptor%3Dcondor%26pos%3Dtop%26test%3D0)](https://pubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/jump?co=1&iu=/6978/reg_security/cybercrime&sz=300x50%7C300x100%7C300x250%7C300x251%7C300x252%7C300x600%7C300x601&tile=2&c=2afWE8O-tTJnVO_JbyfcTUwAAARE&t=ct%3Dns%26unitnum%3D2%26raptor%3Dcondor%26pos%3Dtop%26test%3D0)

The US government's cybersecurity agency [added the flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Thursday](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/04/30/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog), confirming attackers are not waiting around. By the time cPanel shipped a [patch](https://www.theregister.com/2026/04/30/cpanel_whn_cves/) on Tuesday, exploitation was already underway.
