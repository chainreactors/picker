The Rhysida ransomware group listed Anne Arundel County, Maryland, on its leak site on 9 October 2026, claiming 814,500 files and 2.6 TB of data that it says include background-check dossiers, Department of Health records, medical-assistance applications, payroll and pension files and detention-centre intelligence. The county, which serves about 590,000 residents around Annapolis, has not responded publicly. It disclosed a separate ransomware intrusion in February 2025 and told residents the stolen data from that incident had been recovered, so the new listing raises the question of whether Rhysida is holding old material or has been back inside the network.

## Who is Rhysida?

Rhysida is a ransomware-as-a-service operation active since May 2023 that is best known for the British Library attack of October 2023 and for a string of hospitals, school districts and local governments. CISA and the FBI published a joint advisory on the group in November 2023, noting its use of external-facing remote services and phishing for initial access and its habit of auctioning stolen data for Bitcoin. The [Rhysida profile on the Ransomtracker](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/group/rhysida/) shows the group in decline by volume, with 44 listings so far in 2026 against 94 in 2025, but its targeting has not changed.
