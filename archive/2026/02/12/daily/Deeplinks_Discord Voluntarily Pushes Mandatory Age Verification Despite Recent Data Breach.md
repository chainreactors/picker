[Discord has begun rolling out mandatory age verification](https://discord.com/press-releases/discord-launches-teen-by-default-settings-globally) and the internet is, understandably, [freaking out](https://www.polygon.com/discord-face-id-requirement-backlash/).

At EFF, we’ve been raising the alarm about age verification mandates for years. In December, we launched our [Age Verification Resource Hub](https://www.eff.org/issues/age-verification) to push back against laws and platform policies that require users to hand over sensitive personal information just to access basic online services. At the time, age gates were largely enforced in polities where it was mandated by law. Now they’re landing [in platforms and jurisdictions where they’re not required](https://discord.com/press-releases/discord-launches-teen-by-default-settings-globally).
