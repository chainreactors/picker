## Friday Squid Blogging: Jurassic Fish Chokes on Squid

Here’s a [fossil](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/etimes/animals/how-a-jurassic-fish-choked-to-death-on-a-floating-squid-150-million-years-ago/articleshow/129823852.cms) of a 150-million year old fish that choked to death on a *belemnite rostrum*: the hard, internal shell of an extinct, squid-like animal.

Original [paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-00163-7).

As usual, you can also use this squid post to talk about the security stories in the news that I haven’t covered.
