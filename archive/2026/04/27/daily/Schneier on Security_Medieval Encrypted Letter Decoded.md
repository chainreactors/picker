@ Tom, ALL,

With regards,

> “I hope they crack the Voynich manuscript someday.”

The last I heard is that it’s assumed by some to be a “colour it in yourself” education book or folio (much like medical students still buy or get given today).

With the individual folios containing a “short-hand” recording of a lecture or equivalent…

Why short hand? Well the cost of calf skin processed into velum, for one good reason. It’s indicated that it would be 15-20 calf skin worths already. And although calf skin from young bulls was plentiful at the time due to slaughter practice in agriculture for milk and veal it was still quite an effort to turn it into usable vellum, thus costly. So any method to reduce cost would be considered and “short hand” would compress what actually gets written.

But beyond such speculation the “added in” illustrations apparently add no usable context.

Thus it is more of a curio than formal manuscript, thus may keep it’s secrets for the ages to come…
