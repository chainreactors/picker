Agents' emoji and symbols drew as tofu: the bundled faces (Inter, JetBrains Mono) have none, the system fallback can't use colour emoji fonts (Apple Color Emoji is skipped; Noto Color Emoji is bitmaps or COLRv1), and the atlas only drew outlines. Even Claude Code's own `⏺` and `⎿` were tofu, and so were the `⌕`, `⚙` and `⤢` icons in the fixture, theme editor and console goldens.

**Fonts** (all embedded, all credited in THIRD\_PARTY\_NOTICES):

| Font | For | Licence | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Twemoji (jdecked/twemoji v17.0.3, built to COLRv0) | colour emoji, up to Emoji 17 | MIT (code) + CC BY 4.0 (art) | 1.8 MB |
| Noto Emoji | monochrome emoji, the fallback for anything Twemoji lacks | OFL 1.1 | 2.0 MB |
| Noto Sans Symbols 2 | symbols: ✔ ✘ ★ ☐ ⏵ ✻ ✢ ⌘ ⏎… | OFL 1.1 | 1.2 MB |
| Noto Sans Symbols | technical symbols: ⎿ and its block | OFL 1.1 | 0.4 MB |

* **Licences.** OFL 1.1 is the licence of the fonts Oriel already ships.
