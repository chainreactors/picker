**DEF CON will always be a cash-at-the-door conference. We will always honor the LineCon. This is the way.**

**Preregistration guarantees you'll receive a DEF CON 34 Human badge (as opposed to a paper badge, if we run out) when redeeming your ticket in Las Vegas. Badges must be picked up onsite.**

**The fine print**
DEF CON 34 preregistration ends July 31st at 8:59pm EST. Onsite registration will begin Thursday August 6, 2026.

**NOTE: ANYONE MAY REDEEM THE TICKET**

**Ticket Pricing is as Follows:**

Early-        **$560**           Early price ends May 22 @ 8:59pm EST                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              Regular-    **$580**          Regular price ends July 17 @ 8:59pm EST                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           Late-         **$600**           Late price ends July 31 @ 8:59pm EST

The fee to cancel/refund a ticket before July 18, 2026, is $34.00.
The fee to cancel/refund after July 18, 2026, is $84.00.
For ticket or billing questions, simply email us.

Upon purchase, the ticket will be emailed to the email address provided. PLEASE MAKE SURE YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS IS CORRECT. We cannot change it after the ticket is emailed.

By purchasing this ticket, you agree to abide by the DEF CON Code of Conduct and the registration terms and conditions listed above.
