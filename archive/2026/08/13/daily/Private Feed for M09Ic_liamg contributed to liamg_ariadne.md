## What changed

Attack pressure on the enemy king zone. One term, no other eval changes.

* 64-entry king zone table. The king square is clamped to files B-G and ranks 2-7 before the 3x3 box is built, so every zone is exactly 9 squares. Without the clamp a castled king on g1 gets a 6-square zone omitting f3/g3/h3 - precisely where a kingside attack develops - and the engine could lower its own danger score by playing Kh1.
* Per-piece attack weights (N/B 2, R 3, Q 5) multiplied by the number of zone squares attacked, accumulated in the existing mobility loop from the attack bitboard already computed there.
* Gated at 2+ attackers, quadratic danger curve, capped at 500cp, midgame only.
