## Summary

* **Enable `BBCongestion` by default**:
  + Pingtunnel previously lacked congestion control on `FrameMgr`, causing large downloads to dump packets at maximum window size, triggering packet loss, bufferbloat, and ICMP flood throttling.
  + Added `-congestion` flag (default `"bb"`, matching `spp`'s `ricmp` implementation) and wired up `BBCongestion` to `FrameMgr` across server and client.
  + Supported `congestion` field in config files (`config.go`).
