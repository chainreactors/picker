## About the Book

**Mobile Digital Forensics** is an English-language technical reference organized into **10 parts and 40 chapters**, supported by practical laboratories, case studies, and technical appendices.

Key subjects include:

* Digital evidence principles, forensic integrity, and chain of custody.
* Android and iOS/iPadOS architecture, encryption, and security controls.
* Logical, file-system, and advanced mobile evidence acquisition.
* Application databases, native logs, user activity, and timeline reconstruction.
* Mobile cloud services, communication artifacts, geolocation, and network evidence.
* Mobile malware, memory analysis, anti-forensics, and evidence correlation.
* Tool validation, reporting, legal considerations, and emerging challenges.
