# Adam reflects on BSides SF and RSAC

Adam finally caught his breath and sat down to reflect on BSides SF and RSAC 2026.
![Conference attendees talk security in a sunny auditorium in San Francisco.](/images/blog/img/2026/Gemini_Generated_Image_SanFrancisco-sunny-conference-1000w.png)

## BSides SF

My BSides passed in a whirl and was heavy on collaborations, including potential future partnerships. Before the main events started, I had time for a long lunch with [Bob Lord](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lordbob/) talking about lessons learned and near misses and secure-by-design story cards, then a great partner dinner with [Stanley Harris](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stanley-harris-56256b2b/) and [Dustin Lehr](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dustinlehr/) of [Katilyst](https://www.katilyst.com/).

## RSA 2026

Monday morning [Adrian Sanabria](https://www.linkedin.com/in/adrian-sanabria/) and I kicked off RSA with [“A Breach is a Terrible Thing to Waste”](https://shostack.org/blog/wasting-failures-rsac-2026/) delivered to a full audience who raised several engaging questions at the end. ([The video is posted](https://path.rsaconference.com/flow/rsac/us26/FullAgenda/page/catalog/session/1756101254392001bKZA), note that RSAC requires log in to view.) Afterwards we got to chat with [Alex Pinto](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexcpsec/) of the [Verizon DBIR (Data Breach Investigations Report) team](https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/reports/dbir/) about data driven risk, a topic near and dear to our hearts.

![Adrian and Adam in mid-talk](/images/blog/img/2026/mid-talk-350w.jpeg)
![Adrian, Alex, Kymberlee and Adam](/images/blog/img/2026/adam-adrian-alex-kym-325w.jpeg)

On Tuesday, I attended [Threat Modeling Connect](https://threat-model-connect.webflow.io/)’s RSAC 2026 Meetup “Tacos and Threats”, seeing the team and joining a panel alongside [Apurva Mohan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/secureproducts/), [Stephen de Vries](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stephen-de-vries-4185a8/) and [Sebastien Deleersnyder](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sebadele/). The panel discussed AI's impact on threat modeling, including if AI will replace threat modeling (it won’t) and how to threat model AI systems. [Angelika Szymanowska](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sebadele/) got us together for a pre-panel photo. We also had a brief chance to bring together [Jamie Dicken](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jdicken/), Kymberlee Price, [Michael Novack](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michael-novack/) and [Shoshana Cox](https://www.linkedin.com/in/disesdi/).

![TMC RSAC Meetup panel in action](/images/blog/img/2026/taco-panel-light-350w.jpeg)
![Members of the TMC RSAC Meetup panel with Angelika](/images/blog/img/2026/taco-panel-selfie-300w.jpeg)

Also on Tuesday, one of my week's highlights is the RSA Scholar’s dinner, for students who won a scholarship to attend RSAC. It’s organized by [Cecilia Murtagh Marinier](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cecilia-murtagh-marinier-14967/) and is a great opportunity to meet some of the next generation of cybersecurity practitioners, as well as see industry experts such as [Alya Franklin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alyajehan/), [Bruce Schneier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Schneier), [Bryson Bort](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brysonbort/), [Paul Kocher](https://www.linkedin.com/in/paulkocher/), and [Whit Diffie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitfield_Diffie).

![Adrian and Bryson](/images/blog/img/2026/adam-bryson-250w.jpeg)
![Group phot of RSA Scholar's dinner](/images/blog/img/2026/student-dinner-350w.jpeg)

## The Hallway Track

As Vandana Verma pointed out in her [Day 1 RSA post](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vandana-verma/): *the real conference happens between the talks*. That held true all week, and I was delighted to meet up with [Max Imbiel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/max-i-186b2252/), [Robin Basham](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robinbasham), [Jay Healy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jasonhealey), [Diana Kelley](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dianakelleysecuritycurve/), [Brian Silverstein](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brsilverstein/), [Tanya Janca](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tanya-janca/), [Olivier Bilodeau](https://www.linkedin.com/in/olivierbilodeau/), and [Vandana](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vandana-verma/). [Geng Yang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gengyang/) [put it well on Mastodon](https://infosec.exchange/%40geng/116294094782740180): *the pioneers who built the security practices we rely on every day are still here, still in the hallways, still in the conversation*. (Though I can’t help but add that many are not. Recent losses of [Parmaster](https://professorsigmund.com/field-notes/in-memoriam-parmaster.html), [FX](https://blog.recurity-labs.com/2026-03-02/Farewell_Felix), and more still hurt.)
