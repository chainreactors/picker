# The Incendiary Bomb Never Seen in Israel Before

March 7, 2026

* [Conflict and Human Rights Team](/tag/conflict-and-human-rights-team)
* [Iran](/tag/iran)
* [Israel](/tag/israel)

The Israeli Air Force (IAF) has dropped 5,000 bombs on Iran since the United States and Israel launched an attack last week, according to a [statement](https://x.com/IAFsite/status/2029206434785710264) by the IAF on March 4.

Bellingcat has [monitored weapons](https://www.bellingcat.com/news/2026/03/03/bombs-will-fall-everywhere-the-american-israeli-and-iranian-weapons-being-deployed-in-middle-east/) used in the first few days of the war, and strikes across the region, including those that [caused civilian harm](https://bsky.app/profile/bellingcat.com/post/3mfwwf6v4oc23). Some weapons, such as the US [Precision Strike Missile](https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2029219939102401017?s=20), have seen their first use in combat. A [variant of the Tomahawk missile](https://www.twz.com/sea/black-tomahawk-cruise-missile-seen-for-the-first-time-in-strikes-on-iran), previously unknown to the public, was also used.

On March 3, the IAF posted three images in [three](https://www.instagram.com/p/DVZgskYDaP7/) [separate](https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=1335078251985229) [posts](https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=1334940231999031) showing a bomb not publicly seen in Israeli service before. The Israel Air Force released these photos accompanied with claims they were of jets participating in the strikes on Iran. Experts told Bellingcat that this bomb appears to have an incendiary component, and may be one intended to destroy chemical or biological warfare agents.

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/03/IAF-IG-post-959x1200.jpg)

*Photo of an Israeli Air Force jet purportedly participating in strikes, equipped with two of these bombs (far left and far right). Source:* [*Israeli Air Force*](https://www.instagram.com/p/DVZgskYDaP7/)*.*

The images appear to show 2,000-pound-class air-delivered bombs fitted with Joint Direct Attack Munition (JDAM) guidance kit with a red band around the nose. Red is commonly used to [denote an incendiary](https://www.bulletpicker.com/pdf/Interpreting-Ammunition-Markings-and-Color-Codes.pdf#page=11), while yellow indicates high explosive effect.

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/03/bomb-cropped.jpg)

*Image of a bomb with the body of a MK 84 2,000-pound-bomb, but with a red band near the nose, and a US JDAM guidance kit. The image is cropped by Bellingcat to focus on the bomb. Source:* [*Israeli Air Force*](https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=1334940231999031)*.*

We identified key details about the munition and shared the images with two weapons experts.

## Apparent Similarities to the MK 84

Dr N.R. Jenzen-Jones, the director of Armament Research Services (ARES), a weapons intelligence consulting company, told Bellingcat these images show a 2,000-pound-class air-delivered bomb fitted with a Joint Direct Attack Munition (JDAM) guidance kit.

Frederic Gras, an Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) expert, also told Bellingcat that the bomb could be of the US MK 80 series, or an Israeli copy, and has a JDAM guidance kit.

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/03/pair-1200x546.jpg)

*Left: 2,000-pound bomb with red band and US JDAM guidance kit posted by the IAF. Right: Standard MK 84 2,000-pound bombs with US JDAM guidance kits. Sources:* [*IAF*](https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=1334940231999031) *and* [*SrA Karalyn Degraffenreed/DVIDS*](https://www.dvidshub.net/image/9462271/us-unleashes-massive-strike-against-isis-syria)*.*

The [US JDAM bomb guidance kit](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104572/joint-direct-attack-munition-gbu-313238/) is designed for use with bombs that use the MK 80 series bomb bodies, and the closely related BLU-109 “bunker buster” body.

The Open Source Munitions Portal added the munition to their website on March 3, describing it as “visually similar to a MK 84 general purpose aerial bomb”, while noting that “the marking scheme is distinctly different”. The War Zone also [reported](https://www.twz.com/uncategorized/israeli-f-16-appears-with-mysterious-jdam-precision-bomb) on these distinct markings, and possible munitions it could be.

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/03/osmp-1200x785.jpg)

*Open Source Munitions Portal’s (OSMP) entry on the bomb, with an analyst note. The OSMP is jointly run by Airwars and ARES, and entries* [*undergo a review*](https://osmp.ngo/about/) *by at least two experts. Source:* [*Open Source Munitions Portal*](https://osmp.ngo/osmp2051/)*.*

“The combination of yellow and red bands probably indicates both a high explosive and incendiary payload, which would be consistent with a 2,000-pound-class bomb of MK 84 form factor known as the BLU-119/B Crash Prompt Agent Defeat (CrashPAD),” Dr Jenzen-Jones told Bellingcat.

Frederic Gras, an Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) expert said that the US and Israel both use red markings to indicate an incendiary payload, or effect. The bomb could be a full incendiary payload, with the yellow band indicating a bursting charge, or it could be a bomb primarily with a high explosive component, and a secondary incendiary effect, Gras added.

## Red Bands on Israeli Weapons

It’s not the first time the Israeli Air Forces has published weapon images with red bands marking the warhead or payload section of a munition. Shortly after the start of the Gaza War in 2023, the [IAF posted a photo](https://www.twz.com/israeli-ah-64-apache-appears-with-mysterious-hellfire-missile) which included an Apache attack helicopter with a Hellfire missile with a red band. The IAF deleted the post and replaced it with a [similar photo of an Apache](https://x.com/IAFsite/status/1721177843986542835) without this missile.

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/03/helicopter-1200x675.jpg)

*Israeli Air Force AH-64 Apache with Hellfire missiles, including one with a red band. Source:* [*Israeli Air Force*](https://www.twz.com/israeli-ah-64-apache-appears-with-mysterious-hellfire-missile)*.*
