# Woflow Data Breach

## What Happened

In March 2026, the AI-driven merchant data platform [Woflow was named as a victim by the ShinyHunters data extortion group](https://cybernews.com/security/shinyhunters-claims-woflow-data-breach/). The group subsequently published tens of thousands of files allegedly obtained from the company, comprising more than 2TB of data. The trove included hundreds of thousands of email addresses, names, phone numbers and physical addresses, with the data indicating it related to Woflow customers and, in turn, the customers of merchants using their platform.
