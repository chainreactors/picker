# [New Wave of Refrigeration Cyberattacks Hits Supermarkets Across Europe, Forcing Stores to Pull Food](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/second-wave-of-cyberattacks-hits.html)

**Swati Khandelwal**Nov 03, 2026Cyberattack / Industrial Security

[![](data:image/png;base64...)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEjScxShEa3bsAhGLHlqkBcsyuRe6UeQwrOVXBFP3TwxzmXtd03vDeDRZHxxBLIKHDYgY5p5CsrFvBwuozg3UsRLtVMO758NhVwSju6D6WgO1J42wJd2NWR9aVzz-B_CWfiMFH2QANUrAnUXbgkKec2KtJjULvN3NTqrCPbLRNnxlnvBFIAkndClXJMIeXY/s1700-nu-rw-lo-l85-e365/fridge-hack.jpg)

Attackers remotely changed the settings on refrigeration controls at supermarkets in Belgium, Sweden, Estonia and other European countries overnight into Monday, November 2, warming fridges and freezers.

No chain reported more than several dozen affected stores, and some had to throw away chilled and frozen food or take it off the shelf. Danfoss, a Danish maker of refrigeration controls, [said its products were involved](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/11/02/341078.htm), as they were three weeks earlier when [attackers hit hundreds of French supermarkets](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attackers-heat-french-supermarket.html).

No one had claimed the attacks by Monday night, and it was not known whether they were linked to the attack in France.

Refrigeration controls are computers that run a store's fridges and freezers. They can be connected to the internet, allowing refrigeration contractors to monitor them and fix faults remotely.

In Belgium, attackers switched cooling units into defrost mode, Stephan Moelans, chief executive of the refrigeration contractor Sabcobel, [told Belgian media](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/liveblog/federaal-parket-opent-strafonderzoek-na-cyberaanval-motief-van~1793610043244/). Defrost mode is meant to clear ice, but in these attacks it made the units heat up instead of keeping food cold. Sabcobel manages refrigeration systems for the Delhaize supermarket chain.

In other cases, the temperature setting was raised. [An ICA store in Sjöbo](https://apnews.com/article/europe-supermarkets-freezers-cyberattacks-a8ea5b30d67da47957769f29101fe686), in southern Sweden, said its fridges and freezers had been set to 50°C and that staff were throwing out the affected goods.

At a [Selver supermarket in Estonia](https://news.err.ee/1610183296/hackers-raised-freezer-temperature-at-selver-supermarket-to-50-c), a freezer was set to 50°C, said Jann Mutso, chief executive of the refrigeration supplier Kitman Coldtech. Its temperature rose by only about 2-3°C, and the problem was resolved within about 10 minutes, with no products damaged.

[![Cybersecurity](data:image/png;base64...)](https://thehackernews.uk/ai-governance-platform-d)

Danfoss's [security advisories page](https://www.danfoss.com/en/service-and-support/coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure/danfoss-security-advisories/) listed no advisory about the attacks when The Hacker News checked on November 3. The most detailed steps for operators are still those in an [October alert from CERT-FR](https://cert.cyber.gouv.fr/alerte/CERTFR-2026-ALE-012/), part of France's national cybersecurity agency.

### Which Companies Say They Were Hit

Each company described the incident in its own words, and not all of them called it a cyberattack.

| Country | Company | Stores affected | What the company or its supplier said |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Belgium, Luxembourg | Delhaize | Several dozen, mostly in Belgium and a few in Luxembourg | An "IT incident" affected refrigeration. The range of fresh and frozen food was temporarily limited. |
| Belgium | Carrefour | 15 of more than 700 | A cyberattack on its refrigeration, with very limited impact. |
| Belgium | Intermarché | 10 to 15 | Problems with its fridges. |
| Belgium | Spar, under Colruyt Group | 5, run by independent owners | Their fridge systems were attacked. |
| Belgium | IKEA | 1, in Mons | A cyber incident hit the store's fridges and freezers. No other IKEA store in Belgium was affected. |
| Sweden | ICA | A limited number. No count given | An incident affected refrigeration and freezer systems. ICA did not confirm a cyberattack. |
| Sweden | Coop | 6 (to TT and AFP) or 7 (to Reuters and Bloomberg), of about 800 | A cyber incident in which someone tampered with its refrigeration without permission. |
| Ireland | Musgrave, owner of SuperValu | 13 of 223 | A "cyber incident" limited to one refrigeration model. |
| Estonia | Selver, through supplier Kitman Coldtech | 1 | An attack on an older Danfoss system. |
| Netherlands | Businesses not named | At least 17 businesses, including butchers and small local shops | Attacked, according to Danfoss. |
| Finland | Kesko | A few smaller stores | Unusual temperatures. The cause was under investigation. The stores' equipment is outside Kesko's central protections. |
| Portugal | Intermarché, Mosqueteiros group | 7 | A cyberattack that appears to have targeted an outside company managing the refrigeration, according to AFP. |
| Italy | Coop supermarkets run by Radenza Group | At least 4, in Sicily | An attack on its remote control system for frozen food sections, according to AFP. |

At a De Laet & Van Haver butcher shop in Hove, south of Antwerp, the fridges were turned up as high as 18°C, owner Luc De Laet [told Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/temperature-soars-swedish-grocery-store-freezers-one-store-blames-hackers-2026-11-02/). He estimated that about €15,000 worth of meat was ruined.

At the 13 SuperValu stores, Musgrave [took chilled and frozen products off sale as a precaution](https://www.irishtimes.com/technology/data-security/2026/11/02/supervalu-removes-frozen-and-chilled-food-from-13-stores-after-cyber-attack-on-fridges/). The company [said on Monday afternoon](https://apnews.com/article/europe-supermarkets-freezers-cyberattacks-a8ea5b30d67da47957769f29101fe686) that the incident had been resolved and restocking was under way.
