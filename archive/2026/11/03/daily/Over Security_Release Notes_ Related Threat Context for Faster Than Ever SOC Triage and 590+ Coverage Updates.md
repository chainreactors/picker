Release Notes: Related Threat Context for Faster Than Ever SOC Triage and 590+ Coverage Updates

Security investigations often begin with a single suspicious IOC, but deciding what it means requires context. The more relevant intelligence analysts can connect to that finding, the better they can determine what deserves attention and what to do next.

**October’s release introduces a new powerful capability in ANY.RUN’s** [**Interactive Sandbox**](https://any.run/features/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=october-release-notes-2026&utm_term=031126&utm_content=linktosandboxlanding)– **Related Threat Context. Now SOCs and MSSPs can save more time by uncovering relevant IOC connections and unlocking investigative insight with less effort.** By bringing relevant intelligence directly into the workflow, analysts can easily assess their findings and decide how to respond without switching between multiple sources.

The release also expands threat coverage and detection capabilities, improving visibility into emerging threats and malicious activity. Together, these updates help SOC and MSSP teams investigate more efficiently, reduce manual effort, and make better-informed decisions with greater confidence.

## Product Updates

This month’s product updates help analysts connect individual indicators with the context needed to decide what to do next. Related Threat Context surfaces relevant intelligence directly in the [Interactive Sandbox](https://any.run/features/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=october-release-notes-2026&utm_term=031126&utm_content=linktosandboxlanding), while [Threat Intelligence Lookup](https://any.run/threat-intelligence-lookup/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=october-release-notes-2026&utm_term=031126&utm_content=linktotilookuplanding) helps explore related infrastructure, observables, and threat activity.

## Related Threat Context: A Major Upgrade That Makes Every SOC Investigation Faster and Smarter

An IOC is only the starting point. To decide what to do next, analysts need to know whether it has appeared in previous investigations, is associated with activity in their industry, or connects to known threats.

**With the introduction of a major new update for SOCs and MSSPs** – **Related Threat Context, ANY.RUN’s** [**Interactive Sandbox**](https://any.run/features/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=october-release-notes-2026&utm_term=031126&utm_content=linktosandboxlanding) **takes threat investigations a step further.**

For the first time, analysts can discover relevant connections to an IOC directly within their analysis workflow, bringing previously scattered intelligence closer to the moment decisions need to be made.
