# Creativity vs. Consumption

The Single Biggest Metric for Managing Your Happiness

August 11, 2026

by Daniel Miessler

[#philosophy](/archives/?tag=philosophy) [#creativity](/archives/?tag=creativity) [#society](/archives/?tag=society)

 Stoic-enduring…

[![Charcoal sketch of a seated figure whose left arm dissolves into a cascade of purple glowing phones while their right hand builds a warm wooden tower](/images/creativity-vs-consumption-header.webp)](/images/creativity-vs-consumption-header.webp)

If there's one metric to watch for managing your happiness level, it's Creativity vs Consumption.

If you feel completely empty and like nothing matters, ask yourself how much time you have spent [watching what other people are doing](/blog/spend-no-5-time-looking-people) or watching shortform video or YouTube or TV.

And compare that to how much time you have spent [building something](/blog/survive-ai-become-creators), or improving the life of someone else.

It's not the only metric that matters, but I would argue it's the biggest one.
