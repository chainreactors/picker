*This is the second post in a series on 3D print blocking, for the first entry check out: [Print Blocking is Anti-Consumer - Permission to Print Part 1](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/04/print-blocking-anti-consumer-permission-print-part-1)*

[Legislators](https://www.news10.com/capitol/3d-ghost-gun-printer-policy/) [across the U.S.](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/proposals-take-aim-at-3d-printing-tech-to-strengthen-washington-state-laws-against-ghost-guns/) are [proposing](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/california-targets-3d-printers-in-new-bill-as-states-diverge-on-gun-control-249507/) [laws](https://coloradosun.com/2026/03/27/colorado-3d-gun-bill-amended-jared-polis-veto/) to force “print blockers” on 3D printers sold in their states. This mandated censorware is doomed to fail for its intended purpose, but will still manage to hurt the professional and hobbyist communities relying on these tools.

3D printers are commonly used to [repair belongings](https://blog.prusa3d.com/replacement-parts-contest-winners_84174/), [decorate homes](https://blog.prusa3d.com/winter-holidays-decorations-2025-winners_128571/), [print figurines](https://www.reddit.com/r/PrintedWarhammer/), and so [much](https://www.reddit.com/r/functionalprint/) [more](https://www.reddit.com/r/3Dprinting/).
