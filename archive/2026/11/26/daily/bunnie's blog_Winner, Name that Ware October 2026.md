## Winner, Name that Ware October 2026

The ware for October 2026 is the control board from a SodaStream drinking water carbonation machine. It was really interesting to see people puzzle over the nature of the board – I would say I’d have a similarly difficult time guessing this. On the flip side, I also never would have suspected that the operating principle of a carbonation controller would be sensing the vibration signature of the gas moving through various valves and releases.

In retrospect, it does make sense – the sound the machine makes is unique and repeatable – so it would have a signature one could discriminate and trigger on. Accelerometers have several hundred hertz (if not more) sensing bandwidth – adequate for the task – and they are a low-cost, mass-produced commodity, thanks in part to smart phones anchoring their demand. Meanwhile, a microphone would pick up too much ambient noise and could miss signatures if operated in a noisy room. All in all, it’s a pretty clever solution to the problem, and the sort of trick learned by taking things apart. One more for the pile of tricks in my head!

[![](https://bunniefoo.com/ntw/ntw_oct_2026_context_sm.jpg)](https://bunniefoo.com/ntw/ntw_oct_2026_context.jpg)

Above is a photo that gives some more context to the overall solution inside the SodaStream. Thanks again to FETguy and [Renew Computers](https://renewcomputers.com/) for sharing this ware! There were many great attempts at guessing this one, but none were quite spot-on, so there is no prize awarded this month.
