# Speaking Freely: Benjamin Ismail

Interviewer: [Jillian York](https://www.eff.org/about/staff/jillian-york)

*Benjamin Ismail is the Campaign and Advocacy Director for* [*GreatFire*](https://en.greatfire.org/)*, where he leads efforts to expose the censorship apparatus of authoritarian regimes worldwide. He also runs/oversees the App Censorship Project, including the AppleCensorship.com and GoogleCensorship.org platforms, which track mobile app censorship globally. From 2011 to 2017, Benjamin headed the Asia-Pacific desk at* [*Reporters Without Borders*](https://rsf.org/en) *(RSF).*

I'm going to start by asking you my first basic question: What does free speech or free expression mean to you?
