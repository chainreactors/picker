# A curl audit by Aisle

[October 14, 2026](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/10/14/a-curl-audit-by-aisle/) [Daniel Stenberg](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/author/daniel/) [Leave a comment](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/10/14/a-curl-audit-by-aisle/#respond)

Over the last year or so, multiple engineers at [Aisle](https://aisle.com/) have spent a lot of time and energy while pointing their AI powered tooling at the curl source code. This has resulted in no less than *thirty-five* published curl vulnerabilities and CVE Ids.

Today, we make Aisle’s [audit report available on the curl website](https://curl.se/docs/audits.html). Yet another audit of curl source code that together with all the other work we do, help keeping the products safe, secure and top quality. Few other software projects have had the same amount and level of scrutiny and verification as curl has gotten over the last few years.

In the curl project we have nothing but positive experiences from having cooperated closely with Aisle all this time. Aisle has found more CVEs in curl than any other group or individual ever did before, and Aisle employees have authored several dozens of commits in curl already.

*With Aisle, we have a cooperation like with no other security organization before. Their vast amount of detailed and accurate findings backed up by a team of skilled humans have made it possible for us to identify and fix more vulnerabilities in curl at a higher rate than ever before, leading to the billions of mostly indirect daily curl users to be more secure.*

Aisle blogged about this: [One AISLE curl Audit Produced 24 CVEs. 18 Ship Today](https://aisle.com/blog/one-aisle-curl-audit-produced-24-cves-18-ship-today).

## Audits

This concludes the *5th* external official audit of curl over several years. Audits help us to prove quality for outsiders to some extent. They also help us ensure there aren’t any *blind spots* in the code or in our development process.

The curl source code is continuously, every day of the week, being scanned and probed by countless humans and AIs trying to find vulnerabilities, but we can’t say for sure how many nor who they are.

We are also scanning and testing curl ourselves with all the best tools and harnesses we have access to, so that we can catch mistakes as early as possible, ideally before they are ever shipped in a release.
