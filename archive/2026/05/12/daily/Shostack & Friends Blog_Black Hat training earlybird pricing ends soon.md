# Black Hat training earlybird pricing ends soon

All about the upcoming Threat Modeling Intensive with Complete AI at Black Hat and why you should be the early bird
![A cute and robotic early bird rounding up worms](/images/blog/img/2026/bird-robot-early-bird-1000w.webp)

Earlybird pricing for [Threat Modeling Intensive with Complete AI at Black Hat](https://blackhat.com/us-26/training/schedule/index.html#adam-shostacks-threat-modeling-intensive-with-complete-ai-51473) ends **May 22**. That's the deadline. This is a good example of a threat you don't actually need to model very carefully: the likelihood of the price going up after May 22 is not uncertain. It's just a date.

If you'd like to get a sense of the course, watch the video below!

This new course covers three things that belong together: the core of intensive threat modeling, effectively using LLMs, and threat modeling LLMs. The first half is our industry-leading Threat Modeling Intensive course. It's hands-on and practical threat modeling built around the Four Questions. Once we've got that baseline, we shift to using LLMs to help you threat model. Using LLMs requires understanding how they're built, where their outputs are reliable, and where they aren't. Day three applies that to delivering real threat modeling work with LLMs. Day four covers threats to LLMs themselves and digs into Google SAIF, NIST's AI/ML and RMF, the Berryville Architectural Risk Analyses, and more.

The course also includes office hours in August, after the Black Hat energy has faded and the work has started to land back home, and access to recorded lectures through the end of the year.

If missing the May 22 deadline is *what could go wrong*, the *what are we going to do about it* is straightforward: [sign up now!](https://blackhat.com/us-26/training/schedule/index.html#adam-shostacks-threat-modeling-intensive-with-complete-ai-51473)
