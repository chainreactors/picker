## About

A personal VM cloud in one Go binary: persistent Linux VMs on macOS, Linux and Windows, AI coding agents working inside them, any port published to HTTPS through Cloudflare Tunnel. Driven from a Mac OS 9 desktop in the browser, over SSH, or by an agent.
