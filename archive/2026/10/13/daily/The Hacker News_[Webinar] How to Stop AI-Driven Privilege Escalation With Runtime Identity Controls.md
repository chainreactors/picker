# [[Webinar] How to Stop AI-Driven Privilege Escalation With Runtime Identity Controls](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/webinar-how-to-stop-ai-driven-privilege.html)

**The Hacker News**Oct 13, 2026Artificial Intelligence / Identity Security

[![](data:image/png;base64...)](https://thehacker.news/runtime-identity-security)

The dangerous part of a stolen identity isn't always the initial login. It's what an attacker can do with that access next.

With AI accelerating reconnaissance, exploitation of vulnerabilities, and privilege escalation, attackers can move through systems faster than security teams can investigate. A compromised account that looks legitimate at first could become the starting point for a much larger attack.

And that's where traditional identity security faces a serious problem: Checking who gets access isn't enough if you can't control what happens after.

**[See How to Stop AI-Powered Identity Attacks — Register for the Webinar](https://thehacker.news/runtime-identity-security)**

### Why Traditional Identity Controls Are Falling Behind

Many identity security controls focus on authentication, permissions, and access policies. But what happens when a trusted identity is compromised, its behavior changes, or an attacker begins exploiting already granted privileges?

That gap becomes more dangerous as AI-powered attacks accelerate.

Attackers can automate parts of the attack chain while defenders are still collecting alerts, investigating activity, and deciding how to respond.

The answer isn't simply detecting attacks faster. It's the ability to evaluate and control access in real time while an identity is actively being used.

This is the idea behind runtime identity security.

In this upcoming expert-led webinar, you'll learn:

* **Where attackers find identity gaps** across cloud, on-premises, SaaS, and AI environments.
* **Why AI-powered attacks challenge traditional defenses** and reduce the time available to respond.
* **How runtime identity security works** to evaluate access continuously and enforce security decisions when they matter.
* **How to prepare your defenses** for AI-driven attacks that move at machine speed.

### The Next Identity Attack May Not Give You Time to React

If attackers can compromise identities and escalate privileges in minutes, discovering suspicious activity after the fact may already be too late.

Security teams need to know not just who has access, but whether that access should still be trusted right now.

This session explores how to close that gap and build an identity security strategy designed to keep pace with the speed of modern attacks.
