## Kids profited from tools used to attack popular websites, say officials

![icon](/design_picker/d518b499f8a6e2c65d4d8c49aca8299d54b03012/graphics/icon/vulture_red.svg)
[Connor Jones](/Author/Connor-Jones "Read more by this author")

Tue 10 Mar 2026  //
11:41 UTC

![](/design_picker/d2e337b97204af4aa34dda04c4e5d56d954b216f/graphics/icons/social_share_icon.svg)

Polish police have referred seven suspected juvenile cybercriminals to family court over an alleged scheme to flog DDoS kits online.

The youths, aged between 12 and 16 at the time of the alleged offenses, all face charges related to selling DDoS tools in what police described as a purely profit-driven scheme.

Poland's Central Bureau for Combating Cybercrime (CBZC) said the tools they sold online were linked to various attacks on organizations.

[![](https://pubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/ad?co=1&iu=/6978/reg_security/cybercrime&sz=300x50%7C300x100%7C300x250%7C300x251%7C300x252%7C300x600%7C300x601&tile=2&c=2abDqExfbv8-cWtOI9c8fnAAAAJc&t=ct%3Dns%26unitnum%3D2%26raptor%3Dcondor%26pos%3Dtop%26test%3D0)](https://pubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/jump?co=1&iu=/6978/reg_security/cybercrime&sz=300x50%7C300x100%7C300x250%7C300x251%7C300x252%7C300x600%7C300x601&tile=2&c=2abDqExfbv8-cWtOI9c8fnAAAAJc&t=ct%3Dns%26unitnum%3D2%26raptor%3Dcondor%26pos%3Dtop%26test%3D0)

"Using the tools they administer, popular websites such as auction and sales portals, IT domains, hosting services, and accommodation booking services were attacked," the force said in an [announcement](https://cbzc.policja.gov.pl/bzc/aktualnosci/849%2CSiedmiu-nastolatkow-sprzedawalo-narzedzia-do-atakow-DDoS.html) (machine translated) on Tuesday.
