# Win11\_26H2 build xta phantom libraries

Posted on [2026-09-14](https://www.hexacorn.com/blog/2026/09/14/win11_26h2-build-xta-phantom-libraries/ "7:11 pm")  by  [adam](https://www.hexacorn.com/blog/author/adam/ "View all posts by adam")

In my older post I covered Procmon findings for [Win11\_24H2](https://www.hexacorn.com/blog/2024/11/05/procmonning-the-win11_24h2-build/) build. The 26H2 introduces 2 additional phantom DLL libraries:

* C:\Windows\System32\xtajitse.dll
* C:\Windows\System32\xtajitf.dll
