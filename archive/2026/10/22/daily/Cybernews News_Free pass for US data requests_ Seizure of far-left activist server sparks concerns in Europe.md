Key takeaways

1. 01

   Dutch police seized an Autistici/Inventati server in Amsterdam after a US request under a legal assistance treaty.
2. 02

   Dutch cloud providers warn the seizure lacked visible judicial oversight and could weaken safeguards for European data.
3. 03

   Prosecutors say the seized data has not yet been shared with US authorities.
4. 04

   The US terrorist designation pushed Autistici/Inventati to lose banking, payments, and its website before shutting down services.
