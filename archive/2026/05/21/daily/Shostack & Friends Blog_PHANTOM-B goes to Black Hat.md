# PHANTOM-B goes to Black Hat

A busy Black Hat: A new talk, a new practical tool, and a deadline you should know about
![a robot viewing symbols on a whiteboard](/images/blog/img/2026/phantom-b-bh-1000w.png)
Black Hat is shaping up to be a busy one for anyone working at the intersection of threat modeling and LLMs. That includes Shostack + Associates.

## The Talk: Threat Modeling LLMs: The PHANTOM-B Model

This one has been a long time in the making. If you've been following the work here this year, you've seen the pieces coming together, like the AI Systems course with Michael Novack and Shoshana Cox, and the ongoing writing about what it actually means to threat model probabilistic systems. The [PHANTOM-B talk at Black Hat](https://blackhat.com/us-26/briefings/schedule/index.html#threat-modeling-llms-the-phantom-b-model-53809) is where a significant piece of that work lands publicly.

The problem it addresses is one we hear constantly: existing resources for threat modeling are built for thoroughness, not speed. PHANTOM-B is a practical and validated threat modeling tool designed for LLM products. It's grounded in cognitive psychology research on how experts actually chunk and scaffold information and scoped to real deployment timelines.

The talk is 40 minutes, in the Application Security: Defense track. We'll share more details closer to the date.

## Before you go: your last chance for Black Hat training early bird pricing

If you're heading to Black Hat and haven't registered for Threat Modeling Intensive with Complete AI, **tomorrow is the last day at early bird pricing**. Four days covering the core Threat Modeling Intensive, threat modeling LLMs, and using LLMs to deliver real threat modeling work. The price goes up after May 22. [Register today!](https://blackhat.com/us-26/training/schedule/index.html#adam-shostacks-threat-modeling-intensive-with-complete-ai-51473)
