Poor accountability, feeble control mechanisms, and insufficient legal frameworks have led to systematic human rights violations in the Americas, with no consistent remedy or reparation to victims. What's needed is to materialize essential guarantees and measures to combat repeated surveillance abuses in the region. To help build a path for solutions, EFF launches the guide [*Tackling Arbitrary Digital Surveillance in the Americas*](https://www.eff.org/wp/tackling-arbitrary-digital-surveillance-americas), adding to our extensive work leveraging human rights norms to confront state privacy violations.
