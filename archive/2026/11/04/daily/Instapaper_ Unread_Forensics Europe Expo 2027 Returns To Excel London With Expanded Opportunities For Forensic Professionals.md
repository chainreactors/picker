# Forensics Europe Expo 2027 Returns To Excel London With Expanded Opportunities For Forensic Professionals

3rd November 2026 by [Forensic Focus](https://www.forensicfocus.com/author/forensicfocus/ "View all posts by Forensic Focus")

![](https://www.forensicfocus.com/stable/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/FEE2027-1200x628-FF_press-release1.png)

*22–23 June 2027 | Excel London, UK*

**Forensics Europe Expo (FEE)** will return on 22–23 June 2027 at Excel London, bringing together forensic scientists, investigators, law enforcement professionals, researchers and technology providers for two days of learning, innovation and collaboration.

Building on the success of its 2026 edition, FEE 2027 will continue to develop as a specialist meeting place for the forensic community, connecting professionals across digital forensics, crime scene investigation and laboratory science.

## A Programme Shaped by Forensic Experts

At the heart of FEE is its expert-led conference programme, designed to address the practical challenges, emerging technologies and developments influencing modern forensic investigation.

In early November, FEE’s Advisory Board members will meet alongside the wider Public Protection Week Advisory Board to discuss key industry priorities, emerging challenges and opportunities for cross-sector collaboration. Their input will help shape the 2027 conference programme, ensuring it reflects the needs of forensic practitioners and the wider investigative community.

## More to Discover at Public Protection Week

For the first time, FEE will form part of **Public Protection Week**, taking place alongside The Blue Light Show, Counter Terror Expo and Prison Expo.

While retaining its dedicated forensic exhibition and conference programme, FEE will offer visitors access to a wider community spanning policing, emergency response, security and criminal justice.

With one badge providing access to all four events, forensic professionals will have additional opportunities to discover technologies, exchange expertise and connect with organisations working across the broader public protection landscape.

The FEE exhibition will showcase products, technologies and specialist services supporting every stage of forensic investigation, from the crime scene and laboratory to digital evidence analysis.

## Register Your Interest for FEE 2027

Whether you’re looking to explore new forensic technologies, gain practical insights from industry experts or connect with colleagues from across the international forensic community, FEE 2027 offers an opportunity to stay informed and engaged with developments across the profession.
