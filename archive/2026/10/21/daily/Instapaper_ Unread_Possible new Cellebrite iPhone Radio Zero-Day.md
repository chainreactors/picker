## Possible new Cellebrite iPhone Radio Zero-Day

Justin.sh is [reporting](https://justin.sh/blog/2026/10/cellebrite-advanced-unlocks/) on a confidential Cellebrite document that has been leaked to him:

> Cellebrite calls it Advanced Unlocks (AU from here on), not to be confused with the “advanced unlocking services” they’ve marketed for years. A [press release](https://web.archive.org/web/20260814034529/https%3A//www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cellebrite-appoints-shiven-ramji-chief-executive-officer-succeeding-thomas-e-hogan-302850249.html) from earlier this year confirms AU is a new product for 2026.
>
> […]
>
> According to Cellebrite, AU:
>
> * is a “next-generation AFU vector that is more resilient to Apple’s OS changes”* works with Stolen Device Protection enabled, which tightens USB restrictions* is enabled by a dedicated appliance with a stated range (!) of about 8 meters.

AU = Advanced Unlocks, Cellebrite’s term. AFU = after first unlock.
