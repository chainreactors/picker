**Paul:** In your research, you refer to concepts like secondary traumatic stress. For those listening who may not be familiar with that term, how would you explain it and why is it so important in these roles?

**Fazeelat:** Secondary traumatic stress is the impact of being exposed to other people’s trauma indirectly rather than experiencing it directly. So law enforcement staff — or secondary investigators and analysts, as I call them just to differentiate them from frontline roles. There’s nothing secondary about their roles, to be honest; it’s just to have that distinction between the frontline roles and the people who are not on the crime scenes.
