# My Thoughts on Renaming AI to Super Intelligence

Why I like the new name for AI, even though I'm not happy about it

October 9, 2026

[#ai](/archives/?tag=ai) [#society](/archives/?tag=society)

[**AIL***1*](/blog/ai-influence-level-ail "AIL 1 — Human Created, Minor AI Assistance")

 Virtue-as-sole-gooding…

[![A colossal purple intelligence building a stone arch while a small figure on a ladder lifts a cheap cracked mask off its face, and a second figure watches with arms crossed](/images/renaming-ai-to-super-intelligence.webp)](/images/renaming-ai-to-super-intelligence.webp)

I'm torn on this one.

First, I hate it because it feels like the [Gulf of America](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/restoring-names-that-honor-american-greatness/). But I also really like it, both as a name and as a strategy.

Both of these reduce down to "artificial" being derogatory.

In [the real internet of things](/blog/the-real-internet-of-things) I also used SI instead of AI, but there I called it "synthetic" intelligence instead of "artificial" intelligence. Same reason. Artificial is just a bad name for anything real. [And AI is real](/blog/this-is-intelligence).

> I prefer the term "synthetic" vs. "artificial" intelligence, as its capabilities will end up being just as real as our own despite having non-biological origins.[The Real Internet of Things (2016)](/blog/the-real-internet-of-things)

Strategically, I actually agree with [Our Great Leader](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/americas/trump-renames-ai-super-intelligence-un-general-assembly-speech) and others that Super Intelligence is far more optimistic, and descriptive. Basically better marketing. And marketing matters.

So I guess I like the proposed name. But I'm not happy about it.
