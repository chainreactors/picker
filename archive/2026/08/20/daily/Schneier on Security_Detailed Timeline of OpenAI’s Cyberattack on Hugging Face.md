## Detailed Timeline of OpenAI’s Cyberattack on Hugging Face

OpenAI [presented](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=87DyyMV0kCY) details of its AI’s model’s cyberattack on Hugging Face at Black Hat last week. Simon Willison [details](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/7/openai-timeline/#atom-everything) the timeline. It’s really interesting to read through—and really impressive cyberoffense work.
