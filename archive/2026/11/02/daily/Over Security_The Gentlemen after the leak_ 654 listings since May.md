The Gentlemen, the ransomware-as-a-service operation whose internal Rocket.Chat database was put up for sale on 5 May 2026, has posted 654 organisations to its leak site in the 171 days since, more than any other ransomware operator in that period and twice its own pace in the 171 days before. I have examined a copy of the leaked chats, 3,733 messages between nine handles from 7 November 2025 to 30 April 2026, and set it against the Ransomtracker record of what the group did next. The leak exposed the crew in detail. It did not slow it down, nobody has been charged, and as of 23 October 2026 the leak site is still publishing.

*About this analysis. The dataset I reviewed is the 44 MB proof set that circulated after the sale post: 22 Rocket.Chat rooms exported to CSV with 312 attached screenshots. I examined it offline for structure, dates and volumes only, and I have not reproduced any message, credential, network detail, personal data or screenshot here, and will not. The victim counts come from the [Ransomtracker](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/), which the Ransomnews Research Team pulled for me on 23 October 2026 and cleaned of 48 entries that were not organisations at all; I explain those below. Where I draw an inference rather than report a record, I say so and give my confidence. Leak-site listings are claims until a victim or a regulator confirms them, and I treat them that way throughout. Ransomnews edited this piece for style and checked the figures against the tracker.*

### In brief

* The Gentlemen listed 654 organisations between 5 May and 23 October 2026, against 311 in the 171 days before the leak. Its weekly rate rose from 19 to 27 listings.
* In that window it is the most prolific ransomware operator on the tracker, ahead of Qilin at 607, with 12 per cent of all 5,334 leak-site listings posted by every group.
* Across 2026 it stands second, 937 listings to Qilin’s 1,100, and it has more than doubled its total since Ransomnews reported 483 in June.
* Targeting moved toward North America: one in ten placed listings before the leak, three in ten after, against the Tier 1 to 3 plus Latin America plan written in the chats.
