## Summary

* Serialize `processLogin` session publish with `cleanupClientConn` via `teardownMu`/`tornDown`, so AuthTimeout (or any conn-group exit) during the `iniService→Store` window cannot leave a ghost entry that permanently leaks `clientNum`.
* Abort login (close services + `needClose`) when teardown already claimed the session; keep unconditional `closeServices` for the reverse-proxy listener-leak fix.
* Add unit coverage for aborted publish, concurrent publish/teardown races, and `processLogin` abort after teardown.
