# The Glasses Were in the Fridge

Written by

[j4vv4d](https://javvadmalik.com/author/j4vv4d/)

in

[blog](https://javvadmalik.com/category/blog/)

I felt personally insulted when the optician suggested varifocal glasses because apparently now in addition to not being able to see things from far away very clearly, I can’t read small things nearby either. I felt as if I was Julius Caesar and my eyes were Brutus. Joining the like of my knees, lower back, and right shoulder in sabotaging me from living my best life.

I bought a pair anyway. thought they made me look thoughtful. One of the kids said I looked like I was about to tell them the library was closing.

For the first few days, I kept finding small things to read. Ingredients. Washing instructions. The tiny writing on the back of the remote control. There’s a whole publishing industry operating underneath household objects, and for the first time in a long time, I could read them without taking a photo on my phone just so I could zoom in on it.

Then the glasses disappeared.

I checked beside the bed, where I distinctly remembered leaving them. They weren’t there, so I checked again, more angrily. This is an important part of searching. Objects sometimes respond to a change in tone.

After twenty minutes, I started suspecting the children. Not of stealing them exactly, but of moving them for reasons that would make perfect sense to a child and require a parliamentary inquiry to explain to me.

They were in the fridge.

I’d taken them off to inspect the date on some yoghurt, put them on the shelf, then carried the yoghurt into the living room. Apparently I can now manage either sight or dairy, but not both.

My daughter suggested one of those cords that keeps your glasses round your neck. I objected on dignity grounds, despite having just spent twenty minutes accusing my family of interfering with an optician’s prescription.

I’ve ordered the cord.

It hasn’t arrived yet, but I keep checking the delivery update on my phone. The writing is quite small, so first I have to find my glasses.
