# [Google Rolls Out Gemini 4 Argon to Trusted Cyber Defenders, Plans Guardrail-Free Version](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/google-rolls-out-gemini-4-argon-to.html)

**Ravie Lakshmanan**Oct 01, 2026Artificial Intelligence / AI Safety

[![](data:image/png;base64...)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhYk4zu5KwFghyphenhyphenGNFmUKfmtScZfRpYSwO3huCn6VYY6EiFZH7t7zWwr6Agn-AqOHA19-uc6wQMnoOHXGY3V8F2ZWfxoTNiTp8VV1ePAvDGYlW3ocD8tXLN-tPuqvzwf7YRX9CXl1_Zj2b0ZcMqbZAo_5Ut3uG3gTTEXTCVxJyyTtcUlbfwXkdqCu8PpY4l6/s1700-nu-rw-lo-l85-e365/gemini-4.jpg)

Google on Wednesday announced its latest frontier artificial intelligence (AI) model, **Gemini 4 Argon**, that it said is being rolled out to a set of trusted cyber defenders through its Fairwind Program.

"It delivers frontier performance in complex workflows across real-world software engineering, enterprise knowledge work like legal and finance, and cybersecurity defense," Koray Kavukcuoglu, senior vice president of Google DeepMind and Chief AI Architect at Google, [said](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/).

The development comes nearly a month after the tech giant unveiled [Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/google-anthropic-and-openai-unveil.html), which it described as the most capable cybersecurity model.

Like similar models from rivals Anthropic and OpenAI, Argon is assessed to be highly capable at autonomously finding, validating, and patching critical software vulnerabilities.

[![Cybersecurity](data:image/png;base64...)](https://thehackernews.uk/growth-ai-control-d)

This includes a previously unknown critical vulnerability exposing sensitive personal information across healthcare software used by hospitals worldwide. Google did not reveal which software was affected by the security flaw.

Argon, according to Google, demonstrates "impressive leaps" in vulnerability discovery over 3.8 Flash Cyber, and outperforms the model when it comes to discovering the attack surface and generating proof-of-concepts (PoCs) to validate the findings.

[![](data:image/png;base64...)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgvuPZnI_rVPR0sU6Kbd6cXmEYM_S3ihKtLXIP2btofVHkXDO1_KXs0GIQ0Oo3yPfki8kjSxLODjBBGQ9ZwaIbIM3HllVka-MNU8ig2Pzcm03e6UJVgjXT3nBWa_6q5NkwezTq5ymtvdVwKnX7DoxNFoAsJM-W6pH5v0NW1owGqf4cCookczFpOXo4ZeOTa/s1700-nu-rw-lo-l85-e365/gemini.gif)

Google said it plans to release a version of Argon without cyber guardrails to trusted defenders and its internal teams so that they can take advantage of its full capabilities.

Ahead of a broader rollout, the company said it's working to strengthen safeguards to rein in misalignment, prevent model misuse by bad actors, and make it resilient to indirect prompt injections (IPIs). According to a [model evaluation](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini/gemini_4_argon_model_evaluation.pdf) released by Google, Argon outperforms other models to take the top spot in the Gray Swan's IPI benchmark.

"We are deploying misalignment mitigations that monitor Argon’s chain-of-thought and actions and stop execution when necessary," Google said. "We strongly encourage the rest of the industry to preserve reasoning transparency in these pivotal moments of increased capabilities while navigating alignment risks, so that model thoughts remain helpful in identifying and diagnosing misalignment."
