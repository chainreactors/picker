# Register now for our training at OWASP

Join us in San Francisco for our popular 3-day Threat Modeling Intensive With AI
![An image of a threat modeling classroom where robots are acting as instructor assistants](/images/blog/img/2026/owaspUSA-course-announcement-1000w.jpeg)

Join us as Adam leads the delivery of one of our most popular courses, [Threat Modeling Intensive Using AI](https://owaspglobalappsecusa2026.sched.com/event/2O5ER), at OWASP Global AppSec USA 2026 in San Francisco from November 2 to November 4. This three day course teaches you both how to Threat Model and also how to complement those skills with a variety of AI systems. Whether you're in town for OWASP or are a Bay Area engineer who's been wanting to master threat modeling, now's the time to sign up.

Training tickets are completely separate from general conference passes. [Purchase your training ticket directly through the OWASP registration system](https://owasp.glueup.com/event/167174/register/). Spots are limited, so secure your seat today.

Stay tuned because there are plenty more updates about OWASP coming your way soon!
