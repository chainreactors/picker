# UAE claims it stopped ‘terrorist’ ransomware attack

The United Arab Emirates said it stopped a ransomware attack this weekend that allegedly targeted the country’s digital infrastructure.

The country’s Cyber Security Council published a statement on Saturday that said they “successfully thwarted organized cyberattacks of a terrorist nature that targeted the country’s digital infrastructure and vital sectors in an attempt to destabilize the nation and disrupt essential services.”

“The Council indicated that the attacks included attempts to infiltrate networks, deploy ransomware, and conduct systematic phishing campaigns targeting national platforms,” the council said. It did not respond to requests for comment.

“The attacks also involved the exploitation of artificial intelligence technologies to develop sophisticated offensive tools, reflecting a qualitative shift in the methods employed by terrorist groups and their ability to harness modern technologies to carry out digital attacks.”

The incident takes place amid heightened tensions in the region as Iran and the U.S. work on a nuclear weapons deal under [threat of military airstrikes](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/22/us/politics/trump-iran-strike-attack.html) by President Donald Trump.

The U.S. Treasury Department and the UAE’s Cyber Security Council [signed a deal in 2023](https://therecord.media/treasury-deal-with-uae-cybersecurity) outlining increased cyber cooperation and, since then, the country has faced an array of attacks by actors allegedly based in Iran.

The FBI and U.S. Defense Department [said](https://therecord.media/iran-government-working-with-ransomware-hackers-fbi-advisory) in 2024 that the government of Iran coordinated with ransomware gangs to launch attacks on several countries including the UAE.

Last year, researchers [attributed a malware campaign](https://therecord.media/sosano-malware-targets-uae-iran-suspected) targeting the UAE’s aviation, satellite communications and transportation sectors to hackers possibly affiliated with Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC).

Google-owned Mandiant [previously said](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/suspected-iranian-unc1549-targets-israel-middle-east/) suspected Iranian hackers targeted the aerospace, aviation and defense industries in the UAE.

In a note last week, head of the UAE’s Cyber Security Council Mohamed Hamad Al Kuwaiti [claimed](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/byswxn8-714-cyber-threats-targeting-uae-state-sponsored) more than 70% of the threat actors targeting the country are state-sponsored groups.
