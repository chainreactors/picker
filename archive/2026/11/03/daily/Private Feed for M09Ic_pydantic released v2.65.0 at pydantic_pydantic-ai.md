* Count `BrowserUse` model calls toward the host run's usage and `UsageLimits` by [@mpfaffenberger](https://github.com/mpfaffenberger) in [#11860](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/11860)

* Raise `ModelQuotaExceededError` instead of `ModelRateLimitError` for Z.ai balance and plan-limit 429s by [@DouweM](https://github.com/DouweM) in [#12042](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/12042)
