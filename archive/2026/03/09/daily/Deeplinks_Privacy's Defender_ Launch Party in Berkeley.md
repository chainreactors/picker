We're celebrating the launch of Privacy's Defender, a new book by [EFF Executive Director Cindy Cohn](https://www.eff.org/event/privacys-defender-book-launch-party#cindy) on Thursday, March 12—and we want you to join us! Cindy has tangled with the feds, fought for your data security, and argued before judges to protect our access to science and knowledge on the internet. In Privacy's Defender she asks: can we still have private conversations if we live our lives online?

Join the festivities for a live conversation between [Cindy Cohn](https://www.eff.org/event/privacys-defender-book-launch-party#cindy) and [Annalee Newitz](https://www.eff.org/event/privacys-defender-book-launch-party#annalee) followed by a book signing with Cindy.
