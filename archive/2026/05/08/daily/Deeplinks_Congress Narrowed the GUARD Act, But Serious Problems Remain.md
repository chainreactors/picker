Following criticism, lawmakers have narrowed the [GUARD Act](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/3062/text), a bill aimed at restricting minors’ access to certain AI systems. The [earlier version could have applied broadly](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/04/guard-act-isnt-targeting-dangerous-ai-its-blocking-everyday-internet-use) to nearly every AI-powered chatbot or search tool. The amended bill focuses more narrowly on so-called “AI companions”—conversational systems designed to simulate emotional or interpersonal interactions with users.

That change does address some of the broadest concerns raised about the original proposal, though some questions about the bill’s reach remain. Bottom line: the revised bill still creates serious problems for privacy, online speech, and parental choice.
