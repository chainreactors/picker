# SongTrivia2 Data Breach

## What Happened

In April 2026, the music trivia platform [SongTrivia2 suffered a data breach that was subsequently published to a public hacking forum](https://x.com/DarkWebInformer/status/2039804363225391147). The data contained a total of 291k unique email addresses sourced from either Google OAuth logins or accounts created on the site, the latter also containing bcrypt password hashes. The data also included names, usernames and avatars.
