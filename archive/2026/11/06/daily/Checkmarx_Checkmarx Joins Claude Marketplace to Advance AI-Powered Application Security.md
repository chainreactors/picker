*Anthropic customers can now apply spend toward Checkmarx Fusion SAST, Triage, Remediation Assist*

Buying security software is rarely slowed down by the technology, it’s slowed down by process and approvals. Budget requests, procurement queues, and vendor onboarding can add weeks to time it takes to turn on new tools after the decision is already made.

![](https://checkmarx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/1e718af6e553b3be7262fad50ba0b8129cabd613.png)

That wait time just got shorter, because Anthropic announced that [Checkmarx is now available in the Claude Marketplace](https://claude.com/resources/articles/new-agents-and-products-claude-marketplace). Anthropic customers in the U.S. can put part of their existing Anthropic spend commitment toward Checkmarx, with no new budget line and no separate buying cycle.
