# I want a better watch

[j4vv4d](https://javvadmalik.com/author/j4vv4d/)

[blog](https://javvadmalik.com/category/blog/)

September 16, 2026
1 Minute

I want a watch that understands I am middle-aged and have already done quite a lot today. I went upstairs. I carried something from one room to another. I stood up during an advert.

Unlike Apple, which seems to think what I really want from a watch is for it to listen to my conversations all day and send me a report, I want mine to improve my life.

Features should include:

* A vibration telling me to sit down because I have earned it.
* A nap alert when I have been awake for more than six hours.
* A warning when I am about to enter a pointless meeting.
* A notification saying, “You have walked far enough. You are not being chased.”
* A button that orders a tea and biscuit.
* A setting that blocks anyone using the phrase “quick call”.
* A happiness score, rather than one that makes me feel guilty about my steps.
* An emergency message to my family if I voluntarily go to the gym.

I do not want a watch that makes me fitter, healthier or more productive.

I want one that looks at my heart rate, checks the calendar and says:

**“This all seems unnecessary. Go and have a lie down.”**
