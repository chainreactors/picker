# British hacker gets 6 year sentence for $11.6M crypto theft scheme

A British hacker tied to the Scattered Spider cybercrime group was sentenced Tuesday to six years in federal prison for his part in a sprawling phishing campaign that stole more than $11.6 million in cryptocurrency from individuals in the United States.

Tyler Robert Buchanan, 24, of Dundee, Scotland, was previously described as the [suspected ringleader](https://therecord.media/suspected-scattered-spider-cybercriminal-arrested-spain) of the hacking collective, which Microsoft called “one of the most dangerous financial criminal groups” around.

Buchanan was arrested at Palma Airport in Spain in June 2024, and named among five defendants [charged](https://therecord.media/five-scattered-spider-members-charged-breaches-11-million-theft) that November with targeting victims including [Coinbase](https://therecord.media/coinbase-explains-how-0ktapus-hacker-accessed-corporate-directory), [Twilio](https://therecord.media/twilio-signal-phishing), [Mailchimp](https://therecord.media/more-than-100-mailchimp-accounts-accessed-via-social-engineering-cyberattack) and [LastPass](https://therecord.media/lastpass-attacker-hacked-engineers-home-computer-keylogger), as well as with the ransomware attack on casino giant [MGM Resorts](https://therecord.media/mgm-resorts-cyberattack-cost-millions). That attack left parts of Las Vegas paralyzed for days and cost MGM an [estimated](https://therecord.media/mgm-resorts-cyberattack-cost-millions) $100 million.

Buchanan additionally admitted using “the information stolen from company intrusions to identify and gain access to virtual currency accounts and wallets belonging to individual victims to steal millions of dollars’ worth of virtual currency,” stated the U.S. Attorney’s Office, Central District of California.

Those thefts “had severe, life-altering consequences for many victims and underscore both the seriousness and far-reaching impact of this conspiracy,” wrote prosecutors in a sentencing memorandum.

Buchanan is the last of the five defendants to be sentenced. Noah Michael Urban, 22, of Palm Coast, Florida, is serving 10 years. Ahmed Hossam Eldin Elbadawy, 25, of College Station, Texas, and Evans Onyeaka Osiebo, 22, of Dallas, each got 45 months. Joel Martin Evans, 27, of Jacksonville, North Carolina, is serving two years.

A restitution hearing is scheduled for December 11.
