## Ex-CISA boss also says no reason to panic about AI and security

![icon](/design_picker/d518b499f8a6e2c65d4d8c49aca8299d54b03012/graphics/icon/vulture_red.svg)
[Jessica Lyons](/Author/Jessica-Lyons "Read more by this author")

![](/design_picker/d2e337b97204af4aa34dda04c4e5d56d954b216f/graphics/icons/social_share_icon.svg)

RSAC 2026 "Everybody feels massive FOMO if they don't get to RSAC," Jen Easterly says.

To be fair, she has a vested interest in saying this.

After [serving as the director](https://www.theregister.com/2025/01/22/trump_cyber_policy/) of the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) agency for nearly four years - during which time she was a regular at the RSA Conference, speaking on panels and giving keynotes - in January, she [signed on as the chief executive](https://www.theregister.com/2026/01/24/cisa_skipping_rsa_exclusive) of RSAC.

[![](https://pubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/ad?co=1&iu=/6978/reg_specialfeatures/rsa&sz=300x50%7C300x100%7C300x250%7C300x251%7C300x252%7C300x600%7C300x601&tile=2&c=2acS2z7n0x2HxUswBf253QwAAAEs&t=ct%3Dns%26unitnum%3D2%26raptor%3Dcondor%26pos%3Dtop%26test%3D0)](https://pubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/jump?co=1&iu=/6978/reg_specialfeatures/rsa&sz=300x50%7C300x100%7C300x250%7C300x251%7C300x252%7C300x600%7C300x601&tile=2&c=2acS2z7n0x2HxUswBf253QwAAAEs&t=ct%3Dns%26unitnum%3D2%26raptor%3Dcondor%26pos%3Dtop%26test%3D0)

Her first conference at the helm of the organization has brought 43,000 people to San Francisco, and happens during what Easterly describes as the "most exciting time to be in cyber - and I've been in a long time."
