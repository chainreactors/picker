Key takeaways

1. 01

   NoName057(16) published personal data on 18 EU politicians, police officials, and journalists it calls hostile to Moscow.
2. 02

   The group offered cryptocurrency bounties for more sensitive information about targets and their family members.
3. 03

   The doxxing appears linked to Operation Eastwood, which disrupted the group’s botnet and took over 100 servers offline.
4. 04

   Italian Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani said he would not be intimidated after his data appeared online.
