# The intelligence asymmetry: The strategic case for autonomous defensive intelligence

Why the next generation of cyber defense will depend on systems that learn, adapt and remain governed by people

![Frost Radar 2026 badge naming Sekoia an Innovator in AI Security Operations Centers, next to the headline “Sekoia named an Innovator in AI Security Operations Centers” on a navy background.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69b190e7f47e4c44b0ee07d8/6af3712ad3030bad8cdaba8d_blog-the-intelligence-asymmetry-the-strategic-case-img-cover.webp)

## Key takeaways

AI is widening the gap between attack and defense. Closing it will take an operating model that helps security teams use intelligence at scale, learn from experience, and keep people in control.

* AI is giving threat actors more speed and scale.
* Security AI needs the right context around it to make useful decisions.
* Sekoia is building infrastructure for defensive intelligence across existing environments.
* The system should learn from the work security teams are already doing.
* Frost & Sullivan’s recognition gives organizations an external reference point as they move from AI experiments to governed execution.

The Director General of MI5 recently described the world as having entered a new era of national security, one requiring a new response. His annual threat update placed artificial intelligence within a broader resilience challenge involving critical infrastructure, businesses, communities and technology.

The message is relevant well beyond national security agencies. The operating environment is changing for every organization responsible for protecting people, services, data and trust.

Artificial intelligence is already changing the economics of cyber conflict. Adversaries can use it to accelerate reconnaissance, discover vulnerabilities, generate convincing content and coordinate complex activity. Defensive teams, however, remain constrained by fragmented environments, disconnected tools, human attention and decision latency.

This is the intelligence asymmetry.

Attackers are beginning to scale with intelligence and computation. Defense still too often scales with people.

## The next challenge is not simply better AI

The market is responding quickly. Security vendors are adding copilots and agentic features. Data and telemetry providers are moving into detection and response. Intelligence providers are embedding AI into research and analysis workflows.
