The extortion group [n0n](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/group/n0n/) named 59 organisations on its leak site on 16 November 2026, including Eli Lilly, Visa, Mastercard, JPMorgan Chase, Walmart and Procter & Gamble. None has confirmed a breach. The data n0n describes is marketing, analytics and customer-database material of the kind suppliers hold, and Expedia, which n0n named a week earlier, has confirmed that attackers reached it through a third-party data platform.

### In brief

* **Who was named:** 59 organisations in 11 countries. Of those, 46 had been on the site since 9 November as numbered placeholders such as “Company #150”. n0n swapped the numbers for names and marked all 59 as unpaid.
* **Eli Lilly:** n0n claims prescription claims data covering 1.24 million patients. It does not say whether the patients are named.
* **Card companies:** the Visa, Mastercard and JPMorgan Chase entries describe marketing records. None mentions card numbers.
* **Confirmed:** nothing by any of the 59 that we could find. Expedia confirmed a supplier breach on 12 November.
* **Caution:** Bitdefender says data from n0n’s own file server shows it targeted just over 20 victims. It listed about 150 in October alone.
