![Supreme Court](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/supreme-court-3b.jpg?itok=voFogClC "Supreme Court")

Federal agencies like Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) and Customs and Border Protection (CBP) have [descended into utter lawlessness](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/01/eff-statement-lawless-actions-ice-and-cbp), most recently in [Minnesota](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/01/eff-close-friday-solidarity-national-shutdown). The violence is shocking. So are the intrusions on digital rights. For example, we have a First Amendment [right to record](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/02/yes-you-have-right-film-ice) on-duty police, including ICE and CBP, but federal agents [are](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jan/29/minneapolis-ice-observers) [violating](https://www.fox9.com/news/ice-detains-woodbury-man-filming-agents) [this](https://people.com/ice-agent-captured-asking-have-yall-not-learned-after-renee-good-death-11885415) [right](https://www.facebook.com/ABC7/videos/2308348492979002/?extid=CL-UNK-UNK-UNK-IOS_GK0T-GK1C&mibextid=wwXIfr). Indeed, [Alex Pretti](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/01/24/us/minneapolis-shooting-ice) was exercising this right shortly before federal agents shot and killed him. So were the many people who filmed agents shooting and killing Pretti and Renee Good – thereby creating [valuable](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/01/15/video/ice-shooting-renee-good-minneapolis-videos.html) [evidence](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/01/24/us/minneapolis-shooting-federal-agents-video.html) that contradicts false claims by government leaders.
