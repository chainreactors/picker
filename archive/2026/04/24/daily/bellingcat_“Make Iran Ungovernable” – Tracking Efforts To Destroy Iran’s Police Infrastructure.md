# “Make Iran Ungovernable” – Tracking Efforts To Destroy Iran’s Police Infrastructure

April 24, 2026

* [Geolocation](/tag/geolocation)
* [Iran](/tag/iran)
* [Police](/tag/police)

Bellingcat has identified at least 80 police stations or infrastructure related to law enforcement agencies and the Basij paramilitary group that has been damaged or destroyed in the first three weeks of the United States and Israel’s war against Iran. Experts told Bellingcat that [both](https://www.timesofisrael.com/idf-planning-3-more-weeks-of-operations-to-systematically-degrade-irans-defense-industry/) [countries](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/read-trumps-full-statement-on-iran-attack) aim to degrade the Iranian regime’s “repressive capacity”.

Combined, the US and Israel have [conducted](https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2037135250522861686) [thousands](https://t.me/manniefabian/56612) of strikes during the course of the 2026 war in Iran. Targets [range](https://x.com/CENTCOM/status/2041251108224417869) from Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) sites, Navy vessels to Iranian weapons manufacturers.

In early March, a [Bellingcat analysis](https://www.bellingcat.com/news/middle-east/2026/03/04/satellite-imagery-reveals-strikes-on-iranian-police-stations/) using satellite imagery and available photos and videos identified police stations as another apparent target, with at least 15 damaged or destroyed in the capital, Tehran.

We also identified multiple strikes against police infrastructure in the country’s north and west; these areas were targeted by the Israel Defence Forces according to a [map released by the IDF](https://t.me/manniefabian/57247) on March 31.

“We are providing the brave people of Iran with the conditions to take their destiny into their own hands,” [declared](https://x.com/IsraelPersian/status/2029452531651240035) the Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs official X account, along with a photo of a destroyed police station.

In all, the majority of strikes Bellingcat analysed focused on police stations (30 incidents) and command centers or headquarters (29 incidents). Locations also include sites related to Basij, a plainclothes paramilitary organisation (9) affiliated with the IRGC that were “[involved in the deadly crackdown](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/campaigns/2026/01/what-happened-at-the-protests-in-iran/)” of protests in January 2026, others are associated with special forces (3) and traffic (2) or diplomatic (2) police compounds.

#### [Satellite Imagery Reveals Strikes on Iranian Police Stations](https://www.bellingcat.com/news/middle-east/2026/03/04/satellite-imagery-reveals-strikes-on-iranian-police-stations/)

Due to commercial satellite companies [limiting access](https://www.bellingcat.com/resources/2026/04/07/tool-damage-assessment-destruction-sentinel-satellite-imagery-iran-us-gulf/) to imagery over Iran and neighbouring countries we relied on Sentinel-2 imagery data to help verify the incidents, as well as videos and photos, some of which were also verified by [independent geolocators](https://x.com/zarGEOINT) and contributors to the [Geoconfirmed volunteer community](https://geoconfirmed.org/iran) and confirmed by Bellingcat researchers.

Location data was partly determined using open source mapping data either from [Wikimapia](https://wikimapia.org/#lang=en&lat=34.143635&lon=52.569580&z=7&m=w), [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=7/34.298/53.438) or [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/%4034.7112494%2C52.7215114%2C865587m). When video footage or photos were available for incidents reportedly targeting police stations, the location was verified with geolocation and satellite imagery analysis using either Planet Labs medium resolution PlanetScope data (restricted to imagery collected by March 9) or low resolution Sentinel-2 data.

Some locations were discovered utilising location data taken from OpenStreetMap [using Overpass Turbo](https://bellingcat.gitbook.io/toolkit/more/all-tools/overpass-turbo) and comparing that with available Sentinel-2 data throughout Iran.

## A Problem of Scale

Israel has released [multiple](https://t.me/idfofficial/16858) [videos](https://t.me/manniefabian/56513) showing the targeting of bases and checkpoints belonging to the Basij. In mid-March, the IDF [announced](https://t.me/manniefabian/56481?single) the killing of the paramilitary group’s commander, Gholamreza Soleimani.

Targeting the Basij is part of Israel’s and the US’ agenda “to degrade the regime’s repressive capacity,” Ali Vaez, the director of International Crisis Group Iran Project, told Bellingcat. Police stations are “not involved in repression in the way that crowd control police or Basij centers are”, so targeting them “appears more aimed at preventing the Islamic Republic from being able to maintain control internally,” he said.

Vaez told Bellingcat that, when considered alongside the broader range of targets, including industrial factories, the widespread targeting of police stations is part of a strategy “to make Iran ungovernable for the existing regime or whatever comes after”.

Vaez was skeptical about the short term effects: “It’s a problem of scale. Iran is such a large country, even if you are able to completely destroy, not just degrade, the capacity of the regime in policing, oppressing, etc – it really requires not just maybe weeks but maybe months if not years.”
