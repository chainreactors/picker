# Vultr Data Breach

## What Happened

In March 2023, the "AI-first global cloud platform" [Vultr disclosed a security incident at a third-party vendor](https://blogs.vultr.com/important-update). Dating back to the previous year, the incident was attributed to the ActiveCampaign email marketing service provider and resulted in the exposure of 188k unique email addresses. A small number of records also included name, IP address and country of origin. No Vultr systems or additional customer data were impacted. Vultr subsequently self-submitted the impacted data to HIBP.
