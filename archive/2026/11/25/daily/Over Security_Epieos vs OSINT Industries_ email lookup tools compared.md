[Epieos](https://epieos.com/) and [OSINT Industries](https://www.osint.industries/) are commercial lookup services that take an email address or phone number and report the online accounts registered to it. Epieos, registered in Paris, has a free tier and a €29.99 monthly plan. OSINT Industries, registered in London, starts at £19 a month, also accepts usernames, names and crypto wallets, and says it vets customers. Neither publishes an opt-out form for the people searched.

### In brief

* Epieos searches by email address or phone number only. OSINT Industries also takes usernames, names, crypto wallets, IP addresses, domain names and images.
* Epieos’s free member account covers three modules and carries what its pricing page calls a heavy watermark. The €29.99 Osinter plan gives 30 full-access requests a month across 25 named modules.
* OSINT Industries has no free self-serve tier. Its plans cost £19, £49 and £99 a month for 30, 100 and 300 credits, and the two dearer plans list API access.
* Both companies’ terms limit use to business or professional customers, and OSINT Industries’ terms also bar looking someone up over a personal dispute or out of curiosity.
* The two companies say they keep no record of searches and name the customer as the data controller for each lookup, so a person who is searched has no published way to find out about it or to opt out.

## What are Epieos and OSINT Industries?

Epieos and OSINT Industries are web services that check an email address or phone number against online platforms and return the accounts and public profile details tied to it. Investigators call that input a selector, and it is the starting point in our guide to [pivoting on threat-actor handles](https://ransomnews.com/threat-actor-handle-pivoting-osint-2026/).

Epieos’s [privacy policy](https://epieos.com/privacy) names its operator as EPIEOS SAS, with a registered office at 10 rue de la Paix in Paris, and its [about page](https://epieos.com/aboutus) says the company was founded by a cybersecurity and OSINT specialist.
