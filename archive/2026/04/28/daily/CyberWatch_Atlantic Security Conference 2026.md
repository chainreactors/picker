# Atlantic Security Conference 2026

Posted on [April 28, 2026](https://cyber-watch.ca/2026/04/28/atlantic-security-conference-2026/ "2:20 am")  by  [Cheryl Biswas](https://cyber-watch.ca/author/acbiswas2014/ "View all posts by Cheryl Biswas")

It was wonderful! 😊 Thank you for all the “Atlantic Joy” AtlSecCon ❤️

This was my fifth time presenting at an excellent Canadian cybersecurity event that brings together people from the business, researchers, practitioners, students, government and defence sectors. I was truly honoured to be selected as a speaker this year, given the high number of excellent submissions. Thank you to everyone who came to my talk especially Lina Dabit and Manuel Charette, CCISO, CISSP.

[![Black background, white graphics. AtlSecCon wave logo. ](https://cyber-watch.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2f7d8c1a-4164-4f71-8474-2ed094b37eef_1_201_a.jpeg?w=584)](https://cyber-watch.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2f7d8c1a-4164-4f71-8474-2ed094b37eef_1_201_a.jpeg)

Screenshot

Highlights included kickstarting the day with Dave Lewis and Wendy Nather ❤️ The joy of reconnecting and making new connections. Chance encounters at coat racks with Shruti Mukherjee 😃 Let’s do that again next year! Enjoying presentations and lunch table conversations with new attendees Elena Kolomeets. 😊 Seeking desserts but finding Jarett Parent instead. Geeking out over the need for community resilience and growing local skills is better than pastry! 🥮 Attending so many great talks! Sharing life lessons and popcorn in Amy Yee ; “No way!” War stories with Brendan Hohenadel ; Appreciating the sincerity of leadership and community building Julien Richard brings; Prompting fake friends with AI and Snapchat by Ian MacMillan😳 Dinners and hugs with great people 🤗 David Shipley Gurjeet C. Max Cizauskas Bhavya Kapoor and more.
This is why ❤️
