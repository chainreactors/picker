The parser cache only hashed the project dir. Edits to a local module outside it (e.g. `../modules/x`) replayed a stale parse.

* Hash every `dependency_paths` entry (file, dir or glob) into the fingerprint.
* A missing entry hashes a marker, so it appearing later is a cache miss.
* Old cache entries miss once after upgrade.

Fixes TRK-1043
