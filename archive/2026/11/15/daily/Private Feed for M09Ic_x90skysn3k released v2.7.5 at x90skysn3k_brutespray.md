## What's New in v2.7.5

> **v2.7.5 fixes a crash during mass RDP brute-forcing ([#363](https://github.com/x90skysn3k/brutespray/issues/363))**: RDP servers that push FastPath output right after the CredSSP handshake previously killed the whole process with a nil-pointer SIGSEGV inside the RDP library.

### Fixed: RDP Brute-Force Process Crash (`SIGSEGV`)

* **Root cause** — the RDP credential path uses auth-only login (X.224 negotiation + CredSSP/NLA, no full session stack), which never registers a FastPath listener on the TPKT transport layer; the background read loop still starts immediately and routes any non-X.224 frame to the FastPath handler, which dereferenced the nil listener
* **Crash mode** — servers (or intermediaries) sending FastPath output after a successful NLA handshake triggered a nil-pointer SIGSEGV on an unrecovered library goroutine, terminating the entire brutespray process mid-run; no recover boundary was reachable from brutespray
* **Fix** — pinned `github.com/x90skysn3k/grdp v1.0.4 → v1.0.5` ([x90skysn3k/grdp#3](https://github.com/x90skysn3k/grdp/pull/3)): FastPath frames arriving with no listener registered are dropped and the read loop continues; full-session RDP clients are unaffected
