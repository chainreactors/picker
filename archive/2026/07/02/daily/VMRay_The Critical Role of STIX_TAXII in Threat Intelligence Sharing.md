Cyber threat intelligence is only as useful as a team’s ability to share, understand, and act on it. For years, inconsistent formats and manual handoffs slowed that down, leaving security teams with threat data they couldn’t easily operationalize. STIX/TAXII changed that.

This article explains what these standards are, why they matter for threat intelligence sharing, and how security teams can operationalize structured threat intelligence.

---

## How STIX and TAXII Emerged

Before standardized formats existed, threat information moved between organizations inconsistently: spreadsheets, PDFs, emails, and proprietary feeds. Every handoff required manual translation, and automation was nearly impossible.
