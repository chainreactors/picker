The Gentlemen ransomware operation listed seven high-profile organisations on its leak site between 9 and 11 October 2026: Deloitte, the Royal Thai Air Force, KFC, Placer.ai, MoneyGram, Confluent and BigID. Each entry carries a countdown to publication. None of the seven has confirmed an intrusion, the group had published no samples for the largest names as of the first reports on 12 October, and the listings arrived during the operator’s busiest year on record, with 944 listings recorded on the Ransomtracker so far in 2026.

## Who are The Gentlemen?

The Gentlemen is a ransomware-as-a-service operation that surfaced in mid-2025 and has become the second most active leak-site poster of 2026. The [operator profile on the Ransomtracker](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/group/the-gentlemen/) shows 944 listings this year against 78 in all of 2025, behind only Qilin’s 1,071. August was its busiest month with 163 listings, September added 106, and the first half of October brought 50 more. Sophos tracks the operation as GOLD SHERWOOD and describes affiliates who escalate privileges quickly, lean on legitimate remote-access tools, attempt to disable security controls, and in some cases deploy the encryptor within 24 hours of the first observed activity. We profiled the group’s leaked internal playbook and its victim pattern earlier this year in [The Gentlemen: 483 victims and a leaked playbook](https://ransomnews.com/the-gentlemen-ransomware-2026/); the count has nearly doubled since.

## What was listed, and when?

The [live leak-site index](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/) recorded the seven entries in this order: Deloitte on 9 October at 16:37 UTC, the Royal Thai Air Force on 10 October at 20:12, KFC at 23:36, Placer.ai at 23:49, then MoneyGram, Confluent and BigID between 00:02 and 00:25 on 11 October.
