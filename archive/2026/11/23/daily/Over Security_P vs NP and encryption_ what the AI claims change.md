P vs NP is still unsolved, and neither OpenAI nor Anthropic has claimed to solve it. In October 2026 each lab said a model had found a way to solve 3-SAT faster than a long-standing conjecture, the Exponential Time Hypothesis, allowed. Outside researchers are still checking both proofs. The method only overtakes older ones on formulas too large to write down, so RSA, AES and password hashes are unaffected.

### In brief

* The Clay Mathematics Institute still lists P vs NP as unsolved.
* OpenAI and Anthropic released separate papers on 26 and 27 October 2026 claiming the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false. Neither paper claims that P = NP.
* Both proofs pass the Lean proof checker. The review led by the conjecture’s authors has reported no flaw and has not certified the result.
* The lowest published estimate of where Anthropic’s method overtakes earlier ones is a formula with about 2^420 clauses, a 127-digit number.
* A proof of P = NP that came with a practical algorithm would break RSA, elliptic-curve and AES keys. The October papers are not such a proof.

## Has AI solved P vs NP?

No AI system has solved P vs NP, and [the Clay Mathematics Institute lists the problem as unsolved](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/p-vs-np/). The question asks whether every problem whose answer is quick to check is also quick to solve. The talk of a breakthrough comes from a narrower result about one such problem, 3-SAT, and from a month of commentary that followed it.
