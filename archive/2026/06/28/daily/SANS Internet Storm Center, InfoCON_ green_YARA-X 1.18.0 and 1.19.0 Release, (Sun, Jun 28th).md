# [YARA-X 1.18.0 and 1.19.0 Release](/forums/diary/YARAX%2B1180%2Band%2B1190%2BRelease/33106/)

**Published**: 2026-06-28. **Last Updated**: 2026-06-28 07:56:44 UTC
**by** [Didier Stevens](/handler_list.html#didier-stevens) (Version: 1)

[0 comment(s)](/diary/YARAX%2B1180%2Band%2B1190%2BRelease/33106/#comments)

[YARA-X's 1.18.0](https://github.com/VirusTotal/yara-x/releases/tag/v1.18.0) release brings 3 improvements and 2 bugfixes.

One of the improvements is a new command-line option, --cpu-limit, allowing one to limit the amount of CPU YARA requires.

[YARA-X's 1.19.0](https://github.com/VirusTotal/yara-x/releases/tag/v1.19.0) release brings 4 improvements and 2 bugfixes.
