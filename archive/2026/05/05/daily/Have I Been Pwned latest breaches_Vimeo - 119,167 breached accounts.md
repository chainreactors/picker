# Vimeo Data Breach

## What Happened

In April 2026, the ShinyHunters extortion group [listed Vimeo on their extortion portal as part of their "pay or leak" campaign](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/video-service-vimeo-confirms-anodot-breach-exposed-user-data/). They subsequently published hundreds of gigabytes of data, predominantly consisting of video titles, technical data and metadata. The data also included 119k unique email addresses, sometimes accompanied by names. [Vimeo attributed the exposure](https://vimeo.com/blog/post/anodot-third-party-security-incident) to a breach of Anodot, a third-party analytics vendor, and advised the incident *does not* include "Vimeo video content, valid user login credentials, or payment card information".
