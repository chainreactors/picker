The Qilin ransomware group listed Joliet Public Schools District 86 on its leak site on 14 October 2026. The district runs 22 schools for about 10,300 pre-kindergarten to eighth-grade pupils in Joliet, Illinois, and it has been here before: LockBit claimed the same district in July 2024. Qilin’s entry gives no data volume, sample or deadline, the district has not commented, and the listing lands in the most active year any ransomware operator has had on the Ransomtracker, with 1,071 Qilin victims recorded since January.

## Who is Qilin?

### The most prolific leak-site poster of 2026

Qilin is a ransomware-as-a-service operation, originally known as Agenda, that has run since 2022 and now leads every volume table we keep. The [Qilin profile on the Ransomtracker](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/group/qilin/) shows 1,071 listings so far in 2026 after 1,029 in all of 2025, with 27 new listings in the past seven days alone. Its best-known attack remains the June 2024 intrusion at pathology provider Synnovis, which cancelled thousands of appointments and operations across London hospitals. Schools are a steady part of its intake rather than an exception: in the same week Qilin also posted Friendship Christian School, and two other operators listed public school districts with their names masked, which is how routinely education appears on the [live leak-site index](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/).
