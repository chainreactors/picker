# Why simple phishing is dying, and what that means for your SOC

* Date:
  2026-04-28

The era of the single malicious link is fading.

Today’s phishing attacks are multi-stage, multi-format delivery chains designed to exhaust analysts and evade gateways. Here’s what’s changed, and how VMRay helps you keep up.

---

A few years ago, a phishing email was a phishing email. A sketchy link, a credential harvesting page, a verdict. Done.

That’s no longer the world we live in.

The attacks landing in SOC queues today look nothing like that. They arrive as clean-looking emails carrying password-protected Word files, PDFs with embedded QR codes, and links that redirect through multiple legitimate services before anything malicious appears. The initial payload often passes every perimeter check — because, on its own, it *is* benign. The malicious intent only reveals itself several stages deep, after a document is opened, a QR code is scanned, a URL is followed, and a clipboard payload is executed.

These are complex delivery chains. And they are quickly becoming the norm, not the exception.
