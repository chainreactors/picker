# Combining Sekoia Intelligence and OpenCTI

The Filigran x Sekoia.io partnership announcement is an opportunity to put the spotlight back on the benefits of the integration between OpenCTI and Sekoia Threat Intelligence.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69b190e7f47e4c44b0ee07d8/6a2a7da9b97c48a61ea5815c_Sekoia.io-Filigran.webp)

## Key takeaways

Sekoia highlights its Filigran partnership and how the OpenCTI connector ingests Sekoia Intelligence with full STIX preservation.

* The post marks a Filigran and Sekoia partnership and revisits the OpenCTI and Sekoia Intelligence integration.
* The Sekoia connector for OpenCTI, released in 2021, is freely available and maintained by Filigran.
* It is a data import connector that ingests new Sekoia intelligence into an OpenCTI instance.
* Native STIX 2.1 support on both sides preserves source data fully during ingestion.
* Readers are pointed to the Filigran blog for a deeper look at how the integration works.

The [**Filigran**](https://filigran.io/) **x Sekoia** partnership announcement is an opportunity to put the spotlight back on the benefits of the integration between OpenCTI and Sekoia Threat Intelligence.

The existing Sekoia connector for OpenCTI has been released in 2021 and is freely available on the OpenCTI Github repository. This connector is regularly updated and maintained by Filigran.

The Integration between OpenCTI and Sekoia.io is a data import connector. This connector leverages the ingestion of **new intelligence from Sekoia.io into an OpenCTI instance with a 100% preservation of source data** based on a native STIX 2.1 support on both sides.

Want to know more on how the integration works? Read more on [Filigran blog](https://blog.filigran.io/sekoia-io-threat-intelligence-in-opencti-2fa8a32c5247).
