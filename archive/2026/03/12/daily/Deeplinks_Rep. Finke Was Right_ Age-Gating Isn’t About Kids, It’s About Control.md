When Rep. Leigh Finke [spoke last month before](https://www.foxnews.com/media/transgender-minnesota-lawmaker-pushes-back-adult-site-age-verification-bill-over-fears-overreach) the Minnesota House Commerce Finance and Policy Committee to testify against [HF1434](https://www.house.mn.gov/bills/Info/HF1434), a broad-sweeping proposal to age-gate the internet, she began with something disarming: agreement.

“I want to support the basic part of this,” she said, the shared goal of protecting young people online. Because that is not controversial: everyone wants kids to be safe. But HF1434, Minnesota’s proposed age-verification bill, simply won’t “protect children.” It mandates that websites hosting speech that is protected by the First Amendment for both adults and young people to verify users’ identities, often through government IDs or biometric data.
