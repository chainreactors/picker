CI configures with `cmake -S . -B build` and no `CMAKE_BUILD_TYPE`, so every job builds at `-O0`. Two bugs were living in that gap. Neither reproduces unoptimized.

## `type_size` / `type_align` mutate their by-value parameter

Both recursed on their own by-value `Type`:

```
case TY_ARRAY:  return type_size(*t.elem_type) * t.length;
```

clang rewrites that into a loop that overwrites the argument slot in place, and the slot never gets the private copy `byval` promises. Two distinct failures follow.
