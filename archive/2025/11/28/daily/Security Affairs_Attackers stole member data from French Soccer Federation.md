## The French Soccer Federation (FFF) disclosed a data breach after hackers used a compromised account to steal member data.

A compromised account allowed attackers to breach the [French Soccer Federation (FFF)](https://www.fff.fr/), stealing data belonging to its members. The organization confirmed the cyberattack on Thursday, but did not disclose the number of members impacted...
