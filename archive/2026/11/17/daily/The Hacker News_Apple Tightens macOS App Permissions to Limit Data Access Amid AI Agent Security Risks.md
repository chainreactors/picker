# [Apple Tightens macOS App Permissions to Limit Data Access Amid AI Agent Security Risks](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/apple-tightens-macos-app-permissions-to.html)

**Ravie Lakshmanan**Nov 17, 2026Artificial Intelligence / Data Privacy

[![](data:image/png;base64...)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEjpfVz_pnzHGcrDTsga77xG6yDhi_sMihXsGCjULyapjweerWHdHByPuWqWYmWQsDAAV37EfmctWtSZgYCHJ-2FMGEE6K94YRDVFjHVO0i1jZxn-TIewKbDqIn3y-pNMH7pqJ9SA1X2Sk_r_vn_SfRdxbPB2kH9-hHkNrigumGuQKXaA6xlHqCdWu4ZDAFH/s1700-nu-rw-lo-l85-e365/apple-macos.jpg)

Apple on Monday [introduced](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=oqng0zjk) new controls that allow Mac users to give more granular access to data they give to apps, including those that use artificial intelligence (AI) agents.

The changes, currently previewed in macOS 27.3 beta 2, relate to increased transparency and more options for two of its APIs: Device Control and Data Access, and Full Disk Access.

"Before granting an app such extraordinary access, users should understand that it puts all of the data on their Mac at risk," Apple said in a statement. "These changes are designed to help them do that and to empower them to make informed decisions about their own data and privacy."

To that end, Device Control and Data Access is being split into two settings: System & App Protection Override and Accessibility.

Other changes include -

* A single method for granting permissions by selecting the requesting app from a list
* Encouraging users to grant access to just the specific apps (for System & App Protection Override) or folders (for Full Disk Access) that are required for their workflows
* Increased visibility into the range of data that apps will receive access to or have control over when users grant full permissions
* An option for users to configure apps to ask every time they require access
* A transparency indicator when one app is controlling another app using System & App Protection Override

Apple stressed that app permissions previously granted by users will remain intact, adding that developers should be prepared to make changes to their onboarding workflows to ensure their apps can handle scenarios where access is granted to only a limited set of folders or apps via these APIs.

[![Cybersecurity](data:image/png;base64...)](https://thehackernews.uk/network-defense-dd)

In addition, apps that use Full Disk Access for disk backup or the new Accessibility settings for assistive technologies can request entitlements to offer the same experience for new installations that they offered before macOS 27.3.

"Currently, known apps aren't affected and will keep the same experience without an entitlement," the tech giant added. "Additional entitlement details will be available soon."

The development comes a little over a month after Apple [began taking steps](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/apple-plans-tighter-macos-full-disk.html) to tighten controls around the Full Disk Access macOS setting due to security risks posed by artificial intelligence (AI) agents.
