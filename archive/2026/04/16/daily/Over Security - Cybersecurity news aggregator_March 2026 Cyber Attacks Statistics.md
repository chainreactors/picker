After the cyber attacks timelines ([part I](https://www.hackmageddon.com/2026/03/26/1-15-march-2026-cyber-attacks-timeline/) and [part II](https://www.hackmageddon.com/2026/04/14/16-31-march-2025-cyber-attacks-timeline/)), it’s time to publish the statistics for March 2026 where I collected and analyzed 282 events: a sharp increase compared to the 176 events of the [previous month](https://www.hackmageddon.com/2026/03/12/february-2026-cyber-attacks-statistics/).

In March 2026, **Cyber Crime** continued to lead the **Motivations** chart with **64%** (it was 62% in February), ahead of **Cyber Espionage** at number two with **15%** (it was 21%). **Hacktivism** took over the third position with 6%, ahead of **Cyber Warfare** with **3%**.

Once again, **Malware** led the **Attack Techniques** with **43%**(it was 37%), followed by **Account Takeovers** with **19%** (up from 11%) and **Ransomware**, which lost the third position with **9%** (it was 16%).

And even in March 2026 **Social Engineering** led the **Initial Access** vectors with **16%**, down from 18%, and in colocation with **Phishing**. **Supply Chain Compromises** are still in third position with 10%, slightly down from 12% of February and January 2026.

And in March 2026, the **Distribution of Targets** chart was led by organizations in the **Public Administration** sector with **21%**, a sharp increase from 14% of the previous month. Targets in the **Information and Communication** sector slid at number two with **18%**, in any case up from 15%, ahead of **Financial** sector with 10%.

As always bear in mind that the sample refers exclusively to the attacks included in my [timelines](https://www.hackmageddon.com/category/security/cyber-attacks-timeline/), available from public sources such as blogs and news sites. Obviously the sample cannot be complete, but only aims to provide an high level overview of the threat landscape.
