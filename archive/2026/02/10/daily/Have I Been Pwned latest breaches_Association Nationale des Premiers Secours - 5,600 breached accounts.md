# Association Nationale des Premiers Secours Data Breach

## What Happened

In January 2026, [a data breach impacting the French non-profit Association Nationale des Premiers Secours (ANPS) was posted to a hacking forum](https://x.com/justabreach/status/2017260468448981108/). The breach exposed 5.6k unique email addresses along with names, dates of birth and places of birth. ANPS self-submitted the data to HIBP and advised the incident was traced back to a legacy system and did not impact health data, financial information or passwords.

## Compromised Data

Dates of birth

Email addresses

Names

Places of birth

Salutations
