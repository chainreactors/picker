# Heading to San Francisco? Meet PortSwigger at OWASP Global AppSec USA 2026

[ ]

Lucy Ashall |
Wednesday, 28 October 2026 at 15:32 UTC

![OWASP AppSec 2026](/cms/images/0e/f9/765e-article-pre_owasp_blog_and_linkedin_blog_header.png)

*Join us at booth G14 on 5–6 November at the Hyatt Regency San Francisco. We’re a Gold sponsor, with live Burp AT demos, conversations about your AppSec programme, and something new to share.*

OWASP is celebrating its 25th anniversary this year, and we’re heading to San Francisco to join the AppSec community. Whether you’re a pentester, an AppSec lead, or building your organisation’s testing programme, we’d love to meet you.

## See what’s new at booth G14

Find us in the expo hall on Thursday 5 and Friday 6 November for:

* **Burp AT, live.** See how agentic AI extends human-led [pentesting](/solutions/penetration-testing). Explore how it can support your investigations, and bring your questions to the team.
* **[Burp Suite DAST](/burp/dast).** Talk to us about dynamic scanning across your application portfolio and how it complements manual testing.
* **Something new for AppSec teams.** We’re keeping the details under wraps for now. Visit the booth to see what’s next.

## Get your passport stamped

Collecting stamps for the OWASP vendor passport? Add booth G14 to your route. Stop by for a demo, a chat, and your stamp.

## Join us at the networking reception

The expo hall reception runs from 4:30–6:30pm on Thursday 5 November (San Francisco time). We’ll be at the booth throughout, so come and catch up with the team.

## Make time for a conversation

Want to talk through your AppSec programme or take a closer look at Burp AT? Request a meeting slot in advance, and we’ll set aside time for you.

[Request a meeting slot with our team](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1q8xf-MT1ews5tlfaNuy271OcpyhcRgE0JIrGJ2x87_M/viewform)

## See you in San Francisco

Can’t make it? Follow us on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/portswigger/) and [X](https://x.com/Burp_Suite) for updates from the show. If you’re joining us, tag us in your posts and come and say hello at booth G14.
