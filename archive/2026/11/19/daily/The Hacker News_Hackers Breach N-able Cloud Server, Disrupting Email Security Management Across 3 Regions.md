# [Hackers Breach N-able Cloud Server, Disrupting Email Security Management Across 3 Regions](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/n-able-confirms-hackers-accessed-cloud.html)

**Ravie Lakshmanan**Nov 19, 2026Cloud Security / Email Security

[![](data:image/png;base64...)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEjjPpZ9yL9D7JBQvp4_ZmnP8S_U6HKdq3rSM_FVv_uDgT9Iz8D56tO6GNDNE4rs29pejUiKQlxi1HpZ38KYbaSB01PmgPItVL8N-Y9G9vcuJlnadltKvmGhvUKYntAEIVCXFixjdBMyu6S9-zEUlIgGf7E5euvodJbn9LIYWTkRpxwH0g7nz2xp2v5PGMdq/s1700-nu-rw-lo-l85-e365/n-able-hacked.jpg)

N-able on Wednesday disclosed that unknown threat actors gained unauthorized access to one of the servers in the Mail Assure and Spam Experts Hosted Cloud, causing it to disable management interfaces and APIs.

"We have taken steps to mitigate the threat and are continuing to harden our services to reduce the risk of recurrence," N-able [said](https://uptime.n-able.com/event/202672/).

"The investigation is ongoing, but at this time we do not have evidence that customer data was compromised, including access to tenants' mailboxes."

The following services remain impacted -

* Mail Assure (Americas)
* Mail Assure (APAC)
* Mail Assure (Europe)
* SpamExperts (Americas)
* SpamExperts (APAC)
* SpamExperts (Europe)

The discovery comes after N-able began an investigation into a service disruption affecting Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud on November 16, 2026.

[![Cybersecurity](data:image/png;base64...)](https://thehackernews.uk/threat-protection-d)

While it was initially traced to a combination of platform configuration and service dependencies within the Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud environment, it has since been determined that the disruption was actually caused by a security incident.

N-able said it's continuing to work on restoring Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud across all regions, while core mail flow and inbound and outbound mail filtering remain available.

"The management portal (UI), APIs, quarantine, and logging remain offline while our teams work to restore them securely. Access will be restored once validation is complete," it said.

The company also noted that it has directly reached out to Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud partners with "information relevant to their environment and whether action is required or not."

As of November 19, 2026, at 8 a.m. UTC, N-able said the restoration process is still underway, and that it's making steady progress on validating and hardening the management portal and its supporting services.

It's worth noting that the incident is unrelated to the discovery of multiple vulnerabilities impacting N-central referred to as [Able2Shell](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/n-able-takes-hosted-n-central-offline.html), [4EverAble2Shell, and Agent2Shell](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/n-able-patches-17-n-central-flaws-as.html).
