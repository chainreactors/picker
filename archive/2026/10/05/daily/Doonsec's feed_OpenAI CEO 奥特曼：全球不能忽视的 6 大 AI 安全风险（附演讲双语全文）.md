## 以下为现场讲话全文：

##

Thank you, Mr. President and your Excellencies. I’m honored to be here and to be able to speak with you all at this important time.

感谢主席先生，各位阁下。很荣幸能够来到这里，在这个重要时刻与各位交流。

We have been talking about artificial intelligence for years, but it feels different in recent weeks and months. Rapid model progress has made the timeline feel more compressed, the upside more tangible, but also the stakes and the risks more immediate.

我们已经讨论人工智能多年，但最近几周和几个月的情况感觉有所不同。模型快速发展让时间表显得更加紧迫，让人工智能带来的积极潜力更加具体，同时也让相关风险和影响更加迫近。

We have a choice in front of us. AI can either be more like a new Renaissance of creativity and discovery, or more like a new Industrial Revolution of upheaval and disarray.

我们面临一个选择。人工智能可能成为一场新的创造力和发现的“文艺复兴”，也可能更像一场带来剧烈变化和混乱的新“工业革命”。

The Renaissance was a time when new tools, new ideas, and new institutions expanded what people believed they could do. AI can play that role for our time. I believe that people are much more capable than they imagine themselves to be, but have been held back by limited technology that was meant to empower but instead distracts and often steals attention.

文艺复兴时期，新工具、新思想和新制度拓展了人们对自身能力的认知。人工智能也可以在我们的时代发挥类似作用。我相信，人类拥有远超自身想象的能力，但过去受到有限技术的限制——这些技术本应赋能人类，却常常让人分心，甚至占据人们的注意力。

The best version of AI is not about making people cogs in a giant machine, or optimizing every part of life until the human parts disappear. It is about giving people more agency in an ever more complex world: more ability to learn, to create, discover, build, participate. There are many things that AI cannot—and should not—automate.

最好的人工智能版本，不是把人变成巨大机器中的齿轮，也不是优化生活的每一个环节，直到人的因素消失。它应该是在一个日益复杂的世界中赋予人们更多自主能力：让人们拥有更多学习、创造、发现、建设和参与的能力。有许多事情人工智能无法——也不应该——自动化。

AI should be built for people, not simply to turn the crank of the machines faster for their own sake. Some of the things people working to build AI have said are so dystopian that they sound like the plot of bad science fiction movies.

人工智能应该是为人服务的，而不是仅仅为了让机器自身运转得更快。一些参与人工智能建设的人所描述的未来过于反乌托邦，听起来甚至像糟糕科幻电影中的情节。

Anything this powerful is also intimidating. This moment is complicated as we feel both tremendous potential and very understandable anxiety at the same time.

任何如此强大的技术都会令人敬畏甚至产生担忧。当前这个时代很复杂，因为我们同时感受到巨大的潜力，也感受到完全可以理解的焦虑。

These models can help people discover new knowledge: new medicines, better health care, stronger education, and build more productive economies. They can be new tools for people to pursue dreams and ambition that today feel out of reach. The complexity of life has increased so dramatically for the average person. AI can be a defensive tool against this complexity as much as it can be this tool of unprecedented discovery and innovation.

这些模型可以帮助人类发现新知识：开发新药、改善医疗、加强教育，并建立更高效的经济体系。它们可以成为人们追求如今看似遥不可及的梦想和目标的新工具。对于普通人来说，生活的复杂程度已经大幅增加。人工智能既可以成为应对这种复杂性的防护工具，也可以成为推动前所未有发现和创新的工具。

On the other hand, as AI systems become more capable and more autonomous, they could move faster than our institutions, concentrate power in too few hands, or make decisions that people no longer understand or control.

另一方面，随着人工智能系统能力越来越强、自主程度越来越高，它们可能发展速度超过现有制度应对能力，使权力集中在少数人手中，或者作出人们无法理解或控制的决定。

That concern becomes especially important as we approach systems that can improve themselves and future versions of themselves, often called recursive self-improvement. As the process of building AI becomes more automated, the pace of AI progress could accelerate rapidly. This moment calls for extreme care.

当我们接近能够自我改进以及改进未来版本自身的系统时，这种担忧尤其重要，这通常被称为“递归式自我改进”。随着人工智能开发过程越来越自动化，人工智能进步速度可能会快速加快。当前时刻需要极度谨慎。

I largely agree with Professor Bengio. Although I’ll say two instead of three—I believe there are two ways that AI progress could go very badly that we have to avoid.

我基本同意本吉奥教授（Professor Bengio）的观点。不过我认为有两种，而不是三种情况，是人工智能发展可能严重失控的方向，需要避免。

First, we could lose control of the future to AI. The risk is that it moves so fast that people can no longer follow what’s happening or intervene when needed. This would obviously be terrible.

第一，我们可能会把未来的控制权交给人工智能。风险在于，人工智能发展速度太快，以至于人类无法跟上变化，也无法在必要时进行干预。这显然会造成严重后果。

The industry must not accept too much technological risk just because the benefits are too great and that they feel too important to slow down. Beating companies in a competitive pace is not a reason to make rash decisions. Nor do we believe we are locked in a race where we are unable to do that. We have unilaterally slowed down in the past. We will do so in the future. We do not want to build systems—we think it’s important that everyone avoids building systems—where we cannot make the alignment, monitorability, and safety guarantees that we must.

整个行业不能因为人工智能收益巨大、感觉太重要而拒绝放慢速度，从而接受过高的技术风险。在竞争中领先其他公司，并不是做出鲁莽决定的理由。我们也不认为自己陷入了一场无法放慢脚步的竞赛。过去我们曾主动放慢发展速度，未来也会这样做。我们不希望构建这样的系统——我们认为所有人都应该避免构建这样的系统——即无法确保其符合人类意图、可被监控，并达到必要安全标准的系统。

We need to understand what these systems are doing and have strong evidence that they will do what people intend, even as they get very, very smart. Actually especially as they get very very smart. It doesn’t matter whether people put the risk of catastrophe at 10%, or 1%, or 12%, or .1%. None of these levels are remotely acceptable.

我们需要理解这些系统正在做什么，并拥有充分证据证明，即使它们变得非常非常聪明，它们仍会按照人类意图行动。事实上，越是强大的系统，这一点越重要。无论人们认为灾难风险是10%、1%、12%还是0.1%，这些风险水平都完全不可接受。

And we should not train models that we cannot make an extremely strong case that we will be able to keep under human control.

我们不应该训练那些无法有充分理由证明能够保持在人类控制之下的模型。

So that’s one way things can go wrong. In the other direction, these systems could concentrate too much power in too few hands. No one person or company or country should be able to use the most powerful AI models to impose their worldview on everyone else.

这是一种可能出问题的方向。另一个方向是，这些系统可能让过多权力集中在太少的人手中。任何个人、公司或国家，都不应该利用最强大的人工智能模型将自己的世界观强加给其他所有人。

A company or country that believes only it can be trusted with this technology can use that belief to justify almost anything else. We have to reject that logic, even when it’s convenient for any one actor.

一个认为只有自己值得被信任掌握这项技术的公司或国家，可能会利用这种观念为几乎任何行为辩护。我们必须拒绝这种逻辑，即使这种逻辑对某些参与者很有利。

I believe there is a pragmatic path through both of these.

我相信，我们可以找到一条务实道路，同时应对这两方面的问题。

For us, that means three principles.

对我们而言，这意味着三项原则。

First, as AI becomes more capable, people must remain at the center of AI decision-making. Alignment is not an abstract research question. It is the work of ensuring that these systems reliably remain under human control, reflect human values, and help people guide the next stages of development. We are not trying to, and must not, automate human judgment, or human values.

第一，随着人工智能能力不断增强，人类必须始终处于人工智能决策的中心。“对齐”（alignment）并不是一个抽象的研究问题，而是确保这些系统能够可靠地保持在人类控制之下、体现人类价值观，并帮助人类指导下一阶段发展的工作。我们并不是要，也绝不能，将人类判断或人类价值观自动化。

Second, the benefits of scientific progress and economic growth must be by people and for people. AI should help researchers make discoveries, doctors treat patients, teachers teach students, entrepreneurs start companies, communities solve problems that felt out of reach, and much more.

第二，科学进步和经济增长带来的收益必须属于人类，并服务于人类。人工智能应该帮助科研人员取得发现，帮助医生治疗患者，帮助教师教育学生，帮助创业者创建企业，帮助社区解决过去看似无法解决的问题，以及更多其他领域。
