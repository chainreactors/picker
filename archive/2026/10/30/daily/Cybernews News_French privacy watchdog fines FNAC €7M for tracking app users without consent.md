Key takeaways

1. 01

   France’s CNIL fined FNAC €7 million for tracking mobile app users without prior consent.
2. 02

   The app collected data such as advertising IDs, device details, IP addresses, and browsing history.
3. 03

   FNAC used the data for personalized ads and to train recommendation systems inside the app.
4. 04

   FNAC must add a consent tool within one month or risk €10,000 daily penalties.
