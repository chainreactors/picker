## **Threat Overview - ARTEX**

ARTEX is an open-source, AI-driven offensive framework originally observed in July 2026 that has been increasingly weaponized by financially motivated and espionage-oriented threat actors. Since August 2026, ARTEX has been observed targeting organizations across the United States, Europe, and Asia, including South Korea, with affected industries spanning financial services, government, technology, defense, healthcare, manufacturing, telecommunications, and energy. Unlike traditional intrusion frameworks, ARTEX utilizes multiple AI agents to automate reconnaissance, exploitation, privilege escalation, and post-compromise activity with limited human interaction. Furthermore, researchers have linked ARTEX-enabled operations to significant data theft, including the exposure of approximately 67,000 individuals' personal information at South Korean financial institutions and asupply chain compromise affecting more than 2,250 ConnectWise Cloud Backup tenants.
