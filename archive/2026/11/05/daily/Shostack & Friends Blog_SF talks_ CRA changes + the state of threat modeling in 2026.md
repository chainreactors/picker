# SF talks: CRA changes + the state of threat modeling in 2026

Adrienne Dandy, Shostack + Associates

Adam is speaking at both OWASP and ThreatModCon this week. Download the slides here.
![A header image for conferences with a full audience ready for a threat modeling talk on the right and space for talk names and venues on the left.](/images/blog/img/2026/OWASP-and-TMC-slide-header-1000w.png)

Adam is onstage [a few times this week](https://shostack.org/blog/updated-plans-for-owasp-and-threatmodcon-in-san-francisco/) and we have the slides for two of those talks ready for you now.

On Friday at 10:30am, he'll be on the OWASP main stage to discuss the impact of the EU’s Cyber Resilience Act for anyone who wants to sell their product in Europe. Get an advance peek at the [slides for CRA Will Be Cloud-Scale Engineering Change](/files/conference/CRA-owasp.pptx/) ahead of the talk.

On Saturday at 3:20pm, he'll be reflecting on the past decade and, more importantly, what the changes in threat modeling mean for where the field is headed and how to threat model now and in the future. Download the [slides for Threat Modeling: Designing for Security in an AI World](/files/conference/ThreatModCon26SanFrancisco_whats-changing-whats-next-Shostack.pptx/) ahead of the talk.
