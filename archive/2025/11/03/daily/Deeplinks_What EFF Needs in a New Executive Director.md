**By** [**Gigi Sohn**](https://www.eff.org/about/staff/gigi-sohn)**, Chair, EFF Board of Directors**

With the [impending departure](https://www.eff.org/press/releases/executive-director-cindy-cohn-will-step-down-after-25-years-eff) of longtime, renowned, and beloved Executive Director Cindy Cohn, EFF and leadership advisory firm [Russell Reynolds Associates](https://www.russellreynolds.com/en/) have developed [a profile for her successor](https://www.eff.org/document/eff-executive-director-position-specification).  While Cindy is irreplaceable, we hope that everyone who knows and loves EFF will help us find our next leader.

First and foremost, we are looking for someone who’ll meet this pivotal moment in EFF’s history. As authoritarian surveillance creeps around the globe and society grapples with debates over AI and other tech, EFF needs a forward-looking, strategic, and collaborative executive director to bring fresh eyes and new ideas while building on our past successes.

The San Francisco-based executive director, who reports to [our board of directors](https://www.eff.org/about/board#main-content), will have responsibility over all administrative, financial, development and programmatic activities at EFF.  They will lead a dedicated team of legal, technical, and advocacy professionals, steward EFF’s strong organizational culture, and ensure long-term organizational sustainability and impact. That means being:
