## Never Miss an Update.

AppSec research and analysis from Checkmarx, straight to your inbox.

**Checkmarx and OpenAI are bringing application security findings into OpenAI Daybreak Defense Factory, connecting trusted vulnerability detection with AI-powered investigation and remediation.**

AI is fundamentally changing how software gets built. Developers can generate, modify and ship code faster than ever. But more code also means more opportunities for vulnerabilities, creating an increasingly difficult backlog for security teams to manage.

Finding vulnerabilities is only part of the problem. Investigating findings, understanding their impact, determining the right remediation and validating fixes all take time. As AI accelerates development, the volume of vulnerabilities can grow faster than teams’ ability to address them.

**That’s the challenge Checkmarx and OpenAI are working together to solve.**

Through a new integration, organizations can bring Checkmarx findings into OpenAI Daybreak Defense Factory, connecting established application security analysis with a frontier lab platform.
