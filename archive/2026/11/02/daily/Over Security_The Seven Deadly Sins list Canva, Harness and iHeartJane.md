The Seven Deadly Sins, a data-extortion crew that says it does not use ransomware, has seven organisations on its leak site as of 15 October 2026: Canva, Harness, iHeartJane, Verano Holdings, Cradlewise, Globeia and Brazil’s Hospital Sírio-Libanês. The group claims more than 230 million rows across the seven, three of the entries describe Salesforce CRM exports, and Canva has confirmed that a vendor compromise gave attackers access to business contact and contract data through its Salesforce connection. Harness has since pointed us to a 9 September advisory confirming brief unauthorised access to its internal sales CRM through a compromised integration, with its product platform unaffected (see update below). The other five companies have not commented, and every figure below is the operator’s claim unless stated otherwise.

## Who are The Seven Deadly Sins?

The Seven Deadly Sins (TSDS) is a new extortion operation that takes data from cloud business applications and threatens publication, and says it does not use ransomware. The [operator profile on the Ransomtracker](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/group/the-seven-deadly-sins/) recorded all seven entries within five minutes on 15 October, which is when the index first captured the group’s site rather than when each victim was posted; the Canva entry, for example, dates from late September. The group told DataBreaches.net that it had received low-eight-figure ransom payments in the preceding month and that it holds access to more than 300 Jira instances and dozens of Salesforce, HubSpot and Zendesk tenants belonging to large companies. None of that has been verified, but the Canva case shows the method is real.
