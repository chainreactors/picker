# Our Lawsuit Against the Trump Administration’s Social Media Surveillance Moves Forward. Here’s What the Court Said.

![Security camera screens display logos for Facebook, YouTube, X, TikTok, and Reddit ](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/social-media-surveillance-2026.jpg?itok=cvzs7A7q)

No one should have to choose between speaking freely and protecting their immigration status. Yet that’s precisely the choice the Trump administration’s mass surveillance of online speech forces on noncitizens living in the U.S.

Last fall, we [sued](https://www.eff.org/press/releases/labor-unions-eff-sue-trump-administration-stop-surveillance-free-speech-online) on behalf of three major labor unions to stop this surveillance program. And just this month, we won our first major ruling: A federal district court [denied](https://www.eff.org/document/uaw-v-dos-opinion-order-denying-motion-dismiss) the government’s motion to dismiss our lawsuit. This means the case can move forward—a win for free speech and a critical step in protecting our First Amendment rights online. We’ve already explained why this is a [big deal](https://www.eff.org/press/releases/victory-court-rejects-government-effort-dismiss-social-media-surveillance-lawsuit) for our clients, so let's take a closer look at the court’s reasoning.

### **Our Lawsuit**

 Since taking power, the Trump administration has used AI and other automated tools to [broadly surveil](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/04/trump-administrations-targeting-international-students-jeopardizes-free-speech-and) the social media and online speech of visa and green card holders, with the goal of identifying and punishing people who express views it opposes. The government is targeting a broad range of views, including [criticism of the](https://theintercept.com/2025/02/11/ice-immigration-social-media-surveillance/) [Trump administration](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/mar/19/trump-musk-french-scientist-detained); [support for Palestine](https://www.axios.com/2025/03/06/state-department-ai-revoke-foreign-student-visas-hamas) or [criticism of Israel](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/07/18/us/politics/us-visa-revocations-israel.html); [“rationalizing or making light of”](https://apnews.com/article/trump-charlie-kirk-visas-revoked-455f43467c0c50e84d3857c1e9c81458) Charlie Kirk’s murder; and anything nebulously [“hostile”](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2025-02009.pdf) to American culture, [founding principles](https://www.kenklippenstein.com/p/exclusive-trump-admin-spies-on-social), [“traditional American views,”](https://www.aclu.org/news/national-security/how-nspm-7-seeks-to-use-domestic-terrorism-to-target-nonprofits-and-activists) or [“our way of life.”](https://www.facebook.com/uscis/posts/everyone-should-be-on-notice-if-youre-a-guest-in-our-country-a...
