# Two conference talks (slides)

[September 9, 2026September 9, 2026](https://dfir.ru/2026/09/09/two-conference-talks-slides/) ~ [Maxim Suhanov](https://dfir.ru/author/msuhanov/)

**Vulnerabilities in Full-Disk Encryption Systems** ([PDF](https://dfir.ru/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/maintrack_suhanov_offzone2026.pdf))

* Plaintext disclosure (re)discovered in BestCrypt Volume Encryption ([NotCVE-2026-0004](https://notcve.org/notcve/NotCVE-2026-0004), not fixed).
* Multiple BitLocker bypasses ([CVE-2024-43513](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/vulnerability/CVE-2024-43513) dubbed *“bitlockpick*”, [CVE-2025-21202](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/vulnerability/CVE-2025-21202) dubbed “*bitlockpick 2*”, [CVE-2026-20928](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/vulnerability/CVE-2026-20928) dubbed “*cold boot attack without cold and boot*”, [CVE-2025-21210 dubbed “CrashXTS”](https://dfir.ru/2025/01/20/cve-2025-21210-aka-crashxts-a-practical-randomization-attack-against-bitlocker/), [CVE-2025-21214](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/vulnerability/CVE-2025-21214) – a bug from the 90’s).
* [CVE-2025-21215](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/vulnerability/CVE-2025-21215) – turning the Windows boot loader into the keylogger to capture the BitLocker’s PIN.
* [CVE-2025-4382](https://lists.gnu.org/archive/html/grub-devel/2025-05/msg00042.html) in GRUB.
* One security vendor distributing a .reg file that disables the encryption of *hiberfil.sys* when using third-party software (like VeraCrypt).
* Our future: wide-block ciphers & modes, authenticated encryption (*operating systems are not ready here!*).

**I’ll hack you via a SYN packet** ([PDF](https://dfir.ru/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/fasttrack_suhanov_offzone2026.pdf))

* [CVE-2026-10817](https://support.citrix.com/support-home/kbsearch/article?articleNumber=CTX696604) in the TCP stack of NetScaler ADC and Gateway.
* Yes, attackers can leak decrypted data via a truncated TCP timestamp!
