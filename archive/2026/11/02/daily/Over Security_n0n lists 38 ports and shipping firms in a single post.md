The extortion group n0n added 38 port, terminal, shipping and logistics organisations to its leak site in a single batch on 12 October 2026, including Ocean Network Express, Santos Brasil, Pusan Newport Company, TTI Algeciras, Eurogate Tanger and NEOM Ports. Settlement demands reviewed by SOCRadar range from $2.4 million to $14 million, with a shared publication deadline of 19 October at 13:27 UTC. As of 16 October no listed company has confirmed an intrusion, and the data sets described are small enough that a single shared system, not 38 separate break-ins, is the leading explanation.

## Who is n0n?

n0n is a data-extortion operation that appeared in September 2026 and does not deploy encryption. The [n0n profile on the Ransomtracker](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/group/n0n/) holds 85 listings to date, which already places a one-month-old operation among the more prolific names on the [live leak-site index](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/). Its earlier listings, according to SOCRadar’s profile of the group, included Fanatics, Transcom WorldWide, AstraZeneca Türkiye and Argentina’s Ministry of Education; SOCRadar noted that Fanatics and Transcom had not confirmed a breach when its research was published.

Two habits distinguish the group. It publishes placeholders such as “Company #66” with a country flag before it names a target, so the index fills with masked entries that are later replaced. And its documented entry method is credentials harvested by infostealer malware, which is the same route we described in [stealer logs explained](https://ransomnews.com/stealer-logs-explained-2026/). SOCRadar notes that this method does not obviously explain the maritime batch, because stealer-log checks returned no credential matches for many of the organisations on it.
