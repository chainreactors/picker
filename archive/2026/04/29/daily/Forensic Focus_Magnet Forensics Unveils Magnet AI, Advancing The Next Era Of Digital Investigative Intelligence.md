# Magnet Forensics Unveils Magnet AI, Advancing The Next Era Of Digital Investigative Intelligence

29th April 202629th April 2026 by [MagnetForensics](https://www.forensicfocus.com/author/magnetforensics/ "View all posts by MagnetForensics")

![](https://www.forensicfocus.com/stable/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2026-04-21_69e7ae367c98c_MF_PressRelease_MagnetAI_tw_li_fb_1200x675.jpg)

*New capabilities transform digital evidence into investigative understanding, helping agencies surface the connections and context behind their cases.*

[Magnet Forensics](https://www.magnetforensics.com/?utm_source=ForensicFocus&utm_medium=PressRelease&utm_campaign=2026_MagnetOne_PR) introduces [Magnet AI](https://www.magnetforensics.com/magnet-ai/?utm_source=ForensicFocus&utm_medium=PressRelease&utm_campaign=2026_MagnetOne_PR), the intelligence engine powering AI-driven innovation across the Magnet Forensics ecosystem. Unveiled during the company’s executive keynote at [Magnet User Summit](https://www.magnetusersummit.com/?utm_source=ForensicFocus&utm_medium=PressRelease&utm_campaign=2026_MagnetOne_PR), Magnet AI represents a foundational shift in how artificial intelligence is applied to digital investigations — not only surfacing artifacts from vast datasets in seconds, but identifying patterns within the evidence, so investigators can focus on what matters most: interpreting evidence and advancing the path to justice, when every second counts.

Digital investigations are at a pivotal moment. Investigators are facing unprecedented volumes of data, increasing complexity, and growing pressure to move cases forward faster without sacrificing accuracy, defensibility, or trust. Agentic AI is emerging as a critical capability for meeting that moment, enabling investigators to identify connections, relationships, and patterns at scale.

> “From the beginning, our goal has been to empower investigators with solutions they can trust,” said Jad Saliba, Co‑Founder of Magnet Forensics. “With Magnet AI, we’re continuing our mission to lead innovation in this space by embedding intelligence across our platform in a way that reduces investigative friction and brings context to the evidence, while elevating human judgment and helping investigators reach the truth faster and with confidence.”

## Magnet AI: Intelligence With Intention

Built on nearly a decade of intentional innovation, Magnet AI is the intelligence layer that helps teams cut through noise, surface meaningful evidence, and focus their expertise on analyzing evidence.

**Intelligent Search**, recently launched within [Magnet Review](https://www.magnetforensics.com/products/magnet-review/?utm_source=ForensicFocus&utm_medium=PressRelease&utm_campaign=2026_MagnetOne_PR), delivers advanced search capabilities, powered by Magnet AI. Intelligent Search uses natural language and advanced AI to surface meaningful leads from thousands of media files, messages, audio, and video files in seconds — dramatically reducing the time spent manually filtering data and helping teams progress cases faster while reducing investigative risk.

## Introducing Intelligent Insights: From Artifacts to Understanding

Magnet Forensics is now taking that impact even further with the introduction of **Intelligent Insights,**powered by Magnet AI, the company’s most advanced AI capability to date, available in Magnet Review.

Purpose-built to help investigators quickly orient themselves within complex cases, Intelligent Insights goes beyond identifying artifacts to deliver the context behind the evidence. Investigators can enter a plain-language query and receive structured investigative summaries, key findings, and suggested lines of analysis — all grounded in verifiable citations linked directly to the source evidence.

> “Investigators don’t just need data — they need understanding,” Saliba said. “Intelligent Insights helps close the gap between having the pieces and seeing the picture, allowing teams to uncover the story behind the evidence and move cases forward with greater speed and confidence.”
