Have you ever seen a really creepy targeted ad online? One that revealed just how much these companies know about your life? It's unsettling enough to see how much companies know about you—but now we have confirmation that the government is also tapping the advertising surveillance machine to get your data. We're explaining [the dangers of targeted advertising](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/03/targeted-advertising-gives-your-location-government-just-ask-cbp) and location tracking, and the latest in the fight for privacy and free speech online, with our [EFFector newsletter](https://eff.org/effector/38/5).
