# New Release: Tor Browser 15.0.13

by [ma1](/author/ma1)
| May 8, 2026

![](/new-release-tor-browser-15013/lead.png)

Tor Browser 15.0.13 is now available from the [Tor Browser download page](https://www.torproject.org/download/) and also from our [distribution directory](https://www.torproject.org/dist/torbrowser/15.0.13/).

## Send us your feedback

If you find a bug or have a suggestion for how we could improve this release, [please let us know](https://support.torproject.org/misc/bug-or-feedback/).

## Full changelog

The [full changelog](https://gitlab.torproject.org/tpo/applications/tor-browser-build/-/raw/maint-15.0/projects/browser/Bundle-Data/Docs-TBB/ChangeLog.txt) since Tor Browser 15.0.12 is:

* All Platforms
  + Updated tor to 0.4.9.8
  + Updated NoScript to 13.6.19.1984
