# Hybrid work, expanded risk: what needs to change

## A practical look at securing identities, devices and applications wherever work happens

[David-Gordon](/Author/David-Gordon "Read more by this author")

Fri 3 Apr 2026  //
16:00 UTC

![](/design_picker/d2e337b97204af4aa34dda04c4e5d56d954b216f/graphics/icons/social_share_icon.svg)

Webinar Promo The shift to hybrid work has reshaped the enterprise perimeter. Users are logging in from home networks, shared spaces and unmanaged devices, while applications span on-prem systems and multiple clouds. Traditional security models were not designed for this level of fragmentation, leaving many organizations struggling to maintain visibility and control without adding friction.

This challenge matters now because the attack surface has expanded faster than most security strategies have evolved. Phishing, credential theft and lateral movement are harder to detect when users and workloads are dispersed. At the same time, businesses cannot afford to compromise user experience or productivity in the name of tighter controls. Security leaders are under pressure to strike a balance between protection and performance, often with limited resources.

This upcoming event explores how organizations can modernize their approach to securing the hybrid workplace. It focuses on practical frameworks and technologies that align security with the way people actually work today, rather than forcing outdated models onto new environments.

Attendees will gain a clearer understanding of how to reduce risk across users, devices and applications without introducing unnecessary complexity. The event will examine how to enforce consistent policies, improve threat detection and simplify access management across distributed environments. It will also highlight how integrated security approaches can help teams respond faster to incidents while maintaining a seamless user experience.

For IT and security professionals navigating hybrid work challenges, this event offers grounded insights rather than theory. Expect real-world perspectives on what works, what does not, and how to move forward with confidence.
