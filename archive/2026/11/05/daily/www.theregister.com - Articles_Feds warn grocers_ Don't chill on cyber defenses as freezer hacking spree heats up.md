If you have any industrial smart freezers, look out. The US federal government is urging owners and operators to disconnect them from the internet, apply patches, change default passwords, and take other security measures to prevent the kind of attacks that have become prevalent in Europe.

“We are seeing these systems targeted across critical infrastructure sectors globally, putting inventory and equipment at risk,” FBI Cyber Branch Operations Director Brett Leatherman said in a statement.

Since mid-October, unknown miscreants have [hacked thousands](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/15/researchers-warned-attackers-could-defrost-the-food-months-before-french-supermarkets-got-hit/5303425) of [remote-controlled refrigeration systems](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/11/02/frozen-part-ii-hackers-remotely-defrost-produce-in-euro-supermarkets-again/5306924) across[hundreds of grocery stores](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/15/researchers-warned-attackers-could-defrost-the-food-months-before-french-supermarkets-got-hit/5303425) in France, Belgium, Ireland, Luxembourg, and Sweden. The attacks [increase the temperature inside fridges](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/13/cyberattacks-crank-french-supermarket-refrigeration-systems-up-to-50c/5302784) to highs of 50ºC (122°F), spoiling food, and sparking fires.

REG AD

The Register asked the FBI if it has documented any successful or attempted attacks against refrigeration and cold chain monitoring systems in America. We had not received a response to our questions at the time of publication, nor are we aware of any documented digital attacks against these systems in American supermarkets.

REG AD

But there are signs that stateside operators are aware of the issue.

Dutch grocery giant Ahold Delhaize, which operates US supermarket chains Food Lion, Giant Food, and Stop & Shop, last month said it proactively [took IT systems offline](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/19/grocery-giant-ahold-delhaize-confirms-cyberattack-forced-us-and-european-systems-offline/5303970) following a cyberattack that affected most of its US and European brands.

Delhaize supermarkets in Europe are among those hit by the destructive freezer attacks.

The Pentagon is also [reportedly treating](https://www.reuters.com/technology/pentagon-treating-military-base-freezer-malfunctions-suspected-cyberattack-2026-10-29/) a series of freezer malfunctions that spoiled food at military bases across the US in August as a suspected cyberattack, according to Reuters, which cited four officials familiar with the matter.
