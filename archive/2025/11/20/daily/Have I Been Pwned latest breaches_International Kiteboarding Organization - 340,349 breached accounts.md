# International Kiteboarding Organization Data Breach

## What Happened

In November 2025, [the International Kiteboarding Organization suffered a data breach that exposed 340k user records](https://botcrawl.com/international-kiteboarding-organization-data-breach/). The data was subsequently listed for sale on a hacking forum and included email addresses, names, usernames and in many cases, the user's city and country.
