Don't mind the recent arrests. The ShinyHunters extortion gang may still be operating, just under the new name of Inception, according to Okta's threat intelligence group.

ShinyHunters is the brash cybercriminal group that, most recently, breached [rival gang Clop](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/09/21/clop-gets-a-taste-of-its-own-medicine-after-shinyhunters-hijack-leak-site/5297702) before [hacking the FBI](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/09/25/shinyhunters-tells-the-reg-we-hacked-the-fbi-to-protect-our-business/5299250), resulting in [at least three arrests](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/12/imagine-theres-no-shiny-hunters-ai-could-make-human-hacking-crews-obsolete/5302430) of [key suspects](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/05/fbi-confirms-multiple-arrests-related-to-shinyhunters-hack/5301178) in September and October. The crims - and their old data leak site - have since gone dark.

“While this threat cluster has changed their brand affiliation, the operators remain active in targeting organizations with voice phishing attacks and account takeovers resulting in data theft and extortion,” Okta threat hunters Moussa Diallo and Katie Nickels [said](https://www.okta.com/blog/threat-intelligence/former_shinyhunters_cluster_moves_to_new_inception_site/) on Monday.

REG AD

Okta tracks this threat cluster as O-UNC-053 - the “O-UNC” prefix stands for an Okta Uncategorized cluster, and it uses this label before formally attributing the activity to a named threat group.

REG AD

While Okta hasn't formally confirmed O-UNC-053 to be the same crime crew as ShinyHunters, the identity and access management provider’s researchers note that it does use nearly identical social engineering tactics: [voice phishing](https://www.theregister.com/special-features/2026/03/23/voice-phishing-skyrockets-as-smooth-crims-talk-their-way-in/5223759), during which the scammers trick targets into entering credentials into phishing pages and approving multi-factor authentication (MFA).

Plus, they note: O-UNC-053 operators change the themes of their phishing domains about every four to six weeks, and they also post claimed victims on ShinyHunters’ data leak site. In November, however, organizations targeted by O-UNC-053 began appearing on a new data leak site branded as Inception and not ShinyHunters.

The first five - Advanced Micro Devices, Cigna Healthcare, Paychex, PepsiCo, and Henry Schein - appeared November 17 on the site, and [all were marked “leaked.”](https://x.com/AlvieriD/status/2123008950874353993) Soon after, security maven Dominic Alvieri questioned if Inception was linked to ShinyHunters. “ShinyHunters are not all in jail,” he [wrote]...
