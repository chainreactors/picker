# SplitVPN Data Breach

## What Happened

In July 2026, [the Russian VPN service SplitVPN (previously known as NotVPN) suffered a data breach](https://securityaffairs.com/196197/security/vpn-breach-exposes-58-million-connection-logs-despite-no-logs-claims.html). The incident exposed millions of customer records, including 865k unique email addresses. Other impacted data included IP addresses, the user's country, and partial payment card data (first 6 and last 4 digits plus expiry date).

## Compromised Data

Device information

Email addresses

Geographic locations

IP addresses

Partial credit card data
