# American Tower Data Breach

## What Happened

In June 2026, telecommunications tower infrastructure company [American Tower was the target of a ShinyHunters "pay or leak" extortion campaign](https://x.com/H4ckmanac/status/2065383723739046213). The group subsequently published data allegedly taken from the company containing more than 200k unique email addresses belonging to employees, contractors, customers, and leads. Exposed data also included names, addresses, and phone numbers.
