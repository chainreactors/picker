Employee credentials captured by infostealer malware exist for **89.6% of the 1,856 large organisations** in our 36-country panel, and **55.5% of them gained at least one new stolen login** in the 30 days to 1 November 2026, up from 53.2% in October. The panel added 28,473 employee credentials in that window, 15% more than the month before. This is the first month-on-month edition of the Ransomnews Infostealer Exposure Dataset.

### What changed since October

* New employee credentials in the 30-day window rose from 24,826 to **28,473**, and the share of organisations that gained one in the final seven days jumped from 34.3% to **45.4%**.
* **Telefónica** added 1,152 employee credentials in the window, 897 of them in the last week of October, the largest single batch in either snapshot. It took Spain’s monthly total from 1,220 to 2,310.
* Universities kept climbing with the new academic year: 94.9% gained new credentials in the month and 86.1% in the final week, against 69.2% a month earlier.
* Vidar added more records to the index than any other family (132,257), ahead of LummaC2 (96,868) and Acreed (79,612). Acreed’s share rose by 0.36 points to 5.2%, the largest gain of any family.
* The headline rates barely moved: 1,663 organisations exposed against 1,659, a median of 32 credentials against 31. Exposure accumulates; it does not reset.
