## About EFF

The Electronic Frontier Foundation is the leading nonprofit defending civil liberties in the digital world. EFF’s work to protect your rights on the internet is supported by over 30,000 members who have [joined our mission](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/join-eff-4--wsds) by donating just this year.

For over 35 years, our lawyers, activists, and technologists have been thinking about the next big thing in tech before anyone else—whether that’s age verification, AI, or Palantir. Whatever causes you fight for, EFF protects the internet infrastructure you rely on to do so.

[JOIN EFF TODAY](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/join-eff-4--wsds)

To learn more about our work, [follow EFF on social media](https://www.eff.org/pages/where-find-eff) and subscribe to EFF's EFFector newsletter below to learn about the ways the internet and online rights are changing and what that means for you. And join EFF to support our fight—because if you use technology, this fight is yours.
