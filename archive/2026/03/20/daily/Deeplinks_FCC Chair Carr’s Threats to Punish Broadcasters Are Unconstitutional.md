EFF joined other digital rights and civil liberties organizations in [calling out the unconstitutionality of](https://techfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Response-to-FCC-Chair-Brendan-Carr-March-2026.pdf)[Federal Communications Commission chair](https://techfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Response-to-FCC-Chair-Brendan-Carr-March-2026.pdf) Brendan Carr’s recent threats to punish broadcasters for airing statements he disagrees with.
