# Breach of Confidence: 07 August 2026

[j4vv4d](https://javvadmalik.com/author/j4vv4d/)

[blog](https://javvadmalik.com/category/blog/)

August 7, 2026
2 Minutes

![Breach of Confidence](https://i0.wp.com/j4vv4d.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/breach_of_confidence_header.png?w=1100&ssl=1)

I’ve spent the week explaining to people that stateless protocols are not a commentary on government, and I’m not sure I’ve convinced anyone.

**One Protocol To Rule Them All**

MCP 2.0 has gone stateless. One request instead of two, no handshake, no server keeping track of who you were three seconds ago. It scales because it doesn’t have to remember anything, and smaller models can actually drive it without falling over. The best architecture is the one that forgets you immediately.

[https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/31/stateless-mcp/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/31/stateless-mcp/%20)

**Nobody’s Problem Is Everybody’s Problem**

The AI agent security argument has reached peak absurdity. Every vendor claims they own it, every team insists it’s their remit, and the actual answer is that nobody does. Which is exactly why it’s going to become everyone’s mess to clean up in about six months when something spectacularly stupid happens.

<https://api.cyfluencer.com/s/understanding-the-ai-agent-security-ecosystem-roles-responsibilities-and-where-runtime-authority-fits-28828>

**Long Live The Dinosaurs**

Dinosaurs had a 165-million-year run. Mayflies get a day if they’re lucky. Yet we keep treating “legacy” like it’s a disease and “new” like it’s a cure. Sometimes the thing that’s been working since the Cretaceous period is working precisely because it survived the Cretaceous period.

<https://blog.knowbe4.com/what-security-can-learn-from-dinosaurs>

**Your Calendar Is Now A Dead Drop**

HOLLOWGRAPH turns Microsoft 365 calendars into command and control infrastructure. Commands arrive as calendar events scheduled for 2099, exfiltrated data leaves as encrypted attachments, and it all flows through Microsoft Graph like legitimate traffic because technically it is. Never touches attacker infrastructure. Never needs to. Your productivity suite is doing the work for them.

<https://cybersec.picussecurity.com/s/hollowgraph-backdoor-turns-microsoft-365-calendars-into-a-c2-channel-28829>

**The Archive They Actually Released**

Hungary’s state news agency staff spent a decade documenting every political order, every directive to bury stories, every instruction on how to manufacture consent. Then they published all 500 pages of it. Either it’s the most extraordinary act of institutional transparency in modern European media, or someone’s playing a game so long nobody’s figured out the rules yet.

<https://telex.hu/english/2026/08/04/nearly-500-page-document-compiled-by-hungary-s-state-news-service-staff-details-how-news-was-manipulated-during-orban-era>

**The Fraud Detector From 1938**

Benford’s Law caught accountants making up numbers before computers existed. Now it catches AI making up pixels. It’s one signal among six, not a silver bullet, but there’s something satisfying about using statistical patterns from the 1930s to spot synthetic imagery in 2026.

<https://kennethbspringer.au/2026/08/05/benfords-law-detecting-ai-generated-images-without-ai/>

**The Agentic Division**

Meta’s AI model apparently hacked another company during testing. Not a simulation. Not a sandbox. An actual target. The AI arms race now has a category for models that go off-script and start pentesting the neighbourhood without asking.

<https://www.reuters.com/technology/metas-ai-model-hacked-another-company-during-testing-information-reports-2026-08-05/>

**Schrödinger’s Backdoor**

Zbtlink says their routers definitely don’t have backdoors but they’re pulling all the firmware downloads to fix unspecified security issues just to be safe. The confidence required to deny and patch simultaneously is genuinely impressive.

<https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/06/chinese-router-vendor-denies-its-firmware-contains-backdoors-but-pauses-downloads-to-fix-security-issues-anyway/5283794>

**The Memory Shortage Nobody’s Mentioning**

Memory manufacturers have already sold out 2027’s entire production. The buyers aren’t saying much because admitting you’re panic-buying HBM makes you look desperate, and looking desperate makes the price go up even more.

<https://www.digitimes.com.tw/tech/dt/n/shwnws.asp?CnlID=1&id=0000763847_DVY7YHX65GMLYZ6UQEEMP>

—

That’s your week. If any of this made you laugh, wince, or forward it to someone with “told you so” in the subject line, you can find me on some social media platform making the same observations in real time.

Stay cynical.
