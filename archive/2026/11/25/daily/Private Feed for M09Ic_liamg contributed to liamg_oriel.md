Owner

Switching to a request's tab left focus on its conversation, so you had to click the reply box before typing.

* The reply field (`Write to the request`) is now the request pane's default focus target (`ProseOpts.DefaultFocus`) whenever there's a request to reply to. The toolkit takes focus there when the tab opens or is switched back to (`panes` → `Ctx.FocusInto`). The conversation stays the default on the "new request" screen.
* `Esc` in the field still goes to the requests list, where j/k/o/R work.
* Help: agent/requests, Composer.

## Test

`TestARequestsTabFocusesItsReplyField` (`app/agentui`): open a request and the field is focused. Bring a file's tab to the front, click the request's tab again, and the field is focused, so what's typed is the reply. It fails without the change. Snapshots of the request view regenerated: the reply field now shows focused.
