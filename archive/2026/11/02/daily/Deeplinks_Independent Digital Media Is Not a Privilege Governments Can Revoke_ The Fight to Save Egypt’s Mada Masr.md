# Independent Digital Media Is Not a Privilege Governments Can Revoke: The Fight to Save Egypt’s Mada Masr

![Offline General](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/offline-general.png?itok=Fyjuy2Um "Offline General")

On November 11, Lina Attalah, founder and editor-in-chief of the independent Egyptian news outlet [Mada Masr](https://www.madamasr.com/en/), will face trial for the second time in just two weeks. This time, she faces charges of spreading false news, an accusation that has become one of the Egyptian government's most frequently used tools for prosecuting journalists.

The trial follows a conviction on October 27, when Attalah was [found guilty](https://www.madamasr.com/en/2026/10/27/news/u/lina-attalah-in-custody-after-court-issues-le3-mn-fine-orders-mada-masrs-closure/) of managing an unlicensed news website. The Mada Masr team had repeatedly applied for a license, but their attempts went unacknowledged. The proceedings took place in a single hearing, which Mada Masr [states](https://www.instagram.com/p/DfM44U5m32q/?mdxt=MXI2aWJiaTE5YzJ5bg==) included serious violations of her right to a fair trial. She was sentenced to the maximum fine of 3 million Egyptian pounds (approximately 55,000 USD), forcibly removed from the courtroom, and held for seven hours until the fine was paid. The court also ordered Mada Masr's closure and the confiscation of all equipment belonging to the organization.

The current wave of repression has also targeted [Matsada2sh](https://matsda2sh.com/en), a fact-checking and open-source intelligence initiative. Its entire six-person newsroom was [violently arrested](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/04/egyptian-journalist-faces-terrorism-charges-entire-newsroom-detained-matsadaash-press-freedom), illegally detained, and interrogated under torture before being charged with terrorism-related offenses and spreading false news. The team remains in pretrial detention.

According to CPJ, at least [25 other journalists](https://cpj.org/data/imprisoned/currently-imprisoned/?status=Imprisoned&cc_fips[]=EG&group_by=location&currently-imprisoned=1) are currently incarcerated in Egypt.
