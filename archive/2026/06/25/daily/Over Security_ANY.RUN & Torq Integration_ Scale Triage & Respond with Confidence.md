ANY.RUN & Torq Integration: Scale Triage & Respond with Confidence

**Lack of alert context** makes it difficult for Security Operations Centers (SOC) to distinguish actual threats from false positives. [**ANY.RUN**](https://any.run/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=torq-integration&utm_term=250626&utm_content=linktolanding)’s integration with the [**Torq AI SOC Platform**](https://torq.io/ai-soc-platform/) bridges this gap by delivering conclusive malware & phishing verdicts and actionable intelligence.

**The result for your team is faster incident resolution**, reduced alert fatigue, and proactive threat detection.

## ANY.RUN & Torq Integration

Unlike legacy SOAR approaches that often require custom code and months of implementation, **Torq** allows [SOC](https://any.run/enterprise/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=torq-integration&utm_term=250626&utm_content=linktoenterprise) and [MSSP teams](https://any.run/mssp/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=torq-integration&utm_term=250626&utm_content=linktomssp) to build response logic visually. The ANY.RUN integration adds a critical layer of malware analysis, phishing detection, and IOC enrichment to these workflows.

**ANY.RUN** users have access to **five ready-to-use**[**Torq HyperAgents™**](https://torq.io/hyperagents/) designed to accelerate time-to-verdict:

* [**Threat Intelligence Enrichment with TI Lookup**](https://kb.torq.io/en/articles/15027756-workflow-template-enrich-case-with-threat-intelligence-data-any-run-ti-lookup)

* [**File & URL Analysis with Interactive Sandbox**](https://kb.torq.io/en/?q=any.run)

Results, including reputation data, threat names, tags, and structured JSON responses, are delivered directly into [**Torq Case Management.**](https://torq.io/case-management/) Teams can edit the current templates to fit their specific processes, adding actions, changing conditions, or using ANY.RUN as one specific step in a complex, multi-tool automation.

Available on [ANY.RUN Threat Intelligence](https://any.run/plans-ti/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=torq-integration&utm_term=250626&utm_content=linktotipricing) and [Interactive Sandbox plans](https://any.run/plans/?utm_source=anyrunblog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=torq-integration&utm_term=250626&utm_content=linktosbpricing) **with API access**, the integration helps analysts streamline their workflows, gaining full alert or threat context quickly with an average reduction in MTTR of 21 minutes.
