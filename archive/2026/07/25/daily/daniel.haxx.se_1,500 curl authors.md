# 1,500 curl authors

[July 25, 2026](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/07/25/1500-curl-authors/) [Daniel Stenberg](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/author/daniel/) [Leave a comment](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/07/25/1500-curl-authors/#respond)

It takes a village to make curl. A rather big village.

I have not been a solo maintainer of curl for a long time and I don’t even do half of the commits anymore

Since today, the [curl git repository](https://github.com/curl/curl) holds the accumulated efforts from 1,500 separate and named individuals. Only 4.5 years since we passed [1,000](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2022/01/30/1000-commit-authors/). Yay for us!

Author 1,500 turned out to be Sameeh Jubran who authored [this](https://github.com/curl/curl/commit/a55731050e8c3dbea0b96205cf916443118f6acb).
