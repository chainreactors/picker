## Session videos from Jamf Nation User Conference 2025 now available

November 11, 2025
[rtrouton](https://derflounder.wordpress.com/author/rtrouton/ "Posts by rtrouton") [Leave a comment](#respond)
[Go to comments](#comments)

[Jamf](https://www.jamf.com) has posted the session videos for from [Jamf Nation User Conference 2025](https://reg.jnuc.jamf.com/flow/jamf/jnuc2025/home25/page/sessioncatalogphase2), including the video for my “[MDM and DDM 101](https://youtu.be/SJpYEdNNdG4?si=J5s7z40Gxs15n_2K)” session.

For those interested, [all of the the JNUC 2025 session videos are available on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/%40JAMFMedia/videos), though it doesn’t appear that there’s a dedicated YouTube playlist at this point. For convenience, I’ve linked my session here.
