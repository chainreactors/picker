## Name that Ware, November 2026

The Ware for November 2026 is shown below.

[![](https://bunniefoo.com/ntw/ntw_nov_2026_sm.jpg)](https://bunniefoo.com/ntw/ntw_nov_2026.jpg)

I felt a little nostalgic, as I always do around this time of year. I picked an easy-to-guess ware, but one from that era when I anticipated the holiday season the way a child would. This one was plucked from a trove I found recently – a board I distinctly remember being quite curious about, but which had been kept safely out of reach because back then it was expensive and my father didn’t want me pulling the chips off and breaking it (and apparently it has remained hidden there ever since!).

I find it interesting that even back then, some chips had their part numbers ground off to discourage cloning. Also, note the Foxconn-branded connectors, reflecting the company’s origin story as a manufacturer of discount connector clones. I think this board dates from a vintage when Foxconn was still mostly making connectors and only just starting to diversify into other lines of business.

   Throw in the 40-pin header which would likely be an early IDE interface, and a whopping 1MB of RAM expansion, apparently with parity!
