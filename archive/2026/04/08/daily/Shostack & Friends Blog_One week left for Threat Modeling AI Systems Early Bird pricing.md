# One week left for Threat Modeling AI Systems Early Bird pricing

One week left to take advantage of Early Bird pricing for our new Threat Modeling AI Systems course.
![Banner for new Threat Modeling AI Systems Course from Shostack + Associates](/images/blog/img/2026/TMAISystems-banner-1474w.png)

Early Bird pricing is still available for Shostack + Associates’ newest course, Threat Modeling AI Systems but it will end on April 15, 2026. This in-depth technical course, designed for security professionals already familiar with threat modeling, focuses on the unique challenges that AI brings to modern applications.

Michael Novack and Shoshana Cox will deliver Threat Modeling AI Systems course in May 2026 in Washington DC. [Register soon](https://courses.shostack.org/courses/Threat-Modeling-AI-Systems-225) to receive early pricing!

You can find a list of all our upcoming open courses on our [Training page](https://shostack.org/training), including in-person courses at OWASP Global Appsec EU and BlackHat USA 2026.
