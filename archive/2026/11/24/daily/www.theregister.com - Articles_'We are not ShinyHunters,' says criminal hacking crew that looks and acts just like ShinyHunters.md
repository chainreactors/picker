Inception, a new data theft and extortion gang that security researchers have theorized is a rebrand of the ShinyHunters group, “reject[s] this attribution” and claims it has no affiliation with any prior cybercrime enterprise.

In a lengthy statement posted Tuesday and [shared](https://x.com/DarkWebInformer/status/2125239229462495608) on X, the extortionists insist that they are a new group, with new operators, and that their alleged social engineering attacks aren’t copying anyone - but rather just abusing the weakest link in an organization’s security: “a human being holding a phone.”

The rant follows a Monday report by Okta Threat Intelligence - along with social media speculation since Inception’s inception last week - that the newly emerged group is very likely [ShinyHunters with a different name](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/23/inception-possibly-a-shinyhunters-reboot-claims-seven-victims-in-a-week/5311768).

Okta’s researchers based their analysis on the two groups’ shared infrastructure, plus Inception’s nearly identical tactics: [voice phishing](https://www.theregister.com/special-features/2026/03/23/voice-phishing-skyrockets-as-smooth-crims-talk-their-way-in/5223759), during which the scammers trick targets into entering credentials into phishing pages and approving multi-factor authentication (MFA) requests.

REG AD

Inception apparently took offense to this. “We would like to address this once, clearly, and only once,” the crims' statement says. “We are not ShinyHunters. We were never ShinyHunters. We are Inception.”

The gang argues that its overlapping domain registrars, hosting providers, and DNS services don’t prove anything. These are commodity services, “available to anyone who can pay for them,” Inception wrote.

Plus, their similar methodologies don't necessarily make Inception a reboot. “That is not theft of tradecraft,” they wrote. “It is acknowledgment of a good idea – the same way every group after LockBit used a leak site, and nobody called them LockBit.”

“Inception is a new group, with new operators, our own objectives, and our own name,” the statement continues. “What we share with the past is plumbing.”

To be fair, the new crime crew has every reason to distance itself from ShinyHunters.
