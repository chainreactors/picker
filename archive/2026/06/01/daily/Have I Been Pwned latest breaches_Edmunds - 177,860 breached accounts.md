# Edmunds Data Breach

## What Happened

In January 2026, the automotive research and car-shopping platform [Edmunds was listed by the ShinyHunters hacking group as having been breached](https://cybernews.com/security/edmunds-data-breach-shiny-hunters/). Data purportedly obtained in the incident was later published publicly and included 178k unique email addresses, usernames, passwords, IP addresses, phone numbers and vehicle-related records.
