# Weekly Update 526: Building HIBP AI/SI First

19 October 2026

I have this sense that we've passed the point where "vibe coding" is a derogatory term. I have no empirical evidence for this; it's more a feeling that those weaponising the term have now accepted the inevitability and are just getting with the program. Or they've reskilled into other industries because, let's face it: you can't be a productive, *competitive* developer today without AI. I saw this comment from Linus Torvalds today which I thought really nailed it:

> ‼️ Linus Torvalds called rules against AI coding tools "UTTERLY STUPID" on the Linux kernel mailing list.
> He says people he respects and calls intelligent do not shy away from using new tools. https://t.co/HaQ8H4BrVc
>
> — International Cyber Digest (@IntCyberDigest) [October 17, 2026](https://x.com/IntCyberDigest/status/2111592082297270339?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw&ref=troyhunt.com)

He's always had a way with words 🙂 Anyway, here's what we're doing:
