[The Gentlemen](https://ransomnews.com/ransomtracker/group/the-gentlemen/) ransomware group listed NVIDIA on its leak site on 18 November 2026, with no sample files and a countdown that runs out on 28 November. NVIDIA has not confirmed a breach. The same site already lists two companies that work with the chipmaker: heat-spreader supplier Jentech Precision Industrial, named on 6 October, and IT services firm FPT Software, named on 27 October.

### In brief

* **The claim:** NVIDIA appeared on The Gentlemen’s leak site on 18 November 2026. The entry has no samples, no file list and no data volume.
* **The deadline:** a countdown on the entry runs out on the morning of 28 November, UTC.
* **NVIDIA’s position:** the company has not confirmed a breach, and Ransomnews found no public statement about the listing.
* **The partners:** the gang listed NVIDIA supplier Jentech on 6 October, claiming more than 9TB of data, and FPT Software on 27 October.
* **The contact:** the negotiation ID on the NVIDIA entry matches one that Check Point Research attributed to the group’s administrator.
* **The record:** The Gentlemen have posted 1,107 listings in 2026. Ransomnews has confirmed 91 attacks by the group this year from public sources.
