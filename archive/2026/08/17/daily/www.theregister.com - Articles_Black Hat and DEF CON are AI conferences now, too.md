# Black Hat and DEF CON are AI conferences now, too

On this week's episode of The Reg's Kettle podcast, we revisit 'hacker summer camp,' where the hottest topic was ... sigh... agentic AI

Brandon Vigliarolo
[Brandon
Vigliarolo](https://www.theregister.com/author/brandon-vigliarolo)
GOVERNMENT AND IT NEWS REPORTER

Published
mon 17 Aug 2026 // 08:30 UTC

KETTLE Our cybersecurity editor [Jessica Lyons](https://www.theregister.com/author/jessica-lyons) spent last week in Las Vegas for the Black Hat and DEF CON security conferences, and at both events there was only one thing on everyone's mind: AI agents and their growing threat to cybersecurity defenders.

You can listen to the latest episode of The Kettle right here on this page, as well as on[Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2wQ73I0IszPVy6J8MabObT)[,](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0GDsvVSe00Jsax9tMIRwWs) [Apple Music](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/black-hat-and-def-con-are-ai-conferences-now-too/id1882523636?i=1000783708194)[,](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-ai-bubble-is-popping-we-just-dont-know-it-yet/id1882523636?i=1000779587481) or [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G0GOcTh3Cf4). Those platforms also let you subscribe to Kettle, so you are always notified when the latest episode goes live.

As Jess wrote this week, pretty much every discussion she had last week centered around AI and its [potential effects](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/07/asimov-was-right-about-rules-for-robots-says-ex-us-cyber-director/5284397) on [critical infrastructure](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/14/autonomous-ai-attacks-pose-clear-and-present-danger-to-critical-infrastructure/5287594), with multiple current and former government leaders expressing worry over [recent events](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/07/water-system-controllers-dont-belong-on-the-internet-says-ex-nsa-chief-after-suspected-iran-attacks/5285070) and what they mean for the future of infosec.

REG AD

Join Jess and host [Brandon Vigliarolo](https://www.theregister.com/author/brandon-vigliarolo) for this week's episode of The Kettle, where they break down the [hacker summer camp](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/04/this-one-time-at-hacker-summer-camp/5282999) scuttlebutt and what the security world is doing to protect critical infrastructure from the emerging AI threat.
