# СберСпасибо (SberSpasibo) Data Breach

## What Happened

In March 2023, [data allegedly taken from СберСпасибо (SberSpasibo), the loyalty program of Russian bank Сбербанк (Sberbank), was published online](https://habr.com/ru/articles/721368/). The data contained records relating to 48M users, including 3.3M unique email addresses alongside phone numbers and dates of birth. Data also included bank card numbers stored as unsalted SHA-1 hashes, plus loyalty program details such as membership dates and the redemption of bonus points for partner offers.

## Compromised Data

Bank card numbers

Dates of birth

Email addresses

Loyalty program details

Phone numbers
