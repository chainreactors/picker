# ANOM – The FBI’s Secret Encrypted Phone Firm for Criminals

Published November 20, 2026 |

Views: 152

ANOM was an encrypted phone company secretly run by the FBI, whose devices were sold to criminal groups while a hidden copy of every message went to police, as the Justice Department revealed in June 2021.

![ANOM: The FBI's secret encrypted phone company for criminals, beside a black phone showing a calculator-style keypad with one orange key.](https://www.darknet.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/anom-fbi-encrypted-phone-trojan-shield-1024x576.webp)

ANOM, the encrypted phone company the FBI secretly ran for criminal groups.

This Stickler Files episode follows those copies into court, where judges in Germany, Finland and Australia had to decide whether they could be used as evidence. Finland’s Supreme Court found them unlawfully obtained, and admissible.

## Key highlights

* The Justice Department says ANOM filled the gap left by Phantom Secure, an encrypted phone firm the FBI dismantled in 2018. According to court documents as reported, a Phantom Secure developer had been recruited as a confidential source.
* Motherboard reporters who examined one handset found that, under a second PIN, its calculator app opened a login screen instead.
* By the Justice Department’s count, more than 12,000 ANOM devices and services were sold to more than 300 criminal syndicates in over 100 countries.
* The platform was taken down on 7 June 2021. The next day the Justice Department reported 800 arrests, by its June 2021 count.
* In August 2023 a court in Memmingen, Germany, acquitted a man after ruling, as reported, that it could not use the messages, because the country that collected them and its court orders stayed secret.

## How the FBI’s ANOM phones worked

An Australian High Court judgment describes what one of its judges, Justice Edelman, called the operation’s “one ingenious feature”. Unknown to the user, pressing send created a second, secret copy of the message, with some data from the phone, which went to a relay server called iBot and on to the police. The FBI’s own copies came through a relay server in a third country: according to the unsealed court papers as reported, from October 2019 they reached the FBI three times a week, under a court order there and a mutual legal assistance treaty.

## Questions answered

### Which country helped the FBI collect ANOM messages?

US court documents called it only an unnamed European Union member state that had asked to stay confidential. 404 Media later reported, from a source briefed on the operation, that it was Lithuania. Lithuania’s prosecutors declined to comment, and the FBI has maintained that Operation Trojan Shield was conducted lawfully.

### Why did a German court acquit when a higher court ruled ANOM messages usable?

They were different cases. In January 2025, in a case from Tübingen, Germany’s Federal Court of Justice ruled that gaps in what anyone knew about how the data was gathered, and about the court orders behind it, did not stop the messages being used.

### Did Australia’s High Court say the FBI acted lawfully?

No. In October 2025 it answered whether a 2024 Act of Parliament, which treated communications covered by specified warrants as not intercepted, was invalid, and its answer was no. With that law standing, the original interception argument could no longer change the outcome, so the court withdrew permission for that appeal without deciding it.
