SAN FRANCISCO—Privacy Badger, the [award-winning](https://blog.mozilla.org/addons/2025/12/17/presenting-2025-firefox-extension-developer-award-recipients/) tracker blocker made by the member-supported digital rights nonprofit [Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://www.eff.org/) (EFF), is now available for Safari on macOS.

Safari users can [download the free browser extension](https://privacybadger.org/) today and join the more than 4 million people using Privacy Badger to take a stand against data brokers and online surveillance. Users will need Safari version 26.5 or higher to use it.

Ranked as one of the best ad tracker blockers by [The Wirecutter](https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter/reviews/our-favorite-ad-blockers-and-browser-extensions-to-protect-privacy/), [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/electronics/digital-security/to-protect-against-websites-that-spy-on-you-get-an-adblocker-a1003441908/), [PC Mag](https://www.pcmag.com/picks/best-ad-blockers) and more, Privacy Badger is different from other blocking extensions. Created by EFF to stop companies from spying on you online, Privacy Badger [automatically learns](https://privacybadger.org/#How-is-Privacy-Badger-different-from-Disconnect%2c-Adblock-Plus%2c-Ghostery%2c-and-other-blocking-extensions) to block hidden trackers on the web, along with additional privacy-protecting features.

"Most websites contain invisible tracking code that lets companies collect, share, and profit from your personal data," said Lena Cohen, Staff Technologist at EFF. "Creepy ads are just the tip of the iceberg when it comes to the harms of online tracking: Once your information [enters the data broker ecosystem](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/03/online-tracking-out-control-privacy-badger-can-help-you-fight-back), it’s nearly impossible to know who buys it or what they’re doing with it. Safari support has long been one of Privacy Badger’s most requested features and EFF is excited to be able to make this important tool available to Safari users...
