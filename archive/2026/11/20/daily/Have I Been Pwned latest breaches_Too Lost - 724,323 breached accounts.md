# Too Lost Data Breach

## What Happened

In November 2026, an unauthorised party claimed to have obtained data from the music distributor for independent artists, Too Lost. The data contained 724k unique email addresses belonging to artists, their collaborators and fans. Some records also contained bcrypt password hashes, while a small subset included partial credit card data (last 4 digits and card type), identity verification information (in some cases, passport, driver's licence or national ID numbers), and payouts made to artists. Too Lost advised that its investigation remains ongoing, external forensic specialists have been engaged, and measures have been taken to secure affected systems and credentials. The company is also working with advisors to coordinate communications and support for affected individuals.
