## Companies Can Locate Any Device at Any Time

### Date Published

June 15, 2026

### Topics

[Targeted Surveillance](https://citizenlab.ca/focus-area/targeted-surveillance/)

[telecommunications](https://citizenlab.ca/topic/telecommunications/)

### Mentions

[Swantje Lange](https://citizenlab.ca/person/swantje-lange/)

Share

Hasso Plattner Institute and Citizen Lab doctoral fellow Swantje Lange [spoke](https://www.tagesspiegel.de/wissen/uberwachungsfirmen-konnen-ihr-handy-jederzeit-orten-sicherheitsforscher-weisen-spionage-im-handynetz-nach-15678720.html) with *Tagesspiegel* about the Lab’s recent research on telecom surveillance campaigns*.* The [report](https://citizenlab.ca/research/uncovering-global-telecom-exploitation-by-covert-surveillance-actors/) exposes how commercial surveillance vendors exploit vulnerabilities in the global telecom interconnect ecosystem to track targets.

While it is “mainly people like journalists, politicians, or opposition figures”  who are targeted, the problem is still “societally relevant,” says Lange.

[Read here](https://www.tagesspiegel.de/wissen/uberwachungsfirmen-konnen-ihr-handy-jederzeit-orten-sicherheitsforscher-weisen-spionage-im-handynetz-nach-15678720.html)

In collaboration with the International Consortium of Investigative Journalists (ICIJ), we identified two distinct actors aligned with the People’s Republic of China that have been targeting and impersonating journalists and civil society. Our findings provide insight into the Chinese government’s practice of digital transnational repression and its shift to a system of state-sponsored attacks carried out by private contractors.
