# New Release: Tails 7.16

by [tails](/author/tails)
| October 30, 2026

![](/new-release-tails-7_16/lead.jpg)

## Changes and updates

* Update *Tor Browser* to [16.0](https://blog.torproject.org/new-release-tor-browser-160/).

*Tor Browser* 16.0 is based on *Firefox* 153 and inherits its [redesigned
settings](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/firefox-settings/).

[![](https://tails.net/doc/anonymous_internet/Tor_Browser/security-levels.png)](https://tails.net/doc/anonymous_internet/Tor_Browser/security-levels.png)

* Update the *Tor* client to 0.4.9.15.

## Fixed problems

* Fix OpenPGP support in *Thunderbird*. ([#21841](https://gitlab.tails.net/tails/tails/-/work_items/21841))

## Get Tails 7.16

### To upgrade your Tails USB stick and keep your Persistent Storage

* Automatic upgrades are available from Tails 7.0 or later to 7.16.
* If you cannot do an automatic upgrade or if Tails fails to start after an automatic upgrade, please try to do a [manual upgrade](https://tails.net/doc/upgrade/#manual).

### To install Tails 7.16 on a new USB stick

Follow our [installation instructions](https://tails.net/install/).

The Persistent Storage on the USB stick will be lost if you install instead of
upgrading.

### To download only

If you don't need installation or upgrade instructions, you can download Tails
7.16 directly:

* [For USB sticks (USB image)](https://tails.net/install/download/)
* [For DVDs and virtual machines (ISO image)](https://tails.net/install/download-iso/)
