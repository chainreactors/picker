After the cyber attacks timelines ([part I](https://www.hackmageddon.com/2026/04/24/1-15-april-2026-cyber-attacks-timeline/) and [part II](https://www.hackmageddon.com/2026/05/08/16-30-april-2026-cyber-attacks-timeline/)), it’s time to publish the statistics for April 2026 where I collected and analyzed 202 events: a sharp decrease compared to the 282 events of the [previous month](https://www.hackmageddon.com/2026/04/16/march-2026-cyber-attacks-statistics/).

In April 2026, **Cyber Crime** continued to lead the **Motivations** chart with **65%**, nearly in line with March when it was 64; once again ahead of **Cyber Espionage** up to **22%** from 15%. **Cyber Warfare took** the third place with **5%**, up from 3%, and **Hacktivism** closes the chart with **3%**down from 6%.

As always, **Malware** led the **Attack Techniques** with **45**%, up from 43% of the previous month, once again followed by **Account Takeovers** with **21%** (up from 19%) and **Ransomware**, at the same level of March (**9%**).

A direct comparison with the previous month is not possible for the **Initial Access** vectors since I changed the methodology, however in April the **Exploitation of Public-facing Applications** (**T1190**) was the most used technique for initial access with **22%**, followed by generic **Phishing** (**T1566**) with **9%** and **Spearphishing Attachments** (**T1566.01**) and **Spearphishing Links** (**T1566.02**), both of them with **8%**. It is clear that combining all the flavors, **Phishing** was the main initial access vector.

The **Distribution of Targets** chart was led by organizations in the **Information & Communication** space with **30%** (up from 18%, another consequence of the change of methodology), ahead of **Public Administration** with **23%** (last month ranked at number one with **21%**), and **Finance and** **Insurance**, slightly up to **12%** from 10%.
