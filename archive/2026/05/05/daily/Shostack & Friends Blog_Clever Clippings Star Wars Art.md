# Clever Clippings Star Wars Art

Showcasing some Star Wars art to celebrate Revenge of the Fifth
![Star Wars art](/images/blog/img/2026/clever-clippings-1000w.png)

On a recent trip, I had the joy of discovering Selena’s [Clever
Clippings](https://www.cleverclippings.com), as she puts it, “Fine art for nerdy souls.”

Each print has a collage on the front and an explanation on the back, and the
explanations speak to me. For example, the one for [Vader: Let Go](https://www.cleverclippings.com/products/anakin-let-go) (shown,
left) begins:
> Grief unfolds in layers over time, comes in waves over
> time, and the essence lies in letting go. This collage
> captures the journey of dealing with resurfacing sorrow.
> Anakin’s first lesson on his path to becoming a Jedi was learning not to mourn his loss of his mother, and this sets him on the path to becoming Darth Vader. His biggest mistake was seeing tragedy as something that could be avoided or fixed, rather than something to be endured or weathered like a storm. How would Anakin’s life have been different if he was given a natural way to express his longing for his mother?

That’s very appropriate for today’s Revenge of the Fifth, and so I
wanted to share her work. I also appreciate how she’s grounded in
the story, and doesn’t show a slavish devotion to it.

What's more, her work is very reasonably priced. Pray she doesn’t alter the terms of the deals.
