# The curl 30 years gource

[November 18, 2026](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/11/18/the-curl-30-years-gource/) [Daniel Stenberg](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/author/daniel/) [Leave a comment](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/11/18/the-curl-30-years-gource/#respond)

In association with curl’s 30 year anniversary, I made another [*gource* video](https://gource.io/) visualizing its development.

Unfortunately it doesn’t cover the full 30 years since we only have records from late 1999 until today; but it is still decent.

Instead I added a little bonus: I’m doing a voice over comment track, explaining and highlighting some facts and details.

Enjoy!
