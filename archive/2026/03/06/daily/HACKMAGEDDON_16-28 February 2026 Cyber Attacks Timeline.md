In the second half of February 2026 I collected 80 events (6.15 events/day) with a threat landscape dominated by **malware** with **42%**, (it was 33% in the first half of the same month), ahead of **account takeovers** (up to **14%** from 8**%**) and **ransomware** (down to **11%** from 20%), which swapped their positions.

As always, **cyber crime** led the **motivations** chart with **62%**, nearly the same value of the [previous timeline](https://www.hackmageddon.com/2026/02/18/1-15-february-2026-cyber-attacks-timeline/), when it was 60%; once again ahead of **cyber espionage** stable at **21%,** the same value of the first fortnight. **Cyber warfare** closes the podium at **4%**, whilst no events related to **hacktivism** were recorded in this fortnight.

The **initial access** chart sees **supply chain compromises** on top with **19%**, ahead of **social engineering** with **15%** (it was and generic **software vulnerabilities** (**12%**).

And even in this timeline, targets belonging to the **Information and Communication** sector lead the chart with **13%**, slightly down from 16%, but this time they share the first place with **Public** **Administrations** (slightly down from 14%). Targets belonging to the **Finance & Insurance**sector close the podium with **10%**.

Besides these trends, as always the timeline is rich of interesting events, such as additional [mega breaches](https://www.hackmageddon.com/2026/01/29/mega-breaches-in-2026/) , so my final suggestion does not change: browse the timeline for the details of all the events collected in this fortnight.
