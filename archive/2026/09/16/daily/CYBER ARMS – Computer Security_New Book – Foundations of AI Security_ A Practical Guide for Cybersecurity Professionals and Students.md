# New Book – Foundations of AI Security: A Practical Guide for Cybersecurity Professionals and Students

Foundations of AI Security by Daniel W. Dieterle is now available on Amazon. This practical guide helps cybersecurity professionals and students defend modern AI systems against real‑world threats including prompt injection, model extraction, adversarial attacks, and data poisoning

![Foundations of AI Security: A Practical Guide for Cybersecurity Professionals and Students by Daniel W. Dieterle, with a glowing blue digital circuit background and interconnected nodes representing AI security threats such as prompt injection, model extraction, and data poisoning](https://cyberarms.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/title-wide-banner-style.jpg?w=1017)

***Foundations of AI Security: A Practical Guide for Cybersecurity Professionals and Student**s is my newest book, built to help practitioners, students, and educators understand and apply AI security capabilities in real world environments. It’s a practical, structured guide to using AI as a powerful accelerator for penetration testing and offensive security.*

[![](https://cyberarms.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/cover-full-300dpi-small.jpg?w=600)](https://cyberarms.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/cover-full-300dpi-small.jpg)

The book blends clear, foundational teaching with hands‑on, tool driven practice. The first half builds your understanding of AI models and security concepts, attacker TTPs, and prompt driven manipulation. The second half shifts into real tools, real workflows, and real AI‑assisted pentesting techniques you can apply immediately.

## **What You Will Learn**

* **The AI Threat Chain** – a modern adaptation of the Cyber Kill Chain for AI‑driven attacks.
* **Key AI security frameworks** including the OWASP Top 10 for AI, OWASP AI Exchange, MITRE ATLAS, and the NIST AI RMF.
* **The psychology behind prompt injection** and how attackers exploit model behavior.
* **Hands‑on prompt injection techniques** for real testing scenarios.
* **How BOLA impacts AI systems and model‑adjacent APIs.**
* **How to use tools like Garak, PyRit, and Promptfoo** for AI security testing.
* **How to integrate AI into pentesting workflows** with Gemini‑CLI, Hexstrike‑AI, and Kali‑MCP‑Server.
* **How to build your own AI‑assisted pentesting tools** using Python and MCP servers.
* **Real examples from my own AI security projects.**

## **Expanded Topics Covered in This Book**

Beyond the core curriculum, this book also dives into several modern offensive security concepts we’ve discussed throughout this project:

### **Local LLMs for Offensive Security**

A major theme is the shift away from cloud‑based AI models toward **local, controllable, offline LLMs**. You’ll learn how to use local models for:

* Secure red team operations
* Offline analysis
* Automated reconnaissance
* Controlled prompt injection testing
* MCP‑driven agent workflows

This approach removes cloud dependency and gives operators full control over their AI tooling.

### **MCP Tooling and AI‑Driven Automation**

The book introduces practical ways to use **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** servers and tools to automate repetitive tasks, streamline analysis, and build custom AI‑assisted workflows. You’ll learn how MCP can:

* Automate reconnaissance
* Assist with exploit development
* Analyze logs and output
* Generate structured testing plans
* Support red team operations without exposing data externally

### **Real‑World Offensive Workflows**

Every technique is grounded in real operations. You’ll see:

* How AI can accelerate enumeration
* How MCP agents can support exploitation
* How local LLMs can assist with payload generation
* How AI can help analyze results and identify next steps

This is not “AI for cybersecurity” in the abstract – it’s AI used as a practical force multiplier.

### **Educational and Training Value**

The book is built for:

* Universities
* Technical training centers
* Cybersecurity instructors
* Students entering the field
* Professionals upgrading their skills

It’s structured to support both self‑study and classroom use, with clear explanations, hands‑on exercises, and real‑world examples.

## **Why This Book Stands Out**

This is a practical, example driven guide built for students, pentesters, red teamers, and security engineers. You’ll learn real techniques, real tools, and real workflows you can apply immediately, without hype or filler.

**AI‑Assisted Pentesting** gives you the knowledge and hands‑on experience to work faster, think smarter, and use AI as a powerful ally in offensive security.

Upgrade your toolkit and step into the future of pentesting. Check it out at [Amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com/Foundations-Security-Practical-Cybersecurity-Professionals/dp/B0HJMY2T4J/).
