## United States

### Digital Forensics SME

*ActioNet, Inc.*

**Rockville, MD**

Full-time

Senior DFIR SME role in Rockville, MD requiring 8+ years’ experience. Responsibilities include advanced endpoint/network forensics, malware analysis, incident response, chain-of-custody documentation, IOC production, and federal law enforcement collaboration. Salary $140K–$184K.

[View Job](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/digital-forensics-sme-at-actionet-inc-4415516058)

### Junior Digital Forensic Analyst

*Peraton*

**Arlington, VA**

Full-time

Support a federal Computer Investigations and Forensics Division conducting forensic examinations of digital devices, mobile phones, cloud sources and removable media. Produce court-admissible reports, provide expert testimony, and assist search warrant executions using tools including Cellebrite, Axiom and X-Ways.

[View Job](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/junior-digital-forensic-analyst-at-peraton-4404477811)

### Digital Forensics Examiner

*The Amatriot Group*

**Linthicum Heights, MD**

Full-time

Examines digital media including computers, mobile devices and storage for a federal DoD customer supporting criminal investigations. Performs forensic analysis, evidence preservation, data recovery and court-ready reporting. Requires active Secret clearance and forensic certifications.

[View Job](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/digital-forensics-examiner-at-the-amatriot-group-4397285645)

### Digital Forensics Examiner

*Peraton*

**Lackland Heights, TX**

Full-time

Support AFOSI criminal investigations conducting digital forensic examination of computers, mobile devices and storage media. Duties include evidence acquisition, data recovery, analysis, report writing and potential expert witness testimony in federal/military court. On-site, San Antonio.

[View Job](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/digital-forensics-examiner-at-peraton-4409992969)

### Digital Forensics Engineer

*cFocus Software Incorporated*

**Washington, DC**

Full-time

Support a federal cybersecurity programme by conducting advanced digital forensic investigations across endpoints, cloud, mobile, and network environments. Responsibilities include malware analysis, e-discovery, incident response, evidence preservation, and legal investigation support within a 24×7 SOC.

[View Job](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/digital-forensics-engineer-at-cfocus-software-incorporated-4412178094)

**Bronx, NY**

Full-time

In-house digital forensics analyst role supporting legal defence work. Responsibilities include evidence acquisition and analysis, mobile and computer forensics, cell site mapping, court testimony, and delivering forensics training to legal staff and external practitioners.

[View Job](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/digital-forensics-analyst-digital-forensics-unit-at-the-legal-aid-society-4405814393)
