# Announcing Privacy Badger for Safari on macOS

![A Privacy Badger logo, a confetti emoji, and Safari icon overlap on a gray background.](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/badger-safari-3.png?itok=a6yVlg5E)

Exciting news for Safari users: Privacy Badger is now available on Safari for macOS! You’ll need Safari version 26.5 or higher to use it.

Whether you want to protect yourself from data brokers or just don’t want Big Tech profiting off your browsing data, Privacy Badger is here to help. More than 4 million people already use EFF’s free tracker-blocking extension to take a stand against online surveillance. Safari users can now join them and EFF in the fight for a better web.

[Install Privacy Badger](http://privacybadger.org/)

[Support the Fight Against Online Tracking](https://www.eff.org/safariblog)

Safari support has been one of Privacy Badger’s most [requested](https://github.com/EFForg/privacybadger/issues/549) features for years. Thanks to recent improvements in Safari's extension support, we’ve finally made it happen for Safari version 26.5 and higher. Privacy Badger is now available on all major desktop browsers. And before you ask, yes, we will look into supporting [Safari for iOS](https://github.com/EFForg/privacybadger/issues/549#issuecomment-5849549636) once we've ironed out any issues on macOS.

### Why Install Privacy Badger on Safari?

[Most websites](https://www.forbes.com/sites/johnkoetsier/2020/03/11/google-is-tracking-you-on-86-of-the-top-50000-websites-on-the-planet/) contain invisible tracking code that lets companies collect, share, and profit from your personal data. Some of those companies are data brokers that sell your information to anyone willing to pay. Creepy ads are just the tip of the iceberg when it comes to the harms of online tracking: Once your information enters the data broker ecosystem, it’s nearly impossible to know [who buys it](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/03/targeted-advertising-gives-your-location-government-just-ask-cbp) or what they’re doing with it. Privacy Badger blocks online trackers to prevent your browsing data from being used against you.

Privacy Badger goes beyond Safari’s [built-in privacy features](https://webkit.org/blog/15697/private-browsing-2-0/) to provide additional protection against online tracking. In its default browsing mode, Safari limits the information that trackers can access but still allows them to load.
