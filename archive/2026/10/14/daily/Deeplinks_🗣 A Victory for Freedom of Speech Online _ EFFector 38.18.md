# 🗣 A Victory for Freedom of Speech Online | EFFector 38.18

![](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/effector-green-web.png?itok=fMSatfxr)

Image by EFF (CC-by)

No one should have to think twice before criticizing the government online. But a mass surveillance program known as "Catch and Revoke" was created to make millions do just that. EFF and other defenders of free speech are fighting back. In [our latest EFFector newsletter](https://www.eff.org/effector/38/18), we're talking about a key court win for our lawsuit [challenging this unconstitutional surveillance program](https://www.eff.org/press/releases/victory-court-rejects-government-effort-dismiss-social-media-surveillance-lawsuit?utm_source=effector).

[JOIN OUR NEWSLETTER](https://www.eff.org/effector/)

For over 35 years, [EFFector](https://eff.org/effector) has been your guide to understanding the intersection of technology, civil liberties, and the law. This issue covers our Opt Out October [guides for ditching Big Tech](https://assets-usa.mkt.dynamics.com/94400758-7964-ef11-a66d-6045bd003934/digitalassets/standaloneforms/4a1a4f18-6a86-ef11-ac21-0022480ae97e), a win in [the fight against anti-VPN laws](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/10/court-agrees-eff-utahs-vpn-law-demands-technical-impossibility?utm_source=effector), and a court ruling allowing [the lawsuit against "Catch and Revoke"](https://www.eff.org/press/releases/victory-court-rejects-government-effort-dismiss-social-media-surveillance-lawsuit?utm_source=effector) to move forward.

Prefer to listen in? EFFector is now available on all major podcast platforms. This time we're talking to EFF's Lisa Femia about how the government is using AI to suppress protected speech online—and the important lawsuit she and others are working on to stop it.
