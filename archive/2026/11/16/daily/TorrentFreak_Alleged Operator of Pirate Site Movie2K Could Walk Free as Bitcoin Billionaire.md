# Alleged Operator of Pirate Site Movie2K Could Walk Free as Bitcoin Billionaire

today by
[Ernesto Van der Sar](https://torrentfreak.com/author/ernesto/)

[Home](https://torrentfreak.com "Go to TorrentFreak.") > [Lawsuits](https://torrentfreak.com/category/lawsuits/ "Go to the Lawsuits category archives.") >

The alleged operator of defunct pirate streaming site Movie2K could walk out of a German court a free man, and still a bitcoin billionaire. The presiding judge in Leipzig has proposed dropping the criminal case if he hands over another 40,000 bitcoin, worth roughly $2.6 billion (€2.4 billion) at today’s rate. In April, the court still proposed a suspended prison sentence in exchange for a confession.

![movie2k](https://torrentfreak.com/images/movie2klogo.jpg)More than a decade ago, Movie2K was one of the most visited websites in Germany, beating Twitter and Amazon.

The site shut down in 2013. By then, its operators had allegedly converted much of their advertising income into bitcoin. This was an early bet that eventually turned into billions.

In January 2024, German authorities [secured around 50,000 bitcoin](https://torrentfreak.com/authorities-secure-2-billion-in-bitcoin-from-pirate-site-operators-240130/), which the alleged operator handed over after his arrest in Spain the year before. The state of Saxony [moved these coins to exchanges](https://torrentfreak.com/german-state-moves-3-billion-in-bitcoin-seized-from-pirate-site-operators-240716/) that summer and eventually sold them for roughly $2.9 billion (€2.64 billion).

That wasn’t the first haul. In 2020, the authorities had already [seized 2,700 bitcoin](https://torrentfreak.com/fbi-help-german-authorities-secure-29-7m-in-crypto-from-pirate-streaming-site-200806/) in the same investigation.

The proceeds of the 2024 sale are now held by the state’s judicial cash office. However, according to the court, the money still belongs to the defendant until there’s a final verdict.

## Copyright Charges Expired

The criminal trial against the alleged operator and a co-defendant started at the Leipzig Regional Court in February. Prosecutors initially [accused](https://torrentfreak.com/operator-of-bitcoin-invested-pirate-site-movie2k-charged-after-more-than-a-decade-240419/) the main defendant of commercial copyright infringement in roughly 220,000 cases.

*Movie2K.to*
![Movie2K.to](https://torrentfreak.com/images/movie2k.jpg)

The court concluded that these copyright offenses were time-barred, so they are no longer part of the case. The main defendant now stands trial for, among other things, commercial money laundering in more than 100 cases. The co-defendant faces dozens of money laundering counts and tax evasion.

The piracy accusations still matter though. According to the indictment, the operators made millions from advertising deals on the site and used that money to buy bitcoin.

## From Suspended Sentence to a Dismissal

In April, presiding judge Karsten Nickel already floated a potential deal, [MDR](https://www.mdr.de/nachrichten/sachsen/leipzig/leipzig-leipzig-land/bitcoin-prozess-verstaendigung-kryptowaehrung-richter-100.html) and German news site [Tarnkappe](https://tarnkappe.info/artikel/rechtssachen/movie2k-bitcoin-deal-milliarden-poker-statt-haftstrafe-kritik-waechst-328329.html) reported at the time. If the main defendant confessed, he could get a prison sentence of one to one and a half years, suspended on probation.

That’s far below the maximum sentence of 13 years mentioned in earlier media reports.

As part of that deal, the main defendant would also have to hand over the access credentials to another 57,000 bitcoin. The defense lawyers criticized this approach as mainly “economically driven” and left open whether they would agree to such a deal.

Talks between the court, prosecutors and the defense continued behind closed doors, and last week Judge Nickel came back with a new proposal. This time, there is no sentence on the table at all.

## New Deal

The case against the main defendant could be dropped if he transfers 40,000 bitcoin to the authorities, a spokesperson for the Dresden General Prosecutor’s office [confirmed to dpa](https://www.volksstimme.de/panorama/movie2k-prozess-angeklagter-konnte-milliardar-bleiben-4355728). The court itself had not confirmed the proposal when dpa reported on it.

The alleged operator would also have to pay roughly $42 million (€38 million) to compensate possible victims, such as rightsholders. According to [Freie Presse](https://www.freiepresse.de/nachrichten/sachsen/prozess-um-streaming-portal-winken-sachsen-fuenf-milliarden-euro-extra-aus-bitcoin-gewinn-artikel14482811), he would also give up his claim to the billions Saxony already raised from the earlier sale.

Meanwhile, the co-defendant would have to hand over 600 bitcoin and $19 million (€17 million). His lawyer said she could imagine accepting that offer, while the main defendant’s lawyers are still weighing their options.

## Still a Billionaire?

Even if the main defendant accepts the deal, he could still be left with a sizable fortune. Prosecutors believe he holds roughly 56,000 bitcoin, which means that he could keep about 16,000 coins, worth just over $1 billion (€945 million) at today’s rate.

[Leipziger Volkszeitung](https://www.lvz.de/politik/movie2k-angeklagter-koennte-gerichtssaal-als-milliardaer-verlassen-BH3LABOL75F63PGSRXMICVSI7U.html) reports that the judge doubts whether all the bitcoin were obtained illegally, which complicates the case.

At the same time, it’s not yet certain that the alleged operator actually controls all the bitcoin attributed to him, and the deal depends on that, [Tarnkappe](https://tarnkappe.info/artikel/krypto/movie2k-prozess-fuenf-milliarden-sachsen-bitcoin-deal-335921.html) notes.

For now, nothing is final. The defense and the prosecutors are still reviewing the proposal. The next hearing is scheduled for December 3. If all parties agree, the case could be over before Christmas.
