## Rapid7 says crims broke into more than 250 sites globally, including a US Senate candidate’s campaign page

![icon](/design_picker/d518b499f8a6e2c65d4d8c49aca8299d54b03012/graphics/icon/vulture_red.svg)
[Carly Page](/Author/Carly-Page "Read more by this author")

Tue 10 Mar 2026  //
16:29 UTC

![](/design_picker/d2e337b97204af4aa34dda04c4e5d56d954b216f/graphics/icons/social_share_icon.svg)

Cyber baddies quietly compromised legitimate WordPress websites, including the campaign site of a US Senate candidate, turning them into launchpads for a global infostealer operation.

Researchers at [Rapid7](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/tr-malicious-websites-wordpress-compromise-advances-global-stealer-operation/) say the scheme works by injecting malicious code into compromised sites, which then serve visitors a convincing fake Cloudflare CAPTCHA page. Instead of simply proving you're not a robot, the prompt instructs users to copy and run a command on their machine – a step that ultimately triggers the download of credential-stealing malware.

The trick works because the attack starts on websites that otherwise look perfectly legitimate. Visitors think they're just clearing yet another Cloudflare bot check – the sort that litters the modern web – when in fact they're being talked through the first step of infecting their own machine.
