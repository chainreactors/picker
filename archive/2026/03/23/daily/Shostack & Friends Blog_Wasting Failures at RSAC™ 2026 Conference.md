# Wasting Failures at RSAC™ 2026 Conference

Cybersecurity should learn lessons from industries that are transparent about failure.
![A Failure is a Terrible Thing to Waste - The Case for Breach Transparency](/images/blog/img/2026/RSAC-Blog-800w.png)

Today at RSAC 2026, Adam and Adrian Sanabria talked about the importance of learning lessons, how other disciplines do so, and some of the important lessons we can take from breaches like the one at Equifax. We’re pleased to share the [slides from today’s talk](/files/conference/CSO-M03_A-Failure-is-a-Terrible-Thing-to-Waste_Sanabria_Shostack_RSA2026.pptx/).

If you’d like to dive deeper, check out Adam’s work on [Lesson learning](/resources/lessons) and [Adrian’s Substack](http://defendersinitiative.com/), where he's just published a [deep dive into lessons from the 2023 MGM breach](https://www.defendersinitiative.com/p/breach-lessons-the-2023-mgm-breach).
