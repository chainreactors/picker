# Sekoia achieves ISO 27001 compliance

Sekoia receives ISO 27001, leading international standard in information security management, from the auditor A-LIGN.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69b190e7f47e4c44b0ee07d8/6ace23fa10567e07a2e186d1_6a5943b046a69574db0bd63f_Frame%25202147262669.webp)

## Key takeaways

Sekoia achieves ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification for its information security management system and SOC platform hosting.

* ISO/IEC 27001 is the global benchmark for information security management systems.
* The certification demonstrates Sekoia's commitment to the confidentiality, integrity and availability of client data.
* The process required a full review of security policies, personnel training and an independent audit.
* The scope covers both Sekoia's ISMS and the hosting and operation of the SOC platform in its qualified OVHcloud region.
* The official ISO certificate is available through the Sekoia Trust Center.

Today, we are pleased to celebrate a major achievement for Sekoia with the attainment of the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification. In this blog post, we’ll explain the journey to this high-end certification.

## What is ISO/IEC 27001 Certification?

The ISO/IEC 27001 standard is the global benchmark for information security management. It establishes a rigorous framework for the implementation, management, and continuous improvement of an Information Security Management System (ISMS). This certification covers various aspects, including the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information, thus ensuring that companies adopt best practices to manage data security risks.

## Why is this certification so important to us?

As a provider of security solutions, we understand the importance of the trust our partners and customers place in us.

For Sekoia, this certification materializes our commitment to protecting the sensitive information of our clients while ensuring the robustness of our solutions and the reliability of our procedures.

## The certification process

Obtaining such certification is a long-term commitment. At Sekoia, it involved a comprehensive review of our information security policies and procedures, training our personnel, a strong commitment from company management, and a rigorous audit carried out by an independent organization.
