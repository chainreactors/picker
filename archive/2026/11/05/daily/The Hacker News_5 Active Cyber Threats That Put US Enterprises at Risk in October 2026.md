October 2026 brought another wave of cyber threats targeting US businesses, with attackers exploiting trusted platforms, legitimate authentication processes, and everyday business workflows. For enterprises, these campaigns raise the stakes beyond individual security incidents, increasing the risk of financial losses, data breaches, and costly operational disruption.

Below, we examine five active cyber threats through real-world sandbox investigations, revealing how they operate, the risks they pose to organizations, and how security teams can detect and contain attacks faster to minimize business impact.

## 1. SkyW4lker: Microsoft 365 Phishing Puts US Corporate Accounts at Risk

SkyW4lker is an active phishing kit that exploits Microsoft's legitimate device-code authentication to compromise corporate accounts without stealing passwords or bypassing MFA. ANY.RUN researchers identified **618 related sandbox analyses between June and October 21, 2026, with 46% coming from the US.**

The attack starts with phishing emails disguised as contracts, invoices, or business proposals. Victims pass through CAPTCHA checks and trusted platforms before being directed to Microsoft's legitimate authentication page, where entering a device code can grant attackers unauthorized account access.
