A would-be spy is recruited on Telegram for $15,000 and sent halfway around the world to snoop on Palantir. His cover story when he’s caught? He wanted to ask a woman out on a date.

Meanwhile, Carl Miller investigates a think tank with 300 experts on its books, one of whom is the inconveniently dead Henry Kissinger. But who is its real audience? You might be surprised.

Plus, Coreview’s Andrea Sivieri explains why Microsoft Copilot is brilliant at finding anything in your company, including the salary spreadsheet someone shared with everybody back in 2019.

All this and more in episode 494 of the “Smashing Security” podcast with cybersecurity expert and keynote speaker Graham Cluley, and special guest Carl Miller.
