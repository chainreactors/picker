There is currently no way in warp to time out a request that hasn't completed sending all headers. At the time of writing, hyper configures a default limit of 30s, and aborts the request if the request isn't at a state it can be passed on to a request handler (after that point, it's the responsibility of the request body reader to do further timeouts).

This only works if a TokioTimer is being passed to the Builder, otherwise there's no limit and an open connection may hang indefinitely until the warp process is restarted.

<https://docs.rs/hyper-util/0.1.21/hyper_util/server/conn/auto/struct.Http1Builder.html#method.header_read_timeout>
