# Stupid Intelligence

On November 20, 2026 by [Jonathan Zdziarski](https://www.zdziarski.com/blog/?author=1)

I always wondered how many heads would explode when we finally broke the Turing test. Fascinating how it happened though. If you’ve been following [Felony Bench](https://www.felonybench.com), AI models seem to be hilariously good at committing crimes. Even if you don’t know industry terminology, the average person on the street knows that AI has a value alignment problem; AI is trained to search a solution space for the path to the highest amortized reward. Such reinforcement learning techniques are very good at describing a problem to solve, and very poor at ever fully describing all of the things that could go wrong (hazards) in solving the problem. This is how the mathematics behind RL often ends up leading LLMs to hallucinate, or deploy tools to solve the problem through committing a few crimes. While AI can be capable of some truly incredible things, it is also incredibly stupid and has next to zero common sense when attempting to reach its intended goal state.
