# Donate Equity to Support Digital Rights: Stocks, DAFs, and Giving

![Colorful figure with wings](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/challenge-banner-1.png?itok=q5_VBJh4)

Do the holidays have you thinking about how you can support your favorite nonprofit?

For 35 years, the Electronic Frontier Foundation has been advancing our digital rights, including [defending security researchers](https://www.eff.org/issues/coders), [encrypting the web](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2023/08/celebrating-ten-years-encrypting-web-lets-encrypt), and [fighting illegal surveillance](https://www.eff.org/nsa-spying). Donating equity to EFF can help support this important work.

Perhaps your family has taken advantage of the new tax law around giving by creating a [Donor-Advised Fund](https://www.eff.org/daf).

Perhaps your employer has gone public and you now own stock.

Perhaps you’ve seen some of EFF’s recent successes and thought, *Man, imagine what they could do with more.*

#### **Stocks**

A gift of equity can help your contribution go farther and offer tax savings. When you donate appreciated stock, you avoid paying capital gains tax on any increase in its value. You also receive a charitable income tax deduction for the fair market value of appreciated stock on the date it is transferred. Also, more and more companies are offering gift-matching programs that can double your impact at no additional cost.

If you have owned the stock for less than a year, your gift will not qualify for all of these potential tax advantages. And gifts of appreciated stock are fully deductible—up to a maximum of 30% of your adjusted gross income. Please check with your accountant or tax advisor regarding your particular tax status.

We have helpful information [here](https://www.eff.org/pages/other-ways-give-and-donor-support#stock) to facilitate the transfer of securities. Please email us if you have any questions. And let us know in advance if you are transferring securities. We’ll need to know the amount and type of shares that you will be donating in order to provide a tax receipt.

#### **Donor-Advised Funds**

As a 501(c)(3) organization, EFF can also receive distributions through your [Donor-Advised Fund](https://www.eff.org/pages/other-ways-give-and-donor-support#daf) (DAF), including one-time gifts or recurring donations. Simply inform your DAF provider you would like to make a gift to:

Electronic Frontier Foundation
(EIN 04-3091431)
815 Eddy Street
San Francisco, CA 94109
