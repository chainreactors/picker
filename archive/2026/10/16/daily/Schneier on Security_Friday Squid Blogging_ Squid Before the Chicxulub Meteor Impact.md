## Friday Squid Blogging: Squid Before the Chicxulub Meteor Impact

[New](https://eos.org/articles/squid-were-on-the-move-before-the-chicxulub-impact) [research](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/gsa/geology/article/54/9/938/732212/Squids-radiated-globally-prior-to-the-Cretaceous) shows that squid lived all around the globe even before the Cretaceous-Paleogene mass extinction event.

As usual, you can also use this squid post to talk about the security stories in the news that I haven’t covered.
