## Abstract

> For two decades, the security industry has promised progress: firewalls, antivirus, EDR, XDR, Zero Trust. Budgets have soared, tools have multiplied. And yet attackers still win with the same tricks. Why? Because while we buy shiny solutions, we continue to neglect the basics.

> This keynote cuts through the illusion of progress and shows why culture and discipline - not the next silver bullet product- are the real missing pieces.
