# Surveillance Isn't Safety: A Comic About Organizing Against Flock and Other Foes

![A panel from Bryn Adams' comic: credit Bryn Adams CC NC](https://www.eff.org/files/styles/resized_banner/public/banner_library/flock-comic-banner-2.jpg?itok=eJE2ub-L)

Image by Bryn Adams (CC NC)

In the new comic "Surveillance Isn't Safety"—available for free today— cartoonist Bryn Adams explores the growing movement against mass surveillance through their involvement in the community fight against Flock Safety in Cleveland, Ohio. It's a personal journey that will resonate widely with residents in hundreds of cities where communities are drawing the line against [automated license plate readers](https://sls.eff.org/technologies/automated-license-plate-readers-alprs) (ALPRs).

[Jump to the Comic](#comic)

[Or download the PDF](https://www.eff.org/files/2026/11/17/surveillance-isnt-safety-comic.pdf)

Published in collaboration between EFF and [Crucial Comix](https://www.crucialcomix.com/), Bryn's comic captures the authenticity of the grassroot backlash against the surveillance industry—undercutting Flock founder Garrett Langley's [latest dubious defense](https://www.404media.co/in-leaked-audio-flock-ceo-tells-cops-backlash-to-the-company-isnt-real/) that public opposition of his company's product isn't real.

At the International Association of Chiefs of Police conference this fall, Langley accused the anti-surveillance activism of being driven by Russian bots and as being "inorganic." He pointed to the fact that many local groups were using similar language in their protest signs as supposed proof, when in reality, shared slogans are one of the hallmarks of a social movement. This was all misdirection from the grim realities that Flock and other ALPR systems facilitate: the [mistakes](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/case-case-every-reported-flock-175653036.html) are real; the [abuse](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/08/02/how-police-officers-used-vast-network-cameras-spy-their-exes/) is real; the [racism](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/11/license-plate-surveillance-logs-reveal-racist-policing-against-romani-people) is real; and the targeting of [dissenters](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/11/how-cops-are-using-flock-safetys-alpr-network-surveil-protesters-and-activists), [migrants](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/06/are-your-local-police-using-flock-safety-alprs-scan-immigrants), and [abortion seekers](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/10...
