In the global race to build and dominate the AI industry, it can sure seem like the interests of ordinary people sit last on the agenda. It's just the opposite for EFF. While companies furiously jam AI tools into their veins and your eyeballs, EFF’s technologists, activists, and attorneys have been meticulously cutting through the hype to ensure AI can serve your privacy and free expression. Technology has leaned into a new era, and this summer you can help EFF fight for the people.

[JOIN EFF](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/s26--d1)

Over the next two weeks, we’re encouraging you to support the cause as [an EFF member for as little as $10 each month](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/s26--d1). You can get great member swag every year like our privacy puffy stickers, Claw Back t-shirt, and Privacy Badger Crewneck.

[![A person wears an EFF Claw Back member t-shirt on the left. A person on the right wears a black sweatshirt with the Privacy Badger mascot on the chest.](/files/2026/07/01/claw_f_crewb.jpg "Get new EFF gear when you join!")](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/s26--d1)

Fight mass surveillance! Pictured: Claw Back member t-shirt and Privacy Badger Crewneck.

* [Mobilized people against the GUARD Act](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/05/congress-narrowed-guard-act-serious-problems-remain), which would require problematic age verifications systems for AI companions.
