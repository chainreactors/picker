# Satellite Imagery Documents New Border Patrol Road Construction in Big Bend National Park

August 20, 2026

* [Border Patrol](/tag/border-patrol)
* [Environment](/tag/environment)
* [Open Source in Short](/tag/open-source-in-short)

***Sign up [here](https://bellingcat-investigations.beehiiv.com/) to receive Bellingcat’s biggest investigations by email* *as soon as they are published.***

Satellite images obtained and analysed by Bellingcat show over five miles of new road constructed in Big Bend National Park in southwestern Texas, part of a US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) [“Smart Wall”](https://www.cbp.gov/border-security/along-us-borders/smart-wall-map/faqs) installation, which includes roads, barriers, lighting and cameras.

The project was paused earlier this week after [pushback](https://thehill.com/policy/energy-environment/6039892-big-bend-national-park-border-construction-cbp-opposition/) from local residents, [politicians](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/trump-administration-big-bend-national-park-construction-pause-rcna593005) and environmental groups.

The roads have been built adjacent to the Rio Grande, through the river’s floodplain and riparian forest, and include construction adjacent to Cottonwood Campground, Santa Elena Canyon, and Mariscal Canyon in the National Park.

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/08/overview-copy-1200x688.jpg)

*Planet Labs satellite imagery captured on August 15 and August 19 shows new roads cleared through the riparian forest around the Rio Grande in Big Bend National Park.*

The map above traces the new road construction in yellow. An unannotated version of the satellite image showing the new road can be found [here](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/08/no-annotations-scaled.jpg) while a before image of the same area where no road is visible can be seen [here](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Talley%2B3%2B%26%2B4/%4028.9842188%2C-103.1869036%2C720m/data%3D%213m1%211e3%214m6%213m5%211s0x86f268250b6fae8f%3A0x89314aab16d478b%218m2%213d28.9834219%214d-103.1831379%2116s/g/1s048phjt?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDgxNy4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D).

The new road extends to Cottonwood Campground, one of four campgrounds in Big Bend National Park.

The road can be seen crossing the riparian forest and smaller drainages before joining the campground access road. Imagery also appears to show bright yellow construction equipment staging in the campground area, where [bulldozers were previously seen](https://www.houstonpublicmedia.org/articles/news/texas/2026/08/06/558776/border-project-contractors-arrive-in-big-bend-national-park-as-texas-lawmakers-call-for-a-pause/).

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/08/big-bend-1-copy-2-scaled.jpg)

![](https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2026/08/cottonwood-campground-slider-1-scaled.jpg)

*Planet Labs satellite imagery captured on August 19 shows a new road near Cottonwood Campground in Big Bend National Park. Hover to compare to Planet satellite imagery from September 2022. Note that the removal of cottonwood trees in the campground is [unrelated to CBP construction activity](https://bigbendchat.com/no-more-cottonwoods-campground-t20054.html).*

In the southernmost part of Big Bend National Park, additional new road construction is also visible near Mariscal Canyon and connecting to Talley Road.

[CBP planning documents](https://www.cbp.gov/border-security/along-us-borders/smart-wall-map) indicate that this section of the border will receive a four-to-six foot tall vehicle barrier in addition to the “smart wall” lighting and camera systems. Talley Road currently provides access to riverside backcountry campsites and hiking and boating access to [Mariscal Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/rigr/planyourvisit/mariscal_cyn.htm) on the Rio Grande.

CBP Commissioner Rodney Scott described the construction activities as “survey and design work” in an [August 13 statement](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/announcements/statement-cbp-commissioner-rodney-s-scott-correcting-record-big-bend). He also stated that CBP is “building one new access road, improving existing roads, installing detection technology, and placing vehicle barriers in limited, strategic locations.”

However, by August 17 Scott had announced a “pause” of construction activities in the face of  [bipartisan criticism](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/trump-administration-big-bend-national-park-construction-pause-rcna593005) of the project’s [impact to the environment, recreation access, and the local economy](https://nobigbendwall.org/2026/08/03/letter-to-senator-cornyn-senator-cruz-and-governor-abbott-protect-big-bends-river-recreation-economy-public-river-access-and-texas-natural-heritage-stop-needless-border-barrier/)

The Big Bend Border Patrol Sector sees the [fewest migrant apprehensions](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/stats/southwest-land-border-encounters-by-component) of any part of the US southern border, according to CBP’s own data.
