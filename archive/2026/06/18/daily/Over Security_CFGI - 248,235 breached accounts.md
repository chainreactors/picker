# CFGI Data Breach

## What Happened

In March 2026, the financial consulting and advisory firm [CFGI was the target of a ShinyHunters "pay-or-leak" extortion campaign](https://x.com/AlvieriD/status/2029785330677936135). The group subsequently publicised data allegedly obtained from CFGI comprising corporate contact information, including 243k unique email addresses, names, phone numbers and physical addresses.
